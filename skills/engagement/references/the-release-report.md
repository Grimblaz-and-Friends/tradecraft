# The release report

**Loaded when** composing a change's release report, or deciding what goes first in it.

**The release report is the owner's surface at release, so it opens with what needs them and puts everything settled by checks out of the way.** What they read is what still needs a decision or their judgement: whether their decisions did what they expected. Everything else stays findable without being in their path.

**It is the one thing that says a change is waiting on its owner.** A pull request marked ready for review says only that its planned build is complete and its floor passes, as the connected-reviewer contract states, so the holder tells nobody a change is ready for its owner before this report.

## Its order

1. **Title line and plain brief.** The title line is `**Release report: #N → PR #M** at head <sha>`. The plain brief takes `the-brief.md`'s form, and its first sentence says what needs the owner's decision, or that nothing does. Where `the-brief.md` asks a brief to name a ruling it applies, that line follows this one.
2. **Needs your decision.** Directly after the plain brief: every reviewer finding dispositioned `yours — in the release report`, every open ask on the change, the `filing` cell's one release-report purchase ask, and every bypass of a required stage or gate recorded in the `**Path departures:**` paragraph (`change-paths.md`), any restore decision a builder-damage launch account leaves with the owner (the `work` cell's launch-findings reference), and any merge block named because required review threads stay unresolved (the connected-reviewer disposition rule) — or the word "nothing". An ask keeps its own form and mark, which are `the-ask.md`'s, and the purchase ask keeps the `filing` cell's; the report orders and links them, never restating their forms. A bypass or a merge block is listed so the owner merges knowing it: their answer is the merge decision itself, which is already theirs, so it takes no ask form.
3. **What the reviewers read.** One sentence, `run release-report`'s `reviewers_read.sentence`: which head the reviewers read and how much changed between it and the head the owner merges, with the rest of that section's reading; a second reviewer look that was bought is named in the same sentence with what it found. It sits here so the owner sees how much followed the reviewers' one look without opening the collapsed section, which is what lets them buy a second look where that is large.
4. **Your decisions, and what they did.** One entry per affirmed row whose owner cell names something the owner will see or judge. Each entry carries:
   - what they decided — the row, in a phrase;
   - what they expected — the owner cell's text before `Checks:`, quoted, never the checks;
   - where it landed — links into the code at the report's head;
   - evidence of what it did — output and screenshots from the use runs, the use consumer's observations wherever a use ran, measurements, and how the code reads, from the cold reader's account of the change's reading for its reasons (`the-stretch.md`);
   - one verdict: **as expected**, **different** and how, or **not shown**.

   No `Checks:` text appears in an entry; a row's named checks and their outcomes sit only in the collapsed section, under that row, because the owner agreed them at design and need not read them again. A row whose owner cell is `unchanged` or names nothing to judge appears only there. A brief affirmed before rows named their checks is read the same way, its owner cell standing whole as the expectation.
5. **Upstream misses.** Beside the entries: each call made after affirmation that the fork test this cell states would have made the owner's, but that was made without reaching them, with where it was made. They are listed only; what follows from a miss is not this reference's to say.
6. **Worth a look.** At most three excerpts, each cut from the added or changed lines of the change's diff at the report's head (`git diff <base>...<head>`) rather than retyped, and each carrying a link to its lines and one line on why it is worth reading. The holder chooses them from the nominations in the cold reader's account.
7. **Settled by checks.** One collapsed `<details>` section, grouped by affirmed row. First, each row's checks and their outcomes: the row's named checks where it names them, and the criterion results with their evidence. Then, for a change that wrote no decision record, the cold reader's account of why the change was made, which judges whether it decided nothing and is understood without its brief (`the-stretch.md`). Then the process evidence: the required gate and its verdict, what the reviewers read (`reviewers_read`, on both routes: the head the pull request had when first marked ready and any required reviewer whose first review named another commit, how long after the build began, how many cold readings came before it, whether a fix was pushed before the last report on that head, compared by committer dates, the first cold reading's head and whether it followed the answering commits, and each file changed after that head with its lines, each part it cannot read standing as unknown with its reason), answered reviewer findings, comments from other authors noted unanswered (`change-paths.md`), uses, reach, size, cost (with keep-warm as its own line, carrying its price and outcome counts, and the turns that reloaded anyway, both from the cost report's `keep_warm` and `reloads`), the cold readings, the launch write bound the launch-findings reference has the report quote, every GitHub write granted to a step with the brief comment and row it rests on and every write a step was refused (the `grants` and `refusals` of `run release-report`'s `github_write_bound`, which says `unknown` when a launch's record could not be read), and departures that need no decision. The cold readings are listed one per reading, rereads included: its head, its mode (a first reading, a fix-check or a why-check), whether it read the whole change, for a reread each earlier flag it checked with `answered` or `not-answered` and the reader's reason, the flags that stand with the lines they sit on, which the holder routed and which it accepted with each acceptance's reason, each flag outside the change with its reason (`old-line`, `no-lines` or `not-reached`), never among those accepted, and its model, effort, duration and list-price cost; `run release-report` returns all but the cost under `cold_readings`, from the readings and `open` comments the gate holds, and the cost comes from that run's dispatch record. The owner judges the holder's acceptances from this list. Check outcomes and process evidence never appear above this section.

## On the builder route

**On the builder route (`the-stretch.md`), `run release-report` adds `builder_route` to its payload, and the report places it within the order above.** The holder gives each decline its view, `agrees`, `differs` or `cannot-judge`, because where a decline goes turns on it; a decline given no view needs the owner.

- **Needs your decision** adds, in this order:
  1. the final-pass block: each spot check the holder made and what it found, what it sent back and what came of it, and what stays open after the send-back, including anything the last ready record still owed (`owed_at_ready.last`; the first record's list carries no chain stage where a later record stands, and `first_chain` says so);
  2. each decline, from a check report or a connected reviewer, where the holder's view differs from the builder's or the holder can't judge it. The readiness record supplies the checks' declines, and `reviewer_declines` the connected reviewers';
  3. every builder decision that would change a brief row, or whose text asks for the owner's choice;
  4. a handoff whose text differs from the composed one (`handoff_plan` reads `differs`, holder-written or altered);
  5. one line counting the remaining declines and pointing at their list.
- **Declined findings** is an uncollapsed entry directly after "Your decisions, and what they did". It lists every other decline, check reports' and connected reviewers' alike, each with the builder's reason and the holder's view, and declines sharing a reason are listed together under it. No decline appears only in the collapsed section.
- **Settled by checks** adds the route and its reason, the cost split (the holder before the handoff, the holder after it, and the builder), every limit above its default with its reason, each stretch launch's elapsed seconds beside its limit, each catch-up resume, what was owed at the first and last ready records, the holder's quiet before ready and its launches after it (`holder_quiet`, `holder_after_ready`), the builder's vendor and model with whether they were observed or declared, each row check's reading, the order the change ran under as the comparison ledger records it, and the cold readings' acceptances as the builder's, for the owner to judge. Until the holder's window has a lower bound, its figure before the handoff covers the holder session's whole history before it, and any comparison of the routes' cost reads it that way.

The payload's other fields (the readiness reading, the send-back, plan-writer launches and refusals) are the evidence these placements draw on, and stay in the collapsed section.

## What it leaves where it is

**The pull request body keeps its existing record and its `**Path departures:**` paragraph** (`change-paths.md`), because the report is the owner's surface and the body is the change's record.
