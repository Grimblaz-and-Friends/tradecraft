# Holding the unattended stretch

**Loaded when** coordinating the stretch from an affirmed brief to release readiness, taking one of its dispatched stages, staffing a judging seat, or preparing its reports and records.

## The holder and the implementer

**The session that holds the affirmed brief owns the whole stretch for that change.** It dispatches every stage and receives every return, remaining the owner's counterpart through the reviewed pull request and its posted report. One holding session per change, never one for many, because its context grows with every stage. It edits no file in the implementation tree and takes no judging seat.

**The material handing a party its job states the tools and permission or approval route it must be started with to perform it**, because instructions cannot supply a capability the launch withholds. [D-597]

**The holder resumes the artifact's implementer through substantive cold-reader repairs, the build, and every prescribed repair after judgment or use**, because those stages depend on the builder's choices or on the tree and finding the builder already holds; this includes small fixes. Only the change's own records — its decision entry, decision-index row, settling row and artifact settlement block — go to a fresh implementer, whose independence matters because it has no stake in the claims it checks. Its instruction says to verify every claim against the tree and pull request and return what cannot be verified rather than changing its meaning. **Writing parties run serially in the change's implementation worktree and branch:** the resumed builder finishes and commits the work and fix batch before the fresh record installer starts, and neither writes while the other does. The implementer returns at the extent its dispatch names. A recipient needing another party returns that need to the holder; recipients dispatch no pipeline stage themselves. Each instrument retains its own ordering, evidence, isolation and settlement rules.

**For a Codex implementer**, use `lib/dispatch_implementer.py`, which applies the explicit model and effort, workspace-write with automatic approval review, JSON return capture and a named session id without `--last` or `--ephemeral`. The dispatch tells the implementer to report a sandbox refusal and request approval to retry it, since stopping at the first failure suppresses that route. A fresh turn returns the id its later `--resume SESSION_ID` takes; a completed turn with no trustworthy id returns its product but cannot qualify as resumable. If approval is rejected or unavailable, report that inability to the holder. The [launch spike](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/593#issuecomment-5653831839) and [resume by id](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/593#issuecomment-5654461236) demonstrate the capability; the `dispatch-records.md` entry in this cell carries the launcher and record contract, including the manual path for a Claude implementer.

## Staffing judgment

**Every judging seat is a fresh dispatch on the other vendor from the implementer's**, with only the history its instrument permits: the artifact's cold reader, review seats, defense, judge, and the experience session's consumer. By default Claude holds the brief, Codex implements, and Claude staffs judging seats. The comparison is with the implementer, whatever vendor holds the brief. Each launch names its model and reasoning effort; freshness does not erase the predecessor evidence a role is meant to receive.

**The experience consumer also runs on the runtime the change's users run.** Both requirements apply. Where no qualifying consumer can be supplied, record the missing use and why under the instrument's omission route.

**An experience consumer takes the ordinary judging effort.** Its coldness protects the run from briefing, while the top effort is reserved for open-ended artifact/review perception and the terminal stage. The review roster carries those seats' effort split. An owner's recorded per-change instruction wins over either default without moving a stage between vendors.

**Staffing is read from the actual returned vendor.** A fallback onto the implementer's vendor supplies a qualifying judgment; its staffing value names the fallback and its reason in the `fable (cold-read), opus (rest)` spelling.

## Returns and records

**Every return comes back to the holder and travels onward whole.** A file is the transport where available; the artifact's cold check takes its prescribed inline carrier. The holder passes the source itself, with only the labeled additions the receiving instrument permits and the withholding its cold boundary requires. It merges findings as an index of the source reports; the judging stage rules on the work.

**The holder writes the report and records from those returns, and gives the change's own record content to a fresh implementer to verify, install and commit after the resumed builder's commit.** The implementer returns any correction that would change their meaning. This keeps authorship with the party that saw the run and gives its claims an independent checker. The holder retains every dispatch through this cell's `dispatch-records.md` contract, then applies each instrument's population when it aggregates cost rather than reconstructing it from prose. It publishes the branch, opens the pull request, posts the report, and delivers the outcome to the owner; merging remains the owner's.
