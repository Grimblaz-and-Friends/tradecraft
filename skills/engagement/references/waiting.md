# Waiting for an affirmation

**Loaded when** the owner cannot be reached for an affirmation the work owes, and you are deciding what may be done while that lasts.

**The truncation that settles an unobtainable cold check does not extend to the affirmation.** That check judges the session's own reading, which the session owns and can be accountable for leaving unjudged; the affirmation is the owner's term, which the session does not own and cannot supply for them. A truncated verdict leaves a reading nobody checked; a truncated affirmation would leave a term nobody agreed — not a degraded agreement but the absence of one.

**Waiting for a decision the owner has not made is not the stalling the charter forbids.** That rule bars refusing or stalling a decision they *have* made; a brief they have not yet seen is not one, and neither is a directive of theirs that no brief has yet read back — obeying it is what the drafting does, and the decision still owed is on the reading.

**Waiting is not idling.** Everything the affirmation would unlock except the work itself stays available: draft the brief to the point where it could lock, test the premises that could invalidate it, and post it on the work's issue **labelled a draft and carrying the mark, with the ask block's three lines**, marking which sentences are the session's restatement rather than the owner's own words. The draft label and the mark are different things doing different jobs: one says the brief is not affirmed, the other is what puts it in front of them at all. **That is not the record of the affirmation (`../references/the-brief.md`), and neither the post nor the owner's reading it there supplies the affirmation** — the brief is re-put in conversation on their return and affirmed there; the label is what keeps the two apart. Then hand back in the form a handback message takes (`../references/the-brief.md`).

**The artifact is the work itself for this purpose, and is withheld.** The list above is closed — it is what a waiting session may do in place of the work, not an illustration of it. An artifact drafted here would read a term nobody has agreed, so a seat settling it would judge a reading of nothing.

**Where a job's own terms require what this withholds, those terms do not license it.** Hand back to whoever set them, naming the step the affirmation gates — reported where this cell already sends a call the session made. Whoever set the job can amend it; only the owner can supply the affirmation.
