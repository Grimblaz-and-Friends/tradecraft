# engagement

**Purpose:** keep the surfaces the owner enters to the decisions that are genuinely theirs, and make each one cheap for them to act on. **Audience:** any session working the stretch between the owner's attention and the next call on it — putting a decision to them, or building under one already settled. **Success:** the decisions that reach the owner are the ones genuinely theirs; they can read one brief and know what it is, why it matters, and what they are being asked; the artifact a session leaves behind states what the work is for without them in the room; a session can say which side of the boundary any stage of the work falls on, and where a question arising on the far side goes; and what one session settled with them reaches the session that builds it.

## The plain brief

Every surface the owner enters opens with one, and so does the artifact, which carries the brief because it binds there.

> **In plain terms:** a few sentences of plain English, set off from everything around them, that explain the thing to the owner as a person — what it is, why it matters, what they are being asked, and anything they have already settled.

**Affirmed, it binds; describing, it is accountable.** A brief the owner affirmed is the term of the agreement, and the material under it interprets that term: where the two conflict the brief governs, and where the brief is silent the material does. Everywhere else a brief describes something that already exists — a review's findings, the state of the work — and there it yields to its subject, so a brief that misrepresents its body is a defect found in that body's review. **Authority follows affirmation, not placement**: what the owner approved is what they can be held to, and a rule keyed to a list of surfaces drifts as surfaces are named.

**Its form and its bar, the pass that scores a draft before it is put, the surfaces it opens, how it locks and is affirmed and recorded, the brief a problem's systemic cause gets, and how one is amended: `references/the-brief.md`.**

## A decision put to the owner

**A decision is the owner's when it is a genuine fork**: what turns on the pick is something they would care about — what gets built, what it costs, what others see, what they live with day to day — and undoing a wrong pick costs them something an edit does not undo: habit, muscle memory, what others already saw. Both halves are required — one live option can satisfy the first — and everything else is the session's to decide. The statement of the test and the calibration below are both this cell's.

**Asking where no fork exists is a defect rather than politeness.** The tell is that nothing turns on their answer — the session would proceed the same way whatever they said; that is a report, and putting it as a question spends their attention for nothing. One live option is not by itself the tell: a real search that narrowed to one can still turn on their answer, and the fork test above decides it either way.

- **Theirs:** affirm, amend, or reject what a change is for before it is built; merging; anything outward-facing or destructive; a name, or anything else they live with day to day; spending materially beyond what the task implied.
- **The session's:** implementation approach, file layout, tool choice, and the order work is done in.

**A call the session made is reported with its reason** where the work is already recorded — the work's issue, the PR body, the decision entry, or the review report — and never only in chat. The fork test is what licenses deciding; the report is what keeps deciding from becoming deciding silently. **A report is not an ask** — nothing turns on their answer — so it carries no mark; where the same surface carries something they must rule, the rules on putting an ask (`references/the-ask.md`) apply and the mark is what puts it.

**The argued form what is theirs arrives in, the three habits that qualify every ask, and — where the owner is not in the room — the ask block, the mark that puts it and the query that reads what waits: `references/the-ask.md`.**

## The pre-implementation artifact

The artifact settles what is being built before it is built and lands on the work's own issue, the text a truth-of-text change drafts first excepted. It **opens with the affirmed brief, quoted verbatim from the comment that carries it**, and governed by it, then its own purpose header — the one place a governing document opens with something ahead of that header, because the term it is held to outranks its own statement of itself; the rest is what a reader who was not in the conversation needs in order to build the agreed thing. Its audience is the sessions that build and review from it, so it is written for them rather than presented to the owner.

**How a brief locks, is affirmed and is recorded, and how a brief or a reading is amended: `references/the-brief.md`; what this document carries, its criteria and their falsifiers, and the cold seat that settles it: `references/the-artifact.md`; and what the issue must carry where the work changes hands at the affirmation: `references/the-handoff.md`.**

## The two halves, and the boundary between them

The owner's attention concentrates at two moments and the stretch between them runs without them. Which stages fall where is the map; a session that cannot tell which side it stands on is the failure this exists to stop.

**Theirs, up to and including the affirmation:** whether the work is picked up at all, drafting and settling the brief in conversation, and any fork surfacing while settling it. **The affirmation is the boundary.** **The unattended stretch, from there to a change ready for merge** — which means on a pull request whose review has run and closed, every finding dispositioned, external reviewer comments reconciled, and the report posted: the artifact and the cold seat that settles it, implementation, the guards the change owes, commit and push and the pull request, the experience session the change bought or the line declining it, the review, the fixes. **Theirs again at release:** the report and the outcome delivered, and the merge. Filing sits outside all three — it records something rather than starting work. **Pinging inside the stretch spends the attention the affirmation bought** — the cold check's two-round cap is a scheduled exception, and `references/cold-seat.md` states it.

**Nothing substitutes for the affirmation, and a session that cannot reach the owner for an affirmation its work owes waits.** One route, not two: slow and unreachable get the same answer, because neither licenses proceeding. Work owing no brief owes no affirmation and is not held here. **A directive the owner wrote is not one either** — it is what a brief is drafted from, and the affirmation is their word on the brief that reads it back to them, which nothing else supplies. Their having asked for the work is why it is lawful to draft; it is not the agreement on what drafting produced.

**What that wait forbids, and the closed list of what a waiting session may do in place of the work: `references/waiting.md`.**

**A question arising inside the stretch takes three tests, in this order, the first match governing.**

1. **Does answering it change what the change is for?** The work **stops** — the one thing that stops it. Post what you found and wait, because that is an amendment to what they affirmed.
2. **Does the affirmed brief, or the map above, settle it?** Settled the same way, no report owed beyond the diff. The map settles every stage it assigns, which is why *do I open the pull request?* is answered here.
3. **Otherwise decide it and report it with your reason** — and where this cell makes the matter the owner's, that report is an **argued ask they rule at release**, carried where this cell already sends a reported call — the work's issue, the pull request body, the decision entry, or the review report — rather than a settled call, **and marked there (`references/the-ask.md`), since an ask that is not marked is not put**. The work does not wait on the ruling: proceed on your recommendation.

**One restraint, on all three tests above.** Do not perform an act whose effect this branch cannot take back — whichever way you read the matter, and whether or not you think the call is your own. **An act the map or the affirmed brief already accounts for is exempt**: a stage is accounted for by being assigned, and filing by being placed outside the bands. **The exemption covers performing an accounted-for act at the cost the change warrants and reaches no further** — not the increment beyond that, a panel or a fan-out widened well past what the change warrants, and not an irreversible side-act taken inside it, which is reached whatever that act costs because a side-act is never what it needs in order to complete. So the restraint reaches three things: an act no edit at review retracts that the map does not account for; the increment by which an accounted-for act exceeds what the change warrants; and an irreversible side-act inside one. **A stage run at the cost the change warrants is performed**, and reaching for the restraint there is the misreading that halts the stretch. Where the work cannot continue without an act this restraint withheld, the affirmed brief no longer covers what the change requires, and test 1 applies.
