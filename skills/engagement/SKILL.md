# engagement

**Purpose:** keep the surfaces the owner enters to the decisions that are genuinely theirs, make each one cheap to act on, and keep the change accountable between them. **Audience:** any session designing with the owner or working the stretch between one call on their attention and the next — putting a decision to them, holding the implementation brief or taking a dispatched stage under it. **Success:** every non-mechanical change is designed in turns until the decisions that matter and every reader's outcome are visible in one affirmed item; the implementer's artifact reads only the session-owned work beneath it; a session can say which side of the boundary any stage falls on and where its questions go; and one holding session carries the work through implementation and independent judgment to release.

## The plain brief and the implementation brief

Every surface the owner enters opens with a plain brief. The implementation brief is the distinct whole item settled with the owner before a non-mechanical change is built. The artifact carries the affirmed implementation brief because it binds there.

> **In plain terms:** a few sentences of plain English, set off from everything around them, that explain the thing to the owner as a person — what it is, why it matters, what they are being asked, and anything they have already settled.

**An affirmed implementation brief binds; every other brief describes and is accountable.** The implementation brief is the term of the agreement, and the material under it interprets that term: where the two conflict the implementation brief governs, and where the implementation brief is silent the material does. Everywhere else a brief describes something that already exists — a review's findings, the state of the work — and there it yields to its subject, so a brief that misrepresents its body is a defect found in that body's review. **Authority follows affirmation, not placement**: what the owner approved is what they can be held to, and a rule keyed to a list of surfaces drifts as surfaces are named.

**The implementation brief replaces the paragraph form for this agreement and keeps the name.** Descriptive review-report and handback briefs remain plain briefs and are not affirmed terms.

**The shared plain-brief form and bar, the implementation brief's own form and pre-put check, the surfaces a brief opens, how the whole item is affirmed and recorded, the implementation brief a problem's systemic cause gets, and how one is amended: `references/the-brief.md`.**

## Designing in turns

**Every non-mechanical change is designed with the owner in turns before it is built; there is no trigger and no separate design sitting.** A turn may bring the whole item when nothing decision-bearing remains to add, so the smallest run is one read and one affirm.

**The one conversation form and what a turn carries: `references/design-sitting.md`.**

## A decision put to the owner

**A decision is the owner's when it is a genuine fork**: what turns on the pick is something they would care about — what gets built, what it costs, what others see, what they live with day to day — and undoing a wrong pick costs them something an edit does not undo: habit, muscle memory, what others already saw. Both halves are required — one live option can satisfy the first — and everything else is the session's to decide. The statement of the test and the calibration below are both this cell's.

**Asking where no fork exists is a defect rather than politeness.** The tell is that nothing turns on their answer — the session would proceed the same way whatever they said; that is a report, and putting it as a question spends their attention for nothing. One live option is not by itself the tell: a real search that narrowed to one can still turn on their answer, and the fork test above decides it either way.

- **Theirs:** affirm, amend, or reject what a change is for before it is built; merging; anything outward-facing or destructive; a name, or anything else they live with day to day; spending materially beyond what the task implied.
- **The session's:** implementation approach, file layout, tool choice, and the order work is done in.

**A call the session made is reported with its reason** where the work is already recorded — the work's issue, the PR body, the decision entry, or the review report — and never only in chat. The fork test is what licenses deciding; the report is what keeps deciding from becoming deciding silently. **A report is not an ask** — nothing turns on their answer — so it carries no mark; where the same surface carries something they must rule, the rules on putting an ask (`references/the-ask.md`) apply and the mark is what puts it.

**The argued form what is theirs arrives in, the four habits that qualify every ask, and — where the owner is not in the room — the ask block, the mark that puts it and the query that reads what waits: `references/the-ask.md`.**

## The pre-implementation artifact

The implementer writes the artifact before building from it; on a truth-of-text change, it drafts the replacement text before the artifact. The holder posts the artifact to the work's own issue once settled. It **opens with the affirmed implementation brief, quoted verbatim from the comment that carries it**, and governed by it, then its own purpose header — the one place a governing document opens with something ahead of that header, because the term it is held to outranks its own statement of itself; the rest is what a reader who was not in the conversation needs in order to build the agreed thing. Its audience is the sessions that build and review from it, so it is written for them rather than presented to the owner.

**How an implementation brief is put whole, affirmed and recorded, and how one or a reading is amended: `references/the-brief.md`; what this document carries, its cell-traced criteria and their falsifiers, and the cold seat that settles it: `references/the-artifact.md`; and what the issue must carry where the work changes hands at the affirmation: `references/the-handoff.md`.**

## The two halves, and the boundary between them

The owner's attention concentrates at two moments and the stretch between them runs without them. **The holding session designs with the owner through affirmation and carries the stretch; it builds nothing, and the builder judges nothing.** Responsibility and staffing across that stretch are in `references/the-stretch.md`, so the owner has one counterpart while the work receives independent judgment. Which stages fall where is the map; a session that cannot tell which side it stands on is the failure this exists to stop.

**Theirs, up to and including the affirmation:** whether the work is picked up at all, drafting and settling the implementation brief in conversation, and any fork surfacing while settling it. **The affirmation is the boundary.** **The unattended stretch, from there to a change ready for merge** — which means on a pull request whose executable floor and required experience session have run, or whose declining line is present every connected-reviewer comment is disposed in its thread, and, where the owner bought a panel, its review has run and closed, every finding is dispositioned and its report is posted: the artifact and the cold seat that settles it, implementation, the guards the change owes, commit and push and the pull request, the experience session the change bought or the line declining it, the connected reviewers, any bought panel, and the fixes. **Theirs again at release:** the report and the outcome delivered, and the merge. Filing sits outside all three — it records something rather than starting work. **Pinging inside the stretch spends the attention the affirmation bought** — the cold check's two-round cap is a scheduled exception, and `references/cold-seat.md` states it.

**Nothing substitutes for the affirmation, and a session that cannot reach the owner for an affirmation its work owes waits.** One route, not two: slow and unreachable get the same answer, because neither licenses proceeding. Work owing no implementation brief owes no affirmation and is not held here. **A directive the owner wrote is not one either** — it is what an implementation brief is drafted from, and the affirmation is their word on the implementation brief that reads it back to them, which nothing else supplies. Their having asked for the work is why it is lawful to draft; it is not the agreement on what drafting produced.

**What that wait forbids, and the closed list of what a waiting session may do in place of the work: `references/waiting.md`.**

**From the start of build onward, a surprise is compared first with Shape and then with the reader cells of the affirmed implementation brief.** What each branch carries, when it reaches the owner, and why the run continues are in `references/the-stretch.md`.

**A question arising inside the stretch that is not a build-onward surprise takes three tests, in this order, the first match governing.**

1. **Does answering it change what the change is for?** The work **stops** — the one thing that stops it. Post what you found and wait, because that is an amendment to what they affirmed.
2. **Does the affirmed implementation brief, or the map above, settle it?** Settled the same way, no report owed beyond the diff. The map settles every stage it assigns, which is why *do I open the pull request?* is answered here.
3. **Otherwise decide it and report it with your reason** — and where this cell makes the matter the owner's, that report is an **argued ask they rule at release**, carried where this cell already sends a reported call — the work's issue, the pull request body, the decision entry, or the review report — rather than a settled call, **and marked there (`references/the-ask.md`), since an ask that is not marked is not put**. The work does not wait on the ruling: proceed on your recommendation.

**One restraint, on all three tests above.** Do not perform an act whose effect this branch cannot take back — whichever way you read the matter, and whether or not you think the call is your own. **An act the map or the affirmed implementation brief already accounts for is exempt**: a stage is accounted for by being assigned, and filing by being placed outside the bands. **The exemption covers performing an accounted-for act at the cost the change warrants and reaches no further** — not the increment beyond that, a panel or a fan-out widened well past what the change warrants, and not an irreversible side-act taken inside it, which is reached whatever that act costs because a side-act is never what it needs in order to complete. So the restraint reaches three things: an act no edit at review retracts that the map does not account for; the increment by which an accounted-for act exceeds what the change warrants; and an irreversible side-act inside one. **A stage run at the cost the change warrants is performed**, and reaching for the restraint there is the misreading that halts the stretch. Where the work cannot continue without an act this restraint withheld, the affirmed implementation brief no longer covers what the change requires, and test 1 applies.
