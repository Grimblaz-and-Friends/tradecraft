# Scoring a cold-seat A/B, and licensing a deletion from one

**Loaded when** you are scoring a cold-seat A/B's planted control, or the run's output would be the removal of the text under test. **`../references/cold-seat-ab.md` comes first on either route**, where the run shapes, the plant and the void gate are defined.

**What counts as a citation, without which nothing below can be applied: a decline reasoned from something other than the text under test is not one** — most often the absence of a positive trigger, even where its content coincides with the text's. A seat attributing the effect *to the text* — *"its entry explicitly excludes push-rejection recovery"* — is citing, in whichever arm. Score both arms by the same reading.

**Search the record before dispatching anything, because a hit ends it.** A session that leaned on the text in its own work — in an entry, a review, a session note — owed nothing to a docket and is the stronger witness than a cold seat working one it was given. **Such a hit keeps the text and stops any run that would remove it; a substitution removes nothing, so a hit there neither keeps nor stops — it shows only that the current wording is live, which the default already assumes, and says nothing about the candidate — and is reported with the run.** The session must have **used** the text — given it as its reason for what it did — because a document discussing the text only mentions it; and it must have used **the text as it now reads**, a citation of wording since replaced being a citation of something else.

**A run licenses a deletion where its arms differ in that text alone, no seat in either arm cited the text or its substance, and it ran at least three seats an arm** ([the run this rule comes from](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/260#issuecomment-5503370780)). **Anything else keeps**, one citation from the arm **holding** the text being enough — **except where the only citations came from the arm the text was removed from**, which is **`unresolved`**: the text stays, on the same asymmetry below, but nothing was measured about it, so it may not be recorded as tested-and-kept. **A screen licenses no deletion**: where the arms differ by a whole class the result is attributable to the class, and a per-target confirm has to follow before any one sentence goes. A deletion licensed by a void run is licensed by nothing.

The asymmetry is the cost asymmetry. Three seats an arm can show that text nobody reaches for is not carrying its situation; they cannot show that text one seat leaned on is safe to remove. Keeping inert text costs characters. Deleting live text costs a session routed where the text existed to stop it — which no guard catches, and which surfaces as somebody's bad change months later.

**An `unresolved` run says the instrument was blind, not that the text is live.** A seat reasoning the substance of text it could not see may have rebuilt it from what remained — the cell's name, the summary sentence above it, an always-on surface naming the destination — and which of those it was, if any, is what the run did not establish. **For the licence that is terminal**: such a run fails the rule above by construction, and no further arm rescues it, a run removing a second thing alongside the text no longer being one whose arms differ in that text alone. **For the measurement it is not.** A further arm that removes a candidate reconstruction source alongside the text leaves a seat that much less to rebuild from: seats that stop giving the substance show that source was carrying it, seats that still give it show that one was not, the remaining candidates and the model being what is left. **Either result is a measurement and neither is a licence** — a deletion still needs the clean run the rule above describes.

**A substitution removes nothing, so the asymmetry above has no analogue and neither does its licence.** What such a run bears on is whether to adopt the candidate wording or leave the current one standing, and the default is the wording already there: a reader is working under it, and adopting on a null buys a change with no evidence. **Where neither wording is in force yet** there is no such default, and a null decides nothing — say so rather than pick. **The citation test moves with the shape, and a substitution is scored on two measures.** The first is substance **one wording carries and the other does not** — what a seat can cite discriminatingly. The second is reliance on substance **both** carry, **attributed to the arm the seat sat in** and read as a rate across the arms, which is what a pair sharing all its substance leaves to measure; without it a clarity rewrite returns a determined null however cleanly the arms separated. Both are scored and both are reported, and the seat-by-seat confound reads on whichever measure a claimed separation came from — say which.

**The void gate (`../references/cold-seat-ab.md`) reads the first measure**: arms sharing all their substance agree for its purposes however their reliance rates differ, so a separation on the second measure alone does not discharge the plant reading.

**Neither measure licenses adopting the candidate: this instrument sets no adoption bar.** The run reports both measures, the plant's seat-by-seat overlap, whether the plant was a second route to the behaviour under test, and what its null bounds; the call to adopt, or to leave standing whichever wording is in force — or, where neither is, to pick or decline as the paragraph above says — is the proposer's, argued on that report, and is a decision like any other rather than something the run confers. **Where the two wordings would have a reader do different things, that call is the owner's under the fork test and reaches them argued.** No split and no proportion is named here because none has evidence behind it — no keep-or-delete disposition this instrument has licensed turned on how large a gap was rather than on whether any seat cited, and that presence test is calibrated by the deletion's *keeping is cheap*, which adopting reverses.

**Three seats an arm is the floor, and a floor is all it is.** A substitution strips nothing, so both arms keep everything around the sentence, its signal is weaker by construction, and a null at three here bounds less than a null at three does in a confirm. Reaching the floor makes a run readable, never sufficient.

**And a null bounds the effect rather than showing none**: nothing was removed, and by the paragraph above either wording's substance may reach a seat from what both arms keep — *no difference found* and *no difference this run could have found* being the same sentence otherwise.
