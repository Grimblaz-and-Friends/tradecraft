# Scoring a cold-seat A/B, and licensing a deletion from one

**Loaded when** you are scoring a cold-seat A/B's planted control, or the run's output would be the removal of the text under test.

**What counts as a citation, without which nothing below can be applied: a decline reasoned from something other than the text under test is not one** — most often the absence of a positive trigger, even where its content coincides with the text's. A seat attributing the effect *to the text* — *"its entry explicitly excludes push-rejection recovery"* — is citing, in whichever arm. Score both arms by the same reading, and say who scored: a run scored by the dispatching session is not blind to arm.

**Search the record before dispatching anything.** One place the text is already leaned on — an entry, a review, a session note — settles it for nothing, where the run below costs eight seats. What such a hit licenses is deliberately left open: it is evidence the text works, and whether it *keeps* the text under the rule below is a question this file does not answer.

**Deletion requires a run whose arms differ in that text alone, in which no seat, in either arm, cited the text or its substance, and which ran at least three seats an arm** — the width the [run this rule comes from](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/260#issuecomment-5503370780) separated at, and which that record notes is contested from above rather than below. **Anything else keeps**, one citation by one seat being enough. **A screen licenses nothing**: where the arms differ by a whole class the result is attributable to the class, and a per-target confirm has to follow before any one sentence goes.

The asymmetry is the cost asymmetry. Three seats an arm can show that text nobody reaches for is not carrying its situation; they cannot show that text one seat leaned on is safe to remove. Keeping inert text costs characters. Deleting live text costs a session routed where the text existed to stop it — which no guard catches, and which surfaces as somebody's bad change months later.

**Say which arm a keep came from, because a keep can be the blind spot rather than a measurement.** Where the only citations came from the arm the text had been removed from, a seat reconstructed the substance from what remained; text reconstructible that way can never clear this instrument, and calling it tested-and-kept overstates the run.
