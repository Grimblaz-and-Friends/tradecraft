# Merge, defense, and the terminal stage

**Loaded when** you are merging a panel's findings, defending the artifact against them, or ruling as the terminal stage. A prosecution seat does not need it.

**Merge — parent-owned, before defense.** The dispatching session merges the seats' findings: a duplicate is the same failure mode at the same location; a merged finding credits every finder, primary (first-surfacing) first. A seat that fails or returns unusable output is re-dispatched once; a panel still short proceeds, and the report records the degraded width. Zero findings from all seats is a valid outcome. **The merged list indexes the seat reports; it never replaces, edits, or restates them** [D-102], and content the merge adds that no seat wrote — a correction, a co-finder, an adverse note — is labeled as the merge's own.

**The merge records each finding's consequence shape**, so the stages that turn on it read a value rather than infer one — the same reason the finder states the consequence rather than leaving it to be read off the wording. **A finding's consequence shape is where its consequence lands** — on the artifact a consumer will use, or on the record of having reviewed it — read from the site the finding cites rather than from what the finding is about: a wrong count *in a review report* is apparatus-facing however badly a reader would act on it. **A finding citing sites of both kinds is artifact-facing**, so a cycle is never stopped early by a finding that also reaches the work. The report and the relevance terminus (`../references/the-record.md`) both turn on that axis, and it is what distinguishes a defect in the work from a defect in the record of having checked the work. **The merge records a shape only for what it merged**, so a ruling on an uncarried seat entry has none to read.

**Defense — one pass, always.** Presume the artifact innocent; read every cited line independently; re-run every probe a finding rests on; challenge only with concrete counter-evidence. Verdict per finding: **disproved**, **conceded**, or **insufficient-to-disprove**, with the evidence for each. The defense may originate findings its re-runs surface and may move severity with evidence. **For every finding it does not disprove, it states the remedy's price** [D-107] [D-113] [D-167] — the cost of implementing it, in two terms: what the fix touches (its regression surface), and what it obliges later sessions to do. Argued, never scored. Stating a price is not arguing a drop; a defense that declines a finding without a ruling on the record has suppressed it. Where a defense verdict can be settled by running something, run it.

**The terminal stage's docket is set by rule, not by the dispatcher** [D-102]: every merged finding, **anything in a seat's report that no merged finding carries: a remedy, a dissent, an examination that declined to file**, every defense verdict, every finding a commissioned pass returned (`../references/after-the-fix.md`), and every collision or open matter any stage flagged. Matters the dispatcher wants settled arrive as a labeled note on that docket, never as the docket.

**The terminal stage** — the judge on panels, the defense on routine reviews — applies two clauses per finding. **(a) Is it real, and would acting on it improve the artifact?** No → **drop**, saying what was verified and what was not found; still unclear after verification → drop, with its one-line reason and never silently. Everything else is **sustained**, and clause (b) decides what becomes of it.

**(b) What does acting on it cost in judgment?** Needing neither the owner's attention nor a design call, and not changing what this change is → **fix**, whatever the finding's scope. A defect this change did not undertake is still a defect in the tree it produces, and scope is not what decides whether a determined remedy lands. **Scope and the affirmed boundary are different things**: what a change merely happened not to touch is open, and what its artifact named as out is the owner's — which is the third limb, not this one. Needing any of the three → it is a **decision, not a fix**: it goes to the owner argued, or to the board where it would be worth someone's time as its own issue — never filed merely to be seen not dropping it. Real and its remedy determined, but the evidence that acting is worth it not yet there → **record** it, where a later review of this artifact meets it again; recurrence is what promotes a record. **A dispute the terminal stage cannot itself arbitrate cheaply goes to a spike** [D-167], which the dispatching session runs before it closes the review, or records why not.

**What makes a matter the owner's is the `charter` cell's fork test**, not a stage's sense that it is important — the whole fix bar turns on that word, so it is read there rather than guessed at here.

**A finding whose sites fall on both sides of the test takes one ruling in two tranches** — the determined part fixed, the rest handed on, each named — rather than one disposition forced onto the whole. One ruling per finding still holds; what divides is the remedy, not the ruling.

**The third limb is the owner's by construction** — affirming the pre-implementation artifact is what made this change's boundary theirs. It is deliberately not a size threshold: the smallest change can be the one that decides the most.

**A remedy that adds weight argues that weight.** Where the only available remedy is more prose on a governing surface, **record** beats **fix** unless the consequence earns the words — a review leaves the artifact no larger than its answer requires.

**Fixing needs no permission; declining to fix does.** A **drop**, a **record**, and a lens retirement are ruled by a stage that is not the session that built the change; that session's own moves are to fix, to put the matter to such a stage, or to file. The asymmetry is what lets the fix bar be low without becoming the builder deciding what is obvious.

Rules: verify independently before sustaining (re-read the cited artifact, re-execute contested probes, state what was verified); defer to no party — reject conceded findings and raise argued-down severity where the evidence says so; settle severity and price at the evidence, not the average of positions; one ruling per merged finding and per uncarried seat entry, no rebuttal rounds.

**Post the rulings to the PR or issue before the fix batch begins** — a ruling that lives only in a session cannot be read by the stage that follows it.

Two obligations fall on round one's ruling and are stated where the cycle they govern is (`../references/after-the-fix.md`): it **names the cycle budget**, and the post-fix look plus defense on what it finds is a floor it may not record or hand on in place of running. Reconcile the external pass (`../references/after-the-fix.md`) before the fix batch begins, on the same tree the rulings were made against.
