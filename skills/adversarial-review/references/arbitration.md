# Merge, defense, and the terminal stage

**Loaded when** merging a review's findings, defending the artifact, ruling as the terminal stage, or deciding what a finding met outside a review earns. A prosecution seat does not need it.

**Merge — before defense.** The holding session merges the seats' findings: a duplicate is the same failure mode at the same location; a merged finding credits every finder, primary (first-surfacing) first. A prosecution seat that fails or returns unusable output is re-dispatched once; a review still short proceeds, and the report records the degraded width. Zero findings from all seats is a valid outcome. **The merged list indexes the seat reports; it never replaces, edits, or restates them** [D-102], and content the merge adds that no seat wrote — a correction, a co-finder, an adverse note — is labeled as the merge's own. **Before it dispatches the defense, the merge accounts for everything in a seat's report that no merged finding carries — the class the docket below takes, so a finding and equally a remedy, a dissent, an examination that declined to file — naming each as an uncarried seat entry**, so anything dropped meets a stage that can still decline it. **The account is read off the seat's report itself, never off a total the seat states about itself**, and **like entries are named as one block taking one ruling — a seat's declined examinations are one entry rather than fifty — while a qualification inside a carried finding travels with it.** [D-453]

**The merge records each finding's consequence shape**, so the stages that turn on it read a value rather than infer one — the same reason the finder states the consequence rather than leaving it to be read off the wording. **A finding's consequence shape is where its consequence lands** — on the artifact a consumer will use, or on the record of having reviewed it — read from the site the finding cites rather than from what the finding is about: a wrong count *in a review report* is apparatus-facing however badly a reader would act on it. **A finding citing sites of both kinds is artifact-facing.** The report's split (`../references/the-record.md`) turns on that axis, and it is what distinguishes a defect in the work from a defect in the record of having checked the work. **The merge records a shape only for what it merged**, so a ruling on an uncarried seat entry has none to read.

**A required defense or judge truncates the review only where no seat on either vendor can be obtained; a failed attempt on the preferred vendor routes to the fallback.** Where no qualifying dispatch can supply it, the holder records what was tried, the missing stage and the undecided docket in the report. The review remains incomplete; no fitness ruling is claimed.

**Defense — one pass, always.** Presume the artifact innocent; read every cited line independently; re-run every probe a finding rests on; challenge only with concrete counter-evidence. Verdict per finding: **disproved**, **conceded**, or **insufficient-to-disprove**, with the evidence for each. The defense may originate findings its re-runs surface and may move severity with evidence. **For every finding it does not disprove, it states the remedy's price** [D-107] [D-113] [D-167] — the cost of implementing it, in two terms: what the fix touches (its regression surface), and what it obliges later sessions to do. Argued, never scored. Stating a price is not arguing a drop; a defense that declines a finding without a ruling on the record has suppressed it. Where a defense verdict can be settled by running something, run it.

**The terminal stage's docket is set by rule, not by the dispatcher** [D-102]: every merged finding, **anything in a seat's report that no merged finding carries: a finding, a remedy, a dissent, an examination that declined to file**, every defense verdict, every finding a commissioned pass returned (`../references/after-the-fix.md`), and every collision or open matter any stage flagged. Matters the dispatcher wants settled arrive as a labeled note on that docket, never as the docket.

**Work is bought, never owed.** The terminal stage — the judge on panels, the defense on routine reviews — tests whether each finding is real and what acting on it buys. Its fate is **bought**, **pitched**, or **lapsed**, with one line in the report stating the fate and its reason. A finding unsupported after verification lapses; a real finding is an offer rather than a debt to store for another review. **Sustained** means verified as a real finding, independently of purchase: a real offer lapsed on value or price still counts; an unsupported one does not.

**The affirmed brief is the budget.** A real failure of what it agreed is already bought and fixed. Beyond that undertaking, where the brief did not put the work out, the seat sells the fix to this stage on its value and price: what it touches, what it obliges later consumers to do, and whether it changes what this change is. The stage buys it into the batch or lets the offer lapse. What the owner explicitly put outside the brief, or what would change what the change is for, is theirs: pitch it as an issue in the argued form the `charter` cell routes to. A pitch asks for a purchase; it does not authorize the batch to build it.

**Role separation is the `engagement` cell's.** The builder may fix a finding without permission. [D-230] Outside a review too, a lapse needs an independent ruling recorded on the work. The report and the issue keep the fate and its reason; nothing writes a held-findings entry or asks a later review to meet it again.

**A pitch meets the filing standard.** Search first, and put fresh evidence on an open pitch that covers it rather than creating a symptom issue or a duty to re-file it later. Where the evidence does not earn a pitch, the offer lapses with that reason. A closed pitch is searchable history; recurrence must sell the work on its own evidence. The `filing` cell owns the search and evidence bar.

**Verify independently before buying or lapsing**: re-read the cited artifact, re-execute contested probes, and state what was verified. Defer to no party — reject conceded findings and raise argued-down severity where the evidence says so; severity and remedy price are separate judgments, not an average of positions. A disputed behavior claim goes to a spike where a run can settle it cheaply, before the fate is ruled. Give each merged finding and each uncarried seat entry one ruling; no rebuttal rounds. A lens retirement likewise needs a stage independent of the builder.

**The holder posts the rulings to the PR or issue before returning the fix batch to the implementer** — a ruling that lives only in a session cannot be read by the stage that follows it.

One obligation falls on the ruling and is stated where the floor it governs is (`../references/after-the-fix.md`): the fix batch owes an executable floor it may not record or hand on in place of running. The holder reconciles the external pass (`../references/after-the-fix.md`) before the fix batch begins, on the same tree the rulings were made against.
