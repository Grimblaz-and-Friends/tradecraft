# Merge, defense, and the terminal stage

**Loaded when** you are merging a panel's findings, defending the artifact against them, or ruling as the terminal stage. A prosecution seat does not need it.

**Merge — parent-owned, before defense.** The dispatching session merges the seats' findings: a duplicate is the same failure mode at the same location; a merged finding credits every finder, primary (first-surfacing) first. A seat that fails or returns unusable output is re-dispatched once; a panel still short proceeds, and the report records the degraded width. Zero findings from all seats is a valid outcome. **The merged list indexes the seat reports; it never replaces, edits, or restates them** [D-102], and content the merge adds that no seat wrote — a correction, a co-finder, an adverse note — is labeled as the merge's own.

**The merge records each finding's consequence shape**, so the stages that turn on it read a value rather than infer one — the same reason the finder states the consequence rather than leaving it to be read off the wording. **A finding's consequence shape is where its consequence lands** — on the artifact a consumer will use, or on the record of having reviewed it — read from the site the finding cites rather than from what the finding is about: a wrong count *in a review report* is apparatus-facing however badly a reader would act on it. **A finding citing sites of both kinds is artifact-facing.** The report's split (`../references/the-record.md`) turns on that axis, and it is what distinguishes a defect in the work from a defect in the record of having checked the work. **The merge records a shape only for what it merged**, so a ruling on an uncarried seat entry has none to read.

**Defense — one pass, always.** Presume the artifact innocent; read every cited line independently; re-run every probe a finding rests on; challenge only with concrete counter-evidence. Verdict per finding: **disproved**, **conceded**, or **insufficient-to-disprove**, with the evidence for each. The defense may originate findings its re-runs surface and may move severity with evidence. **For every finding it does not disprove, it states the remedy's price** [D-107] [D-113] [D-167] — the cost of implementing it, in two terms: what the fix touches (its regression surface), and what it obliges later sessions to do. Argued, never scored. Stating a price is not arguing a drop; a defense that declines a finding without a ruling on the record has suppressed it. Where a defense verdict can be settled by running something, run it.

**The terminal stage's docket is set by rule, not by the dispatcher** [D-102]: every merged finding, **anything in a seat's report that no merged finding carries: a remedy, a dissent, an examination that declined to file**, every defense verdict, every finding a commissioned pass returned (`../references/after-the-fix.md`), and every collision or open matter any stage flagged. **Every finding an earlier review recorded against this artifact is on that docket too, and takes one ruling from this stage** — met once per review rather than once per seat. Seats read those entries in their assignment (`../references/roster.md`) and file, as their own findings, whatever an entry now earns; what they do not do is rule it. **A re-filing merges into the carried entry rather than beside it**, so the recurrence and the entry it promotes take one ruling and not two. The ruling sits here rather than at the merge because the merge is the dispatching session's and may be the change's author, and a decline is owed by a stage that is not. [Seventeen entries across five seats cost eighty-five rulings on one round, twelve to fifteen per seat identical because the change never went near the file](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/343). Matters the dispatcher wants settled arrive as a labeled note on that docket, never as the docket.

**The terminal stage** — the judge on panels, the defense on routine reviews — applies two clauses per finding. **(a) Is it real, and would acting on it improve the artifact?** No → **drop**, saying what was verified and what was not found; still unclear after verification → drop, with its one-line reason and never silently. Everything else is **sustained**, and clause (b) decides what becomes of it.

**(b) What does acting on it cost in judgment?** **The batch admits only a finding whose stated consequence is one a consumer would live** — an action they would take wrongly, or a failure that would occur. A finding whose consequence is only that the text is wrong, imprecise or improvable is **record**, whatever its remedy costs, **and that is the precedence wherever fix and record both fit**. **A deletion finding clears the bar on the weight itself** — net growth on governing prose is a finding in its own right, so this bar withdraws no standing this file already gives. Then: needing neither the owner's attention nor a design call, and not changing what this change is → **fix**, whatever the finding's scope. A defect this change did not undertake is still a defect in the tree it produces, and scope is not what decides whether a determined remedy lands. **Scope and the affirmed boundary are different things**: what a change merely happened not to touch is open, and what the brief they affirmed put out is the owner's — which is the third limb, not this one. Needing any of the three → it is a **decision, not a fix**: it goes to the owner argued, or to the board where it would be worth someone's time as its own issue — never filed merely to be seen not dropping it. Real and its remedy determined, but the evidence that acting is worth it not yet there → **record** it, where a later review of this artifact meets it again; recurrence is what promotes a record. **A finding on the change's own record is never `record`**: the entry would be keyed to the change under review, which is a sink (`../references/the-record.md`), so it is fixed before that record freezes, or dropped with its reason. **A dispute the terminal stage cannot itself arbitrate cheaply goes to a spike** [D-167], which the dispatching session runs before it closes the review, or records why not.

**What makes a matter the owner's is the `charter` cell's fork test**, not a stage's sense that it is important — the whole fix bar turns on that word, so it is read there rather than guessed at here.

**A finding whose sites fall on both sides of the test takes one ruling in two tranches** — the determined part fixed, the rest handed on, each named — rather than one disposition forced onto the whole. One ruling per finding still holds; what divides is the remedy, not the ruling.

**The third limb is the owner's when the brief they affirmed reaches the boundary** — what that brief put out of scope is theirs; what only the artifact's own reading excluded is the session's, and a finding crossing it is a fix. It is deliberately not a size threshold: the smallest change can be the one that decides the most.

**A remedy that adds weight argues that weight.** Where the only available remedy is more prose on a governing surface, **record** beats **fix** unless the consequence earns the words — a review leaves the artifact no larger than its answer requires.

**Fixing needs no permission; declining to fix does.** A **drop**, a **record**, and a lens retirement are ruled by a stage that is not the session that built the change; that session's own moves are to fix, to put the matter to such a stage, or to file. The asymmetry is what lets the fix bar be low without becoming the builder deciding what is obvious.

Rules: verify independently before sustaining (re-read the cited artifact, re-execute contested probes, state what was verified); defer to no party — reject conceded findings and raise argued-down severity where the evidence says so; settle severity and price at the evidence, not the average of positions; one ruling per merged finding, per uncarried seat entry, and per carried recorded finding, no rebuttal rounds.

**Post the rulings to the PR or issue before the fix batch begins** — a ruling that lives only in a session cannot be read by the stage that follows it.

One obligation falls on the ruling and is stated where the floor it governs is (`../references/after-the-fix.md`): the fix batch owes an executable floor it may not record or hand on in place of running. Reconcile the external pass (`../references/after-the-fix.md`) before the fix batch begins, on the same tree the rulings were made against.
