# The fix batch — what binds while writing it, what it owes, and the passes around it

**Loaded when** you are writing a review's fix batch, discharging what it owes once it has landed, a pull request is open and an automated reviewer may have posted on it, or you invoked a review tool yourself. The three standards in the first section bind every stage, as they did when the cell body held them; **the rest of this file** a review's seats, defense and judge do not need.

On the unattended stretch, the holder resumes the builder for the whole ruled batch: prescribed remedies depend on the finding and tree, including small ones, and disputed builder choices depend on the builder's reasons. Holder-authored accounts then follow the `engagement` cell's **Source-verification rule**.

## While the fixes are written

- **A new test pin must go red against the pre-fix revision** — a green suite proves nothing about the defect.
- **Derive a correction from its source; never adjust a wrong claim until it looks right.** Re-run the query or re-read the passage at fix time and write what it returns. A count over a corpus the change itself writes into is a query, not a number. Land the fix where the claim is *read* as well as where it is written.
- **A fix changes the state the finding was diagnosed in** — re-derive the diagnosis and its probe against the tree the fix produces; a probe re-run unchanged answers the pre-fix question. A fix that reaches *n* sites creates *n* more to check, including sites the fix itself introduces.

## The fix batch — what it owes, and what looks at the result

**A review is one round, and the fix batch is its last act** [D-371]. The panel or the routine lane's seats, the defense, the terminal stage, the batch, and the floor below — once each, and the review closes. **No further prosecution pass is bought, ruled, or recorded**, and no stage names a budget for one: [six cycles against a budget of two wrote a fresh false or imprecise claim in five of their six batches, every one in the change's own record](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/138#issuecomment-5521380432). What reads the result instead is use, below.

**The executable floor — mandatory, and never recorded or handed on in place of being run.** The resumed builder receiving the whole ruled batch runs it against the tree that batch produces: the suites, the lint, and each sustained finding's own probe re-derived against that tree. **Where a sustained finding carried no probe the floor owes nothing for it** — the floor is what executes, and a check invented at fix time is a fresh instrument answering a question no stage asked. **What the floor could not reach is named in the report**, so *nothing executable touched these fixes* and *the floor ran clean* stop being one silence.

**The implementer re-derives every figure the batch invalidates and re-reads every sentence turning on a term, contract or definition it moved — whose characters never changed — on every surface the batch changes.** It returns changed evidence to the holder, who rechecks and revises the change's records, including the pull request body and a decision entry not yet merged; the implementer rechecks its commit message. A fix moves the tree the records describe, so each writer checks its own text before it is installed or published. **An implementer departing from an ordered remedy returns the ordered shape and its reason to the holder, who records the departure where the batch lands**: with no look after it the departure is stated in the batch or nowhere, and a ruling nobody can tell was departed from has stopped meaning anything. Once the sources exist, the holder sends the report draft and release-ready pull request body through the fresh source check the `engagement` cell's stretch depth assigns before posting the report; after that checked report supplies its URL, dependent record content goes through its own source-ready check before installation.

**What looks at the result is use, and it is two things.** The experience session the batch buys on the fixed tree — where it rewrote what the material instructs, or changed what a consumer can do — reads that tree before the review closes; the holder returns its findings to the implementer for repairs whose batch owes this floor and nothing further. A decline takes an independent ruling; where none is available inside the review, the holder puts the offer to an independent stage outside the review or pitches it under the filing standard. Then **friction met under merged material in ordinary later work is the standing intake**: it routes to a filing, which is where what a further round would have caught now arrives, and the review's residual risk is accepted against it.

## The external pass — reconciled every time

**An external pass is what automation configured on the repository posts on the pull request without being asked**, which is why publishing before the panel makes it concurrent rather than serial. Paths in the collection example below resolve against this reference file's directory, following the anchoring sentence in the `filing` cell's `the-pool.md`. The bundle is review evidence rather than shipped material, so store it outside `skills/` at the repository root, for example `../../../external-pass-PR_NUMBER.json`: `python ../scripts/external_pass.py collect --repo OWNER/REPO --pr PR_NUMBER --output ../../../external-pass-PR_NUMBER.json`. The receipt's printed replay command runs from the repository root. Bundles are not committed; the report carries a durable link beside its receipt, so a later reader retrieves one to the repository-root path that receipt names and replays it. The command preserves complete raw candidates from its three REST collections, not a discovery or count of every configured external reviewer: a source can contain a human comment, while a reviewer reporting only in a pull request body or check run is outside those collections. Before its material first enters a dispatch, the holder collects that bundle and carries it with its complete receipt block under the dispatch contract. The holder reconciles after the terminal stage and before the fix batch, obtaining independent rulings for new offers; it reconciles again if a push triggered a re-review, which reads the fixes and so checks the fix batch independently of the floor above. At each reconciliation it runs the receipt's `verify` command: a match confirms both the recorded bundle bytes and source remain the source being dispositioned, while drift requires a new bundle at a new path and dispositions for every added, removed, moved, or changed item. The receipt's counts are cross-checks only: read every returned object's prose, including issue and inline comments and submitted-review bodies, because a finding need not have an inline-comment object of its own. **A comment arriving after the batch rides that same batch**: the batch is not finished until reconciliation is, and the floor runs after it — a review is one round, and that round does not end while an input it owes a disposition is still arriving. Every external finding gets a disposition, including one in any returned object's body with no standalone inline-comment object: duplicate of an internal finding (named as such), sustained and fixed, pitched with its issue, or lapsed on the evidence or the value with a one-line reason. Lapsing an external comment, including one arriving after the terminal ruling or a fix-batch push, needs a ruling independent of the builder. **Sustain on independent grounds or not at all** — an external reviewer's cited rule may not be one this repo runs; where the repo's own convention contradicts it, the convention wins and the lapse says so. The holder's external-pass job starts in a shell with authenticated `gh`, network access, and its supplied permission or approval route. Retry a failed collection or verification; if GitHub remains unavailable, retain the error as an explicit gap and obtain an independent ruling to defer the review rather than silently lapse its input. A stale receipt cannot close the review. Record what actually posted even when it produced nothing; configured reviewers stub, rate-limit, and skip. Where the repository keeps an index, book that qualitative outcome as a non-empty string under top-level `external`, never as arithmetic and never under `seats`.

**A review tool the session invokes itself is a commissioned pass, not an external one.** On the unattended stretch, the holder commissions it. It is asked for, no push triggers it, and none of the availability states above can apply to it, so the paragraph above does not govern it. It is not a seat either: invoking a tool supplies neither the cold boundary, nor the shared block, nor a lens brief, and who invoked it is not what makes a seat. Its findings **are a member of the terminal stage's docket** (`../references/arbitration.md`), whose enumeration is set by rule and reaches this one, and the report records what was run, what it returned, and each disposition. **It runs before that stage, where an external pass reconciles after it** — the inversion is the point: a pass you commissioned is evidence you chose to gather, and evidence arrives before the ruling it informs. It is never booked under `seats` or in the index's `external`, both of which would misdescribe its provenance.
