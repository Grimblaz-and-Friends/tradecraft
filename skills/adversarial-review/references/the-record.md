# Ending the review

**Loaded when** you are closing a review: writing its final report, ruling whether another pass runs, or appending its row to the review index. Nothing before the terminal ruling needs it.

The final report, posted to the PR or issue, carries: the lane and why; the purpose statement judged against; per-seat counts (raw / merged / sustained, highs broken out), with each seat's model and runtime; **each sustained high's finders** — every credited seat as the merge records them, primary first and a merge-added co-finder labeled as its own; or the stage or seat that originated it where no merge carried it; and where a merge carried it with no finder list to copy, that fact; every finding's disposition, with each drop's one-line reason and each priced-out ruling's price; the external pass's outcome; **the split by consequence shape**; whether a session note was carried; the terminal fitness ruling; and the **next-pass ruling** — run or stop. The default is stop once a pass has sustained no high it will fix; continuing argues the next pass is worth its cost, and stopping names the residual risk accepted and what is expected to catch it instead. A run ruling binds the session that wrote it: dispatch the pass before closing, or record why not.

**A pass continues only while the findings it expects are about the artifact.** A look returning only apparatus-facing material — the review's own bookkeeping, the record of having reviewed — has reached the **relevance terminus**, and the ruling names it as the reason it stops, whatever the cycle budget still allows — the budget round one's ruling named (`../references/after-the-fix.md`), which also carries the count terminus this one races; whichever fires first ends the cycle. It is a second condition because it closes a second failure: findings real, probe-backed and correctly arbitrated, on a subject that has drifted from the change to the record of reviewing it — which a budget counting rounds cannot see. The split above is what makes that drift legible while stopping is still cheap, taken over every finding the pass returned.

Where the repo keeps a review index, append one row per review — date, artifact, lane, per-seat counts, what came of the findings (`dispositions`), the split by consequence shape (`facing`), the model and runtime that staffed it (`staffing`), report URL — written once and never maintained. **Every count sums across every cycle the review ran** — one row per review, never one per cycle — and **the unit counted is the finding as originated and dispositioned**, never a row of a ruling table: folding two findings into one remedy is an execution convenience, not a merge, and a finding split across two limbs takes two rulings and contributes two entries. A seat's `merged` counts that seat's own findings surviving the merge, so a finding spanning two merged entries counts once; a seat's `high` counts the sustained highs that seat is credited on, so the column carries credits and never distinct highs, and summing it across seats does not give the review's high count; **a seat key may book an aggregate** — `panel`, where the merge recorded no per-seat split — saying in the row what it aggregates. **The dispositions are the terminal stage's own four, spelled `fixed`, `routed`, `priced_out`, `dismissed`**, copied from its rulings rather than re-derived, and including the dismissals: a record that counts what a review was worth while never counting what it wasted can only ever argue for more review. **`facing` splits those same rulings by consequence shape** — defined, with the site-of-citation rule and the both-kinds tiebreak, in `../references/arbitration.md` — `artifact` and `apparatus`, one entry per ruling and so summing to the dispositions total, dismissals included — so the trend between them is a query, not a read of every report. **The dispositions do not reconcile against the per-seat columns and are not meant to.** **Where seats were staffed unevenly, say so in the value** — `fable (cold-read), opus (rest)` — and spell a runtime the same way every time — one spelling per runtime, as `claude-code (windows)` — or the per-runtime evidence the record exists for cannot be gathered.

Before reading what such a record says, ask what it could have said: an evidence loop whose instrument cannot express the finding it tests for is not evidence, and a record is silent about everything its fields do not carry. **A convention stated per row is one no later row inherits**, which is why the counting rules above are stated here once. A seat that ran and found nothing appears with zeros; a seat that failed unreplaced is omitted, with the report recording the degraded width. Where the repo keeps no index, the report alone is the record.
