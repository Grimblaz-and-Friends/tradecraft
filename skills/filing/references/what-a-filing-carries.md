# Creation carries the facts; pickup designs the solution

**Loaded when** you are writing the body of an issue or an extending comment, or deciding whether a finding about governing prose has earned a number of its own. The fields are `../references/issue-template.md`'s; this file is the standard they meet.

**An issue states work the owner bought, and carries that work as facts only:** what happened or the observed want, where it happened, the evidence, provenance, ties, and the change on which it was encountered. Observed product harm belongs with those facts. A filing carries no fix, option menu, recommendation, or argument for one remedy's value over another, because purchase is not permission to pre-empt the owner's design judgment at pickup. Observed evidence survives a change in vocabulary; an invented solution does not substitute for it.

**A stated cost carries its evidence status wherever it appears.** Give the run and result that establish it, or mark it `unrun`. At pickup, test an unrun cost before designing against it; if it cannot be tested, record it as an assumption rather than pricing the options as though it were established.

**Pickup designs the solution with the owner in turns, and the implementation brief records that sitting.** It creates no separate solution document and does not inherit a suggested fix from a prior filing or extending comment.

**Where the evidence is itself a rule, the rule's own sentence is the observation.** Quote it with its location, because the quote survives the file moving. Which rule is breached is evidence; what replaces it is design, and stays out.

**An issue whose subject is governing prose — prose a later session is expected to act on — carries an incident from real work, or a run: the same situation put to a session under the text as it stands and under what would replace it, behaving differently.** The breached rule's own sentence above is what is wrong with the text; this is what acts differently for it, and an issue carrying only the first is not filed. The bar sits at creation because an issue picked up is an implementation brief, an artifact and a change — a rule by another door; what an incident is not is the `charter` cell's. Nothing else is gated here — a defect in a script or a guard carries its consequence in the failure it produces.

**Where the defect can be demonstrated, the demonstration is the evidence** — the probe command and its output, not a sentence reporting what running it shows. A reproduction cannot be misread, survives the vocabulary moving around it, and gives pickup a tested fact. Prose is the fallback rather than the default, and the right form for what has no failing run.

**The floor:** carry enough evidence that a session picking the work up can confirm the problem is real without redoing the discovery that found it.

**An issue carries no sentence attesting its own compliance** — not that it is minimal, not that the search was run: the evidence and the ties are its artifact, and a compliance sentence nobody can check buys nothing. Provenance is different and belongs, carried as a keyed element rather than left to the prose — one line under the heading `**Provenance:**` whose first word after that heading is an origin from a closed list, because the point is a key rather than a description; where none fits, the nearest, and why, on the same line.
