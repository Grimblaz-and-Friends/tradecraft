# filing

**Purpose:** make each issue a useful factual statement of work the owner bought, and give every unfixed finding one of two ends. **Audience:** a session creating or extending an issue, or routing a finding it will not fix in the current change. **Success:** every issue carries what happened, where, its evidence, provenance, ties and originating change without a proposed solution; every open issue is work the owner bought; and new instances extend the open issue they support.

## Where this cell's depth lives

- **Creating or extending an issue, or deciding whether a match covers the evidence in hand** → `references/the-search.md`: the search across open and closed issues, the extension that takes no new number, and the ties a new issue carries.
- **Writing the tie block or reading cause links between existing issues** → `references/naming-a-tie.md`: the tie vocabulary, the sub-issue link, and what a ranking does with them.
- **Writing an issue or deciding whether its evidence earns one** → `references/what-a-filing-carries.md`: the evidence floor and governing-prose bar, the facts a filing states, and what design stays with pickup.
- **Copying the shape of an issue body or an extending comment** → `references/issue-template.md`: the fields to copy whole, the tie block first among them; the other files here are the standard those fields meet.

## What an unfixed finding does

**No issue is created for work the owner has not bought, and a purchase does not license the filing to propose its solution.** Deferred attention is attention, taken twice — once to pick the item up, once to agree its brief — so a queue of agent-proposed work costs what asking costs, paid later by someone who did not choose to pay it. The two ends below are how a session discharges an unfixed finding, and it takes exactly one of them.

- **Fixed now** — in the current change where it caused the finding, otherwise in one immediate follow-up pull request carrying the rest together, which keeps the cost one pass rather than one per finding.
- **The release report's ask to the owner, once**, carrying the proposed fix and its cost.

**The owner's answer is the other moment, and it splits two ways: bought or not.** Bought creates an issue carrying the facts the ask established, picked up fresh to design the solution with the owner; not bought creates nothing and is a decline recorded on the work.

**An issue records bought work as facts rather than a proposed solution.** Search first, because new evidence belongs on the open issue it supports rather than in a second number. A repository chooses how it marks and orders the work it decided; this cell owns only what the issue itself carries.
