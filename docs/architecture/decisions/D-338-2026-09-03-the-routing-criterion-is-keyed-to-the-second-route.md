# D-338: A routing criterion names which quantity it claims, and reach is falsifiable only where nothing else routes the situation

**Status:** Accepted 2026-09-03 (PR #338)

**Evidence, and where it can and cannot be checked from here.** Every figure attributed to #255 and #272 below rests on a GitHub comment, so no reader inside this repository can check one; that exposure is stated rather than smoothed, and it is the second consecutive entry carrying it after [D-320]'s own ([#324](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/324)). Everything quoted from a live file is pinned to `skills/` at `70d59b7`, this change's base. Two runs, neither commissioned by this change and neither re-run for it: [#272's cold-seat A/B](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/272#issuecomment-5500944347) and [#255's five-arm harness run](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/255#issuecomment-5486527919). The brief and its affirmation are on [#272](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/272#issuecomment-5520329963); the settled artifact is [there too](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/272#issuecomment-5520387705).

## The condition

`skills/engagement/SKILL.md` carried the general rule — *"A criterion a plausible wrong implementation would also pass measures nothing"* [D-166] — and no instance of it. **PR #269's criterion 1 was written under that rule and passed a check that could not fail.** It read *a session whose only job is reconciling what an automated reviewer posted on a pull request reaches this cell*, named its falsifier as a cold seat holding only the roster, was affirmed by the owner, run, and marked met. That PR's terminal ruling recorded it as a criterion a plausible wrong implementation would also pass, and promoted the recorded finding that became #272.

The session that wrote it had the general rule loaded. That is the whole argument for an instance: a rule stated only in the abstract was applied by a competent session and did not catch the case it exists to catch.

## The decision

**One bullet, immediately after the rule it instantiates.** A criterion claiming a piece of text routes a reader names **which of two quantities** it claims — that a reader *reaches* the destination, or that a reader's *stated reason* names the text. **Reach is falsifiable only where nothing else the reader loads routes that same situation to the same place**, and two routes are named because both have actually defeated a run: the destination's own summary sentence sitting above the trigger under test, and an always-on surface naming the destination. **Warrant stays falsifiable where reach does not**, so the rule hands over a replacement rather than removing a form and leaving nothing in its place.

**The rule is keyed to the second route, not to the quantity.** This is the change's central judgement, it was the owner's ruling, and it is the one thing a later session is most likely to flatten back.

The disposition that scoped this work proposed *"a routing criterion is falsifiable only as a warrant … and never as reach."* **That wording is false against this repository's own second measurement.** #255's port run reached the standard's file **5/6 in the port arm against 0/6 in the control** and 1/6 with a direct cell name — a reach readout that separated cleanly. What killed reach in #272's run was not the quantity: it was that the description's opening summary still routed `substrate` after the trigger clause was deleted, so **both arms carried a working route**. The same defeat is visible in #255 as a control rather than as a surprise — its `brief` case saturated **5/5 in every arm including the negative control**, which that report attributes to the always-on charter sentence naming `engagement`: *"Every arm therefore had a working route and the case could not discriminate."*

So the two runs do not disagree. They are the same mechanism observed with and without a second route, and the rule states the mechanism rather than one of its outcomes.

## What was rejected

**A flat ban on the reach form** — *never write "a session reaches this cell"; write the warrant instead*. It is one clause shorter and needs no judgement from the writer. It was rejected because it would classify #255's port measurement as unfalsifiable, and a rule that outlaws a measurement the repository has already run successfully teaches the next session the wrong thing about its own instruments. Put to the owner as option 2; not ruled.

**Recording the incident and adding no rule**, on the ground that [D-166]'s general rule already covers it. Rejected as the status quo that produced the incident. Put as option 3; not ruled.

**Generalising to any criterion about reader behaviour.** The evidence covers routing — a reader arriving at a destination — and nothing was measured about criteria of other shapes. Generalising from two runs would be the superlative-without-enumeration error this repository has already caught twice, at [D-166] and [D-322].

**Deleting `substrate`'s `when choosing a language for new code,` clause**, which #272's spike priced at 39 always-on characters at no routing cost. [D-320] reads the same evidence the other way — keep on any citation, and every treatment seat cited it — and scoped its own rule to fence clauses expressly to avoid the collision, leaving no rule for positive triggers. **That contradiction is [#325](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/325)'s sixth question and is not settled here.** This entry licenses no deletion.

**A third run.** The premise this rests on is that the second route is what discriminates, and the two existing runs exercise it in opposite directions. A run built to re-measure what they agree on would spend seats to confirm what was already reported.

## The placement call, and what it cost

**The bullet is in the body, not behind a pointer.** `engagement` has the largest body in the tree and no budget — the cell-body rows of `python tools/lint.py`, run from the repository root. Against that: the five items the list carried at `70d59b7` are all in the body, `skills/authoring/SKILL.md` sheds depth to `references/` on a disqualifier rather than on a size, and an instance separated from the rule it instantiates is one a reader meets without the rule that explains it. Moving the whole list behind a pointer is a restructure of the cell and was not in this change's boundary.

**What it cost, since the heading promises a figure.** The bullet's size, and `engagement`'s body before and after, are the cell-body rows of `python tools/lint.py` run from the repository root against this change's base `70d59b7` and against its head; the bullet's own length is the character count of the line it occupies. **The change grows that body**, and a review round's fix batch grew it further rather than shrinking it, which is argued below rather than left as a delta.

## What the review changed, and the one place it grew the surface

A five-seat panel, a defense and a judge ran on `80d4952`. The judge ruled the change **fit for purpose once the named fixes land**, on the ground that the governing condition — *"Reach is falsifiable only where nothing else the reader loads routes that same situation to the same place"* — survived every attack unamended, while the sentences around it did not.

**The rewrite deleted more than it added, and then one finding bought the difference back.** The judge's ordered replacement was a net **9-character reduction**: it struck a consequence clause that overreached (saying reach passes in the ablated arm unconditionally, which is false and is the flat ban this entry records as declined), struck the evidence label *"both measured rather than reasoned"* whose strong reading contradicts [D-320]'s own residual standard, and took with them the only occurrence of the undefined term *arm*. It spent that shed on the ceiling **warrant** has: [D-320] records five places where an ablated seat supplied a removed clause's substance as its reason, so the original closing promise — *"a routing claim always has a form that can fail"* — was a universal this repository had falsified the day before. That was the round's strongest finding.

**Then the external pass found what five seats did not, and it is why the bullet ends larger.** Codex, reviewing `777ddc4`, posted that a second route defeats reach only where it **saturates** the outcome: a route that carries some readers and not others still leaves an ablation able to separate. It cited [D-210]'s own measurement — *"of two cold consumers whose jobs met the condition, one reached the rule through the description and one did not … A description that loads is necessary for firing and is not sufficient"* — and this entry's own #255 figure of 1 of 6 for a body-sited cell name says the same. The panel had reached the defect (`operational` F7, merged as M6) without that evidence and the judge recorded it; the external comment supplied the in-tree instance the decline rested on lacking, so it was fixed rather than re-recorded, under the charter's asymmetry that fixing needs no permission while declining does.

**That clause is what takes the bullet from a net reduction to a net growth.** Both figures are the `engagement` cell-body row of `python tools/lint.py`, run from the repository root at this change's base `70d59b7` and at the tree the review's fix batch produced; **the unit is characters, not bytes** — the struck clause carried an em dash, so a byte count of the same two lines disagrees with the tree's own instrument by two, and one reviewer computed it that way. The reduction the judge's rewrite alone would have made is read off the review record, which states the replacement text it ordered. The weight is argued rather than absorbed: without it the rule is categorically wrong in the direction that matters most here — it would retire the reach form for every destination any loaded text mentions, which is the option the owner declined, arriving through the back door. Four recorded findings decline fixes on this cell for its size, and this entry is on the record as having grown it anyway, for that reason.

**Two findings were sustained and deliberately not fixed.** The bullet is silent on routing *away* — a fence clause claims the mirror, and [D-320]'s disqualifier for that polarity (*"a decline reasoned from the absence of a positive trigger is not a citation"*) ships in no cell — which needs a design call and goes to the board. And `skills/spikes/SKILL.md`'s *"Arms differing in exactly the sentence under test"* is a condition #272's run satisfied while measuring nothing, so the cell a run-designer loads still states an insufficient test; that is the instrument's, and #325's.

## What this change does not decide

**The instrument's own rules stay with the instrument**, on #325 — the planted control, the void rule, keep-on-any-citation and the forced per-cell verdict. This entry says when a criterion is falsifiable; it does not say what a licensing run must carry, and a clause here that constrained how an A/B is built would land the same obligation in two cells.

**Nothing the charter names moves.** Whether an always-on surface should stop naming cells outright is [#303](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/303)'s, and #255's evidence runs the other way there — the charter sentence was the one pointer followed in every arm, 5 of 5, where a direct cell name in a body routed 1 of 6.
