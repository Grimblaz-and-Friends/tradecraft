# D-262: A filing carries the failing run where one exists, one pass's single-change defects land as one number, and the fifth tie verb is declined

**Status:** Accepted 2026-08-30 (PR #262)

## Context

Two wants against `skills/filing/SKILL.md`. [#219](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/219) asks the evidence floor to prefer a failing reproduction where one is possible, on the asymmetry that a filing outlives a review and yet was held to a weaker evidence standard than a review finding is. [#244](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/244) asks for a fourth filing outcome — one pass's several defects landing as one issue rather than several cross-tied numbers — on the observation that three changes under the new emission rules produced eleven filings bundling into roughly six changes.

Both are that cell's design, and the owner ruled on #219 that one opening of the cell carries both in a single convergence. #244 named three questions as deliberately deferred: the wording, where the line falls between a batch and a genuinely separable pair, and whether the flagged *filed-with* relationship joins the tie set. #219 named two: the wording, and what *possible* excludes.

The prompting incident is on the record and is not a review finding about governing prose: [#233](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/233), [#234](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/234) and [#235](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/235) each carry, independently, the line *"This relationship is none of the four verbs — flagged as a candidate for the set."* Three filers reaching for the same missing expressiveness is what admits the want; what it admits is a want, not the remedy those filers reached for.

## Decision

**The evidence floor gains a preference, not a requirement.** Where the defect can be demonstrated, the demonstration is the evidence — the probe command and its output, not a sentence reporting what running it shows. The clause naming what has no failing run is load-bearing rather than decorative: a want, an idea, and a breached rule are named in the same sentence, and the rule case is routed to the cell's existing *the rule's own sentence is the observation* paragraph directly above rather than restated. A mandatory floor would have been the plausible wrong implementation — it blocks the filings that carry the practice's wants, which have nothing to reproduce by construction — and the affirmed artifact's third criterion exists to falsify exactly that.

**The batch test is the change, not the surface: would one change fix these?** #244's own phrasing was *share a surface **and** a likely single change*, and the second half is the load-bearing one. #233, #234 and #235 ran the experiment before the rule existed: filed out of one review, each naming the other two. #233 names `tools/lint.py` and #234 names `tools/roster.py` — **different files, and they landed in one PR** ([#247](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/pull/247)); #235 named a shipped skill and landed alone. A same-file test therefore fails in **both** directions on the one case available, splitting a real batch and admitting a false one, which is why the surface half is stated as neither deciding nor required rather than dropped silently. Re-derive the pairing with `gh issue view 233 --json closedByPullRequestsReferences` and the same on 234 and 235.

**The flagged relationship does not become a fifth tie verb.** The ties table carries its own admission test — *a tie name earns its place by changing what a ranking does with the pair* — and the batch outcome removes the cases that would pass it: filings that would be one change are now one issue and have no pair to rank. What is left is #235's residue, *a session picking up any one can re-use the others' probes*, which is **provenance** — a fact about who found this and how — and the cell already admits provenance and already routes it. The asymmetry decided the close call: declining costs a sentence to add later on a real sighting, while admitting spreads a verb into filed issue bodies across the board that a reversal would strand. This is recorded here rather than only in the PR because three filers proposed the verb independently and a fourth will; the answer a fourth needs is the admission test applied, not the proposal re-litigated.

**[#94](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/94) and [#95](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/95) move to the batch bullet**, and the *file new with named ties* bullet states its rule plainly. That exhibit records two lawful filings against one sentence *"merged into a single PR later"* whose pairing had to be reconstructed — which is the cost of not batching, not the cost of not tying. It sat under the ties bullet because batching did not exist as an outcome when it was written; it is evidence of what this change adds, and moving it is what keeps the addition from needing new evidence of its own.

**The four outcomes stay mutually exclusive and the ties machinery is untouched.** A batch issue names its ties like any other, and the existing rule that a pass creating more than one issue is not done until each names the others still governs the residue — batching reduces how often that rule fires, and does not repeal it.

## Outflow

**None owed, and this is a fact about which surface was edited rather than a judgement about size.** The obligation attaches to editing an always-on surface. The only always-on part of a cell is its name and description, which load whether or not the cell fires; the body costs nothing until it does. This change edits the body alone. `python tools/lint.py` reports the always-on total on any tree, and running it on this tree and on the merge base returns the same total — the check that the claim is a fact and not an assertion.

**The description was considered and deliberately not edited.** Its existing triggers — *when about to create an issue, or when deciding whether something belongs on one that already exists* — already reach a session in both new situations, because a session holding a pass's several defects is by construction about to create an issue. Editing it to name the batch shape would spend always-on characters, owe an outflow, and buy no triggering that the cell does not already get. The failure mode this trades against is under-triggering, so it is not free: if the experience session shows a session in the batch situation failing to fire the cell, the description changes and the outflow comes with it.

## Rejected

- **A fifth tie verb** (`filed-with`, `batched-with`), the remedy three filers reached for. Argued above; available on a sighting where a pair genuinely does not batch and the shared-probe fact changes a ranking, at which point the table's test is what it must pass.
- **A mandatory reproduction.** It is the stronger-sounding rule and it is wrong for this cell: wants and ideas are a large share of what this board holds, none of them has a failing run, and a floor they cannot meet either blocks them or teaches filers to manufacture a repro that demonstrates nothing.
- **#244's *shared surface* as the test**, or as a required conjunct. Falsified in both directions by the only case with a recorded outcome.
- **Editing the frontmatter description** to name the batch shape. Argued above.
- **Two PRs, one per issue.** The owner ruled one convergence on #219, and the change is its own first exhibit: two wants against one cell that one change fixes is exactly the shape the batch outcome names.
- **Restating the rule-quote case inside the new paragraph.** Two half-owners of one sentence kept in agreement by hand is the drift mechanism, not a safeguard; the new sentence points at the paragraph above it instead.
