# D-415: The tie set gains a cause, and the refresh and the landing are told to read it

**Status:** Accepted 2026-09-05 (PR #415)

## Context

[#409](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/409) and [#411](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/411), worked as one change because the owner ruled them together on 2026-09-05 — *"please consider 411 though as well. Should they go together or not?"*, put to him as together with the case against, and affirmed with the brief.

**What the why-chain reached at pickup.** #409 as filed was two symptoms at two stages: a cause does not outrank its symptoms at the refresh, and a fix for a cause closes one issue at landing while the rest stay open. Underneath both is one gap — **the relationship between issues had two vocabularies and no mapping between them.** Ties are written at filing, in `skills/filing/SKILL.md`, with a consequence stated as what *a ranking does*. Bundles are written at the refresh, in `docs/cells/board/SKILL.md`, whose test was *what one change would take*. Neither cell named the other, so a tie had no consequence and a bundle had no origin. #411 is what a session does when it disagrees with a tie nothing consumes; [#264](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/264) is the same gap seen at filing.

**Why now rather than with [D-410](D-410-2026-09-05-brief-is-for-the-systemic-cause.md).** That entry's decision 5 landed the producing half of the tie mechanism and named the consequence it accepted: *"between this merging and #409 landing, sessions write ties that nothing reads."* Its decision 4 parked the row-versus-`supersedes` question here explicitly. The owner's question on 2026-09-05 — *"do we have anything that checks the rest of the board when we've identified a root cause, to see how it impacts what remains?"* — is what moved the consuming half off the deferred list.

**What the tree held at the base.** At `9b622fc`: the tie table's four rows and their consequence column; `docs/cells/board/SKILL.md`'s bundle test, *"The test is what one change would take"*, and its reading rule, *"The answer is the first item that is not `In progress`, `In flight`, `Blocked` or `Deferred`, and not blank"*; `docs/cells/landing/SKILL.md`'s two-sentence *What the pull request owes*; and `tools/board.py` at `9b622fc` carrying `Blocked` in both `STATUSES` and `UNAVAILABLE`. `grep -n -iE "same-subject|supersed|tie" tools/board.py` at that revision returns nothing, which is #409's own evidence that the transport never read a tie.

## Decision

**1. The tie set gains `causes` / `caused-by` rather than `supersedes` carrying the relationship.** #409 left this to the filing cell's own admission test — *"A tie name earns its place by changing what a ranking does with the pair"* — and the row is defined against the four existing consequence columns rather than against a judgement about which reads better. `supersedes` asserts a replacement that has **already happened** and asks a ranking to close or rescope the replaced issue; a cause has not replaced its symptoms, some survive its fix, and which ones is unknown until the fix lands. `same-subject` says *consider one PR for both*, which is a suggestion and withholds nothing. `same-class` says one remedy may serve both. `blocks` orders a pair and says in terms *do not bundle it*. **None of the four produces a group with a head**, which is exactly what the owner's option A asks a ranking to produce, so the set was closed against the relationship the practice most needs it to express.

**2. The board's shape is the owner's, from three argued options, and it is A.** One bundle led by the cause, symptoms under it and not picked up alone while the cause is open unless he rules the exception. Rejected by him: the cause ranked above with symptoms still individually rankable, and symptoms leaving the board while the cause is open. His word: *"A"*.

**3. `Blocked` executes that ruling, and no code was written.** He put the tool-or-by-hand question to the session. The reading rule cannot see `Bundle` or position, so ranking alone leaves `next` free to hand out a symptom the moment the cause goes `In progress` — a status is the only thing that stops it, and `Blocked` is already a value the transport accepts and the reading rule already skips. A tool-side check would have had to parse tie prose out of issue bodies to tell a cause from a symptom, which is a mechanism the rule does not need in order to work. **The refresh reading a pre-verb prose candidate tie the same way is part of this**: [#404](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/404) carries fourteen symptoms in the prose route D-410's decision 4 used, and a rule that only saw the verb would have missed the one live instance on the day it landed. That is what *the refresh being judgment rather than a parser* buys, and it is why nothing here edits #404 or #405.

**4. The batch test's referent is a cause the filer observed, which is what settles #264's contradiction.** *What one change would fix* is a counterfactual about a fix that does not exist; the same cell assigns remedy design to pickup and classes it as design that decays with today's vocabulary. **An observed cause is evidence and re-runnable, which is the standard that cell already sets for everything else a filing carries.** The line between the two filing routes falls out of it: where the defects are in your hands at once under one observed cause, one number; where the symptoms already carry numbers, the cause takes its own and the ties group them. **Three of #264's four faces are settled by that sentence** — the referent, the surface exhibit, and the consumer driven across the evidence/design line. Its fourth is untouched: a filer who declines to batch still needs a tie, and `same-subject`'s consequence column still reads *consider one PR for both*. The affirmed brief has that issue's pickup confirm the settlement rather than this change claiming it, so #264 stays open.

**5. #151 and #152 are re-framed rather than kept or deleted, and the re-framing is forced by decision 4 rather than an extra.** Their own bodies give the surface as the warrant — *"filed here as one batch so the cell is opened once"* on #151, and #152 the same for a different cell. Left cited as *the shape a batch takes* beside a test that rejects the surface, they would have taught the reading the new sentence exists to remove, in the same bullet that disclaims it. What they do illustrate is the form: per-defect evidence, shared probes once, per-item disposition at pickup. #94 and #95 move ahead of them because under the new test they are the exhibit that carries it — two filings against one sentence is one observed cause, and a sentence is a thing anyone can point at. **The `#233`/`#234`/`#235` paragraph stays**, its own words being the new test satisfied rather than breached: *"#233 and #234 share one cause, line-ending handling every guard passes."*

**6. The landing walk is this repository's, not the practice's, and the consequence is stated rather than hidden.** The brief priced four cells. The only shipped cell near the job of a pull request body is `persist-changes`, whose subject is committing and pushing; putting the walk there would have been a fifth cell and a wider change than was affirmed. **So an adopter receives the tie and its generic ranking consequence and no landing rule** — their ranking consumes the tie, their landing does not. That asymmetry is the accepted cost, and it is recorded here because a later session finding it will otherwise read it as an oversight.

**7. The closing-reference form is stated on the landing cell, and [#276](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/276) stays open.** This change multiplies the failure that issue records: a body that named one issue now names a group, so a silent parse failure costs a whole group rather than one issue. #276's deferred list leaves *where the form is stated* open and its own reasoning leans against the always-on surface; this puts it on the surface the writer already has open. **It closes nothing of #276**, whose want is a check that catches the failure, not a sentence stating the form.

**8. The third disposition sits in the systemic-cause paragraph, and the withholding is narrow.** #411's consumer took the owner's-exception clause because it was the only clause naming a disagreement at all — *"there is no slot for 'the asserted parentage is wrong'"* — and that clause funds the instance rather than re-parenting it. The added sentence gives the missing route. Its second half answers that issue's other complaint, the rule as landed having a session write a tie it believes false before the owner has ruled: **only the re-parenting of symptoms whose parentage is at that moment disputed is withheld**, the tying obligation being unchanged everywhere else. That leaves every symptom with one parent on the board, which is what lets decision 3's rule stay a single sentence rather than growing a multi-parent case.

**9. The bundle exclusion was narrowed because this change would otherwise have broken it.** `docs/cells/board/SKILL.md` said *"issues that merely came out of the same review, or name the same issue in their ties, are related and not grouped"* — read after decision 1, that excludes the very group option A asks for, every symptom of one cause naming it. The clause narrows to the verbs it was written about, `same-subject` and `same-class`. **This is a meaning change to a sentence whose other half is unmoved**, named here because that is where amendments are recorded.

## What this change cost, and what it did not touch

**Net growth, argued because `skills/authoring/SKILL.md` requires it.** `python tools/lint.py` on this tree: `engagement` 27,655 → 28,145, `board` 11,557 → 12,623, `filing` 10,527 → 11,255, `landing` 2,554 → 3,188. All four report *no body budget*, so no ceiling is in play and no admission row is owed; the always-on row is unmoved, no description having changed and no outflow therefore being owed. Nothing was deleted. **The one deletion available was the `#151`/`#152` citation**, and decision 5 records why re-framing it cost less than removing an exhibit that still teaches the form. The four existing tie rows were examined and kept: the new row is defined against them, and a reader needs them in front of them to see why it is not `supersedes`.

**The artifact carried three loose statements the cold seat observed and this change did not repair in it**, the body being the text its sha256 names: a locator putting `UNAVAILABLE` at `tools/board.py:74` where it is at `:77`; *"four files, one edit each"* where there are six edits across four files; and *"the repo-only cells, which may name `filing` and already do"*, true of the board cell and not of the landing cell. All three are carried in the settlement block on that comment. The first is the only one false about the tree, and the fact it was cited for — that `Blocked` needs no code — is decision 3 and was verified independently by the seat.

**The cold-seat dispatch departed from `skills/engagement/references/cold-seat.md` at `9b622fc` on one point.** That file has the artifact travel inline because it is committed and posted nowhere, so a path may name something the seat cannot read and a later reader cannot pin. The dispatch pinned it by absolute path outside the repository plus sha256 and byte count, and required the seat to compute both and refuse on any difference, which it did and reported. Inlining would have meant retyping 24,471 bytes into a prompt, and a seat cannot verify an inlined copy against anything. **The rule's reason was met by a stronger carrier; its letter was not**, and whether the rule should say so is not settled here.

## Rejected

**`supersedes` carrying the cause relationship.** Decision 1. It was the cheapest answer and the one #409 named first; rejected because it asserts a replacement that has already happened, where a cause's symptoms may survive its fix and which ones is unknown until it lands.

**The cause ranked above with symptoms still individually rankable**, and **symptoms leaving the board while the cause is open.** Decision 2. Both were put to the owner argued and he took neither.

**A check in `tools/board.py`.** Decision 3. Rejected because it would have to parse tie prose out of issue bodies to tell a cause from a symptom, and the ruled behaviour is executable at this revision with no code.

**Deleting the `#151`/`#152` citation.** Decision 5. It was the available offset for the growth; rejected because those issues still teach the form a batch takes, which nothing else in the cell shows.

**Putting the landing walk in `persist-changes` so an adopter gets it too.** Decision 6. Rejected as a fifth cell beyond the four the affirmed brief priced.

**Editing #404's and #405's tie blocks into the new verb.** Decision 3. Rejected because the refresh reads the prose candidate tie the same way, so nothing depends on the conversion, and because the brief puts re-judging any cause already claimed out of scope.
