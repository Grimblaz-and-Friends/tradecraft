# D-193: The review cell splits at the reader, its body gets a guard, and the cell's total is reported beside it

**Status:** Accepted 2026-08-25 (PR #193)

## Context

`adversarial-review` was 24,155 characters of body at the merge base — 30.4% of all cell prose and 2.34× the next largest, with no `references/` directory and eleven `##` sections. [#54](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/54) had already fixed this cell's size once, by rewrite, and closed on *"the skill went from ~53KB to ~15KB under the purpose-anchored charter."* It regrew 63% from the reset. That a remedy was tried and did not hold is the fact this change had to answer for, and it is why the remedy here is structural.

[#130](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/130) landed the standard that makes it actionable and named this cell as its first candidate, bounding the split out of its own change: *"What every firing needs stays in `SKILL.md`; what one trigger among several needs sits in `references/`… a cell is too big when a session loads prose it had no use for, which a small cell can do and a large one need not."* A disqualifier rather than a cap, and this cell failed it for every reader but one.

## Decision

**The seam is cut at the reader, not at the topic.** The cell's readers are not one population: a dispatching session drives the whole pipeline, while each prosecution seat, the defense, the judge, a session deciding only review depth, and a session deciding only whether another pass runs each use one stage's machinery. On a panel cycle the dispatching session is **one load of seven or more**. So what stays in `SKILL.md` is what binds every role — the purpose header and pause discipline, the charter, the lane choice, the evidence standards, lens promotion — and each stage's machinery goes one hop behind a pointer carrying its load condition: `roster.md`, `dispatch.md`, `arbitration.md`, `after-the-fix.md`, `the-record.md`. Body **24,155 → 8,736**.

**The cell got bigger and the change says so.** Total prose is **26,576** — body 8,736 plus 17,840 of depth — against 24,155, a governing-prose delta of **+2,421** against the merge base `81fb1d9`. What shrank is what any one reader loads; the total grew by five file titles, five load conditions and the pipeline map. A body-only figure would have certified this change as a 64% reduction. It is stated here because the same arithmetic is what the next session shedding depth will be tempted to publish.

**Nothing was deleted, and the affirmed remedy admitted deletions.** None survived the carry-the-reasons rule. The nearest candidate was `Staffing`'s *"the report records which model and runtime staffed each seat"* against `the-record.md`'s spelling convention for that value — a reason and a form, not two copies of one rule. Recorded because a later audit reaching the same pair should not have to re-derive the refusal.

**Two judgement calls were the session's, and neither is maximal shedding.** `Choosing the shape` stays in the body although a prosecution seat has no use for it: it is one of the cell's three declared triggers, and a session firing for *deciding review depth* alone should get its answer without a second hop. The disqualifier is a test about waste, not a rule to run to its limit. And merge, defense and the terminal ruling stay one file, because on the routine lane the defense **is** the terminal stage, so the two must co-load there, while the terminal stage reads what the merge recorded and the defense's price feeds its second clause — three files would be three hops for one chain.

**Meaning changes: four pointers, and nothing else.** Conservation was checked mechanically rather than asserted: of the original body's non-empty lines, six are absent verbatim afterwards — three `##` headings promoted to their file's `#` title, and three lines carrying four cross-references that read *"below"* or *"under *Ending the review*"* and now name the file they mean. Word-level diffs of those three lines show the substitution and no other edit.

**The split created the tree's first sibling pointers between depth files, and the guard could not see them.** From inside `references/` the bare form resolves a directory too deep, into a `references` folder inside `references`, so depth citing sibling depth has only the relative form — and `_token_before` reads its `../` prefix as more path, skipping the whole thing as somebody else's tree. A probe renaming a pointer's target left lint and the suite green. `check_cell_references` now resolves relative `.md` pointers landing inside the naming file's **own cell**. The bound is deliberate: a relative reference out of a cell is the zone wall's or the sideways rule's, unlawful whether or not it resolves, and an existence check over the same text prices one defect as two — the existing zone-wall test went red on the first version and is what set the bound.

**The first version of that guard was wrong and its own negative arm caught it.** It filtered `RELATIVE_REF` matches on a `.md` suffix; that matcher's trailing character class swallows the full stop ending a sentence, so a pointer written at the end of one answered *not markdown* and passed — which is the form a pointer actually takes in prose. `RELATIVE_MD_REF` anchors at `.md` instead, sharing `RELATIVE_REF`'s head so the two agree on where a relative reference starts. Both arms are pinned, the trailing-stop case among them.

**A body budget follows, at 9,000.** [D-184](D-184-2026-08-25-outflow-and-the-always-on-total.md) left this cell unbudgeted on the ground that a number for a cell nobody has argued about is a ruling arriving as a constant, and routed the decision to #177; the owner ruled a budget follows the split. The value is the landed 8,736 plus 264, about one bullet, and the margin is argued in the constant's own comment so a session raising it reads the argument where it is changing it: at zero headroom every reword of the body is a constant change, which turns the cap into noise nobody reads, while a section-sized regrowth cannot fit under it. **It is a ceiling above a measured body, not the measured body** — `authoring`'s cap reads like *no larger than you started* and is not that either, and this one should not be read as it. What the guard holds is the split's whole claim over a rewrite: #54's rewrite regrew because nothing failed when it did.

**A body cap is dodgeable one directory down, so the cell's total is reported beside it.** `figure_cell_total` is emitted whenever `--cell` is given, and is **deliberately unbudgeted** — a ceiling on the total would cap depth-shedding itself, which is the move the standard wants. What it buys is that the dodge is visible to whoever takes it. It lives in `tools/figures.py` rather than the engine because it exists as a consequence of a repo-only guard capping a cell's body, which is the same ground `figure_cell_description` sits on.

**The description is untouched**, so no outflow is owed: the always-on surface is unchanged by this change at 15,753 here and 9,747 for an adopter, and moving prose into `references/` does not reach it.

## Rejected

- **A second rewrite** (fork 1, owner-ruled). It is the remedy already tried, on a cell encoding a dozen decisions, and would have been the largest blast radius available. Split now, rewrite later as its own change was also offered and not taken.
- **Graduating the external pass to its own cell** (fork 2, owner-ruled). It was the one real candidate — reconciling automated reviewer comments has an independent trigger under #130's test, and this repository's flow lists it as its own step — and it stays a `references/` file because a new cell spends always-on surface in every session forever, against a total #184 had just pulled down.
- **A ceiling on the cell total** (fork 3's third option). It closes the body cap's dodge and caps depth-shedding with it. The total is reported instead.
- **No budget at all** (fork 3's second option). It leaves the split's claim — that a pointer makes regrowth visible where prose does not — resting on the same thing that failed #54: somebody noticing.
- **Moving `Choosing the shape` out of the body.** Maximal shedding by the letter of the disqualifier; it costs a whole declared trigger a second hop.
- **Splitting merge, defense and the terminal stage into three files by role.** Three readers, but one chain: the routine lane co-loads two of them by construction.
- **Widening the pointer guard to relative references leaving the cell.** They are already findings under two other guards, and reporting one defect twice is the cost.
