# D-193: The review cell splits at the reader, its body gets a guard, and the cell's total is reported beside it

**Status:** Accepted 2026-08-25 (PR #193)

## Context

`adversarial-review` was 24,155 characters of body at the merge base — 30.92% of all cell prose and 2.400× the next largest (`engagement`, 10,066), with no `references/` directory and eleven `##` sections. Those three figures are one derivation at one tree: an earlier draft carried 30.4% and 2.34×, which are [D-184](D-184-2026-08-25-outflow-and-the-always-on-total.md)'s, correct at `9018f56` where the body was 23,541 and re-labelled here onto a larger one. [#54](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/54) had already fixed this cell's size once, by rewrite, and closed on *"the skill went from ~53KB to ~15KB under the purpose-anchored charter."* It regrew **76.0%** from the reset — 13,721 at `401669f` to 24,155. An earlier draft of this entry said 63%, which no reset-anchored measurement returns. That a remedy was tried and did not hold is the fact this change had to answer for, and it is why the remedy here is structural.

[#130](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/130) landed the standard that makes it actionable and named this cell as its first candidate, bounding the split out of its own change. The standard it landed, quoted from where it lives rather than from the issue — `skills/authoring/SKILL.md`, *Depth leaves the body*: *"What every firing needs stays in `SKILL.md`; what one trigger among several needs sits in `references/`… a cell is too big when a session loads prose it had no use for, which a small cell can do and a large one need not."* A disqualifier rather than a cap, and this cell failed it for every reader but one.

## Decision

**The seam is cut at the reader, not at the topic.** The cell's readers are not one population: a dispatching session drives the whole pipeline, while each prosecution seat, the defense, the judge, a session deciding only review depth, and a session deciding only whether another pass runs each use one stage's machinery. On a panel cycle the dispatching session is **one load of seven or more**. So what stays in `SKILL.md` is what binds every role — the purpose header and pause discipline, the charter, the lane choice, the evidence standards, lens promotion — and each stage's machinery goes one hop behind a pointer carrying its load condition: `roster.md`, `dispatch.md`, `arbitration.md`, `after-the-fix.md`, `the-record.md`. Body **24,155 → 8,738**.

**The cell got bigger and the change says so.** Total prose is **27,965** — body 8,738 plus 19,227 of depth — against 24,155, a governing-prose delta of **+3,810** against the merge base `da2e11b` — `81fb1d9` was the base when this change was designed, and `origin/main` advanced under it mid-flight. What shrank is what any one reader loads; the total grew by five file titles, five load conditions and the pipeline map. A body-only figure would have certified this change as a 64% reduction. It is stated here because the same arithmetic is what the next session shedding depth will be tempted to publish.

**Nothing was deleted, and the affirmed remedy admitted deletions.** None survived the carry-the-reasons rule. The nearest candidate was `Staffing`'s *"the report records which model and runtime staffed each seat"* against `the-record.md`'s spelling convention for that value — a reason and a form, not two copies of one rule. Recorded because a later audit reaching the same pair should not have to re-derive the refusal.

**Two judgement calls were the session's, and neither is maximal shedding.** `Choosing the shape` stays in the body although a prosecution seat has no use for it: it is one of the cell's three declared triggers, and a session firing for *deciding review depth* alone should get its answer without a second hop. The disqualifier is a test about waste, not a rule to run to its limit. **The third trigger — deciding whether another pass runs — was not given the same treatment**, and the asymmetry is deliberate: its answer is the next-pass ruling, which is unreadable without the report contents it sits beside, so a body copy would buy half an answer where the hop buys all of it. And merge, defense and the terminal ruling stay one file, because on the routine lane the defense **is** the terminal stage, so the two must co-load there, while the terminal stage reads what the merge recorded and the defense's price feeds its second clause — three files would be three hops for one chain.

**Meaning changes in the prose that moved: four pointers, and nothing else — and the check that establishes it runs one direction only.** It compares base to head and so answers what *left*; it cannot see what the change *added*, and this change added five load conditions, two authored titles and the pipeline map. Its review found several of those additions false about the readers they excuse, and they are this change's real meaning changes. The scope stated: conservation was checked mechanically rather than asserted, base to head: of the original body's non-empty lines, six are absent verbatim afterwards — three `##` headings promoted to their file's `#` title, and three lines carrying four cross-references that read *"below"* or *"under *Ending the review*"* and now name the file they mean. Word-level diffs of those three lines show the substitution and no other edit.

**The split created the tree's first sibling pointers between depth files, and the guard could not see them.** From inside `references/` the bare form resolves a directory too deep, into a `references` folder inside `references`, so depth citing sibling depth reaches for a relative one — and `_token_before` reads its `../` prefix as more path, skipping the whole thing as somebody else's tree. **Three forms in fact resolve from there and the guard catches two**: the `../` form fires and so does a same-directory form written with a leading dot-slash; a **bare filename** does not. That hole is stated rather than closed — a backticked bare filename cannot be told from a mention of a file elsewhere, and this cell's prose discusses filenames more than any other in the tree. A probe renaming a pointer's target, **and updating the one bare pointer in `SKILL.md` that the old guard could see**, left lint and the suite green; renaming alone leaves the old lint red there, so the second step is what the probe turns on. `check_cell_references` now resolves relative `.md` pointers landing inside the naming file's **own cell**. The bound is deliberate: a relative reference out of a cell is the zone wall's or the sideways rule's, unlawful whether or not it resolves, and an existence check over the same text prices one defect as two — the existing zone-wall test went red on the first version and is what set the bound. **That argument reaches the out-of-cell arm and not the out-of-repo one**: `check_sideways_deps` does catch a target in another cell, and nothing catches one outside the repository at all, so that arm is knowingly silent rather than covered elsewhere.

**The first version of that guard was wrong and its own negative arm caught it.** It filtered `RELATIVE_REF` matches on a `.md` suffix; that matcher's trailing character class swallows the full stop ending a sentence, so a pointer written at the end of one answered *not markdown* and passed — which is the form a pointer actually takes in prose. `RELATIVE_MD_REF` anchors at `.md` instead, sharing `RELATIVE_REF`'s head so the two agree on where a relative reference starts. Both arms are pinned, the trailing-stop case among them.

**A body budget follows, at 9,000.** [D-184](D-184-2026-08-25-outflow-and-the-always-on-total.md) left this cell unbudgeted on the ground that a number for a cell nobody has argued about is a ruling arriving as a constant, and routed the decision to #177; the owner ruled a budget follows the split. The value is 9,000 — the body landed at 8,736 and is 8,738 after this change's own review, about one bullet under the cap — and the margin is argued in the constant's own comment so a session raising it reads the argument where it is changing it: at zero headroom every reword of the body is a constant change, which turns the cap into noise nobody reads, while a section-sized regrowth cannot fit under it. **It is a ceiling above a measured body, not the measured body** — `authoring`'s cap reads like *no larger than you started* and is not that either, and this one should not be read as it. What the guard holds is the split's whole claim over a rewrite: #54's rewrite regrew because nothing failed when it did.

**A body cap is dodgeable one directory down, so the cell's total is reported beside it.** `figure_cell_total` is emitted whenever `--cell` is given, and is **deliberately unbudgeted** — a ceiling on the total would cap depth-shedding itself, which is the move the standard wants. What it buys is that the dodge is visible to whoever takes it. It lives in `tools/figures.py` rather than the engine because it exists as a consequence of a repo-only guard capping a cell's body, which is the same ground `figure_cell_description` sits on.

**The description is untouched**, so no outflow is owed: this change moves the always-on surface by **zero** — 16,241 here and 10,243 for an adopter at both its merge base `87d1d65` and the tree it lands in — and moving prose into `references/` does not reach it. The invariant is the claim; the absolute moves whenever any cell is added, as #192 did while this change was open.

## Rejected

- **A second rewrite** (fork 1, owner-ruled). It is the remedy already tried, on a cell encoding a dozen decisions, and would have been the largest blast radius available. Split now, rewrite later as its own change was also offered and not taken.
- **Graduating the external pass to its own cell** (fork 2, owner-ruled). It was the one real candidate — reconciling automated reviewer comments has an independent trigger under #130's test, and this repository's flow lists it as its own step — and it stays a `references/` file because a new cell spends always-on surface in every session forever, against a total #184 had just pulled down.
- **A ceiling on the cell total** (fork 3's third option). It closes the body cap's dodge and caps depth-shedding with it. The total is reported instead.
- **No budget at all** (fork 3's second option). It leaves the split's claim — that a pointer makes regrowth visible where prose does not — resting on the same thing that failed #54: somebody noticing.
- **Moving `Choosing the shape` out of the body.** Maximal shedding by the letter of the disqualifier; it costs a whole declared trigger a second hop.
- **Splitting merge, defense and the terminal stage into three files by role.** Three readers, but one chain: the routine lane co-loads two of them by construction.
- **Widening the pointer guard to relative references leaving the cell.** They are already findings under two other guards, and reporting one defect twice is the cost.
