# D-225: The values ranking is adopted — one home outside the north star, a doctrine line that binds, and recognition for items at rest

**Status:** Accepted 2026-08-29 (PR #225)

## Context

`docs/north-star/values.md` at `4c4805c` declared a ranking of what this practice values — trustworthiness, compounding, owner attention, evidence, craft — and bound nothing. It landed with `flow.md` in [#214](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/214) as reference, explicitly non-binding until adopted through normal ceremony, and [#215](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/215) is that adoption.

The tilt the ranking exists to resist is stated in the document itself: economic machinery sharpens cost estimates for all work but value estimates only for *measurable* value, so prevention, compounding work and craft lose every tie, one locally rational decision at a time. [#70](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/70)'s governance-ratio measurement is the local sighting — work individually well-executed and collectively not worth its share, with nothing to argue the *worth* half against.

Four questions were open at pickup, three of them named on the issue as deliberately deferred and one arriving as a routed review finding. All four went to the owner as forks and all four were ruled.

## Decision

**The home is `docs/values.md`, promoted out of `docs/north-star/`.** That directory's declared job is to hold reference that binds nothing, and a binding rule inside it invites a future reader to discount it. A status line inside the file is the weaker signal — it is missed exactly once and then the rule is dead — where the directory name is read every time the path is. `flow.md`'s pointer to *"`values.md`, beside this file"* is repaired in the same diff, under mover-pays.

**The doctrine line binds rather than points.** `AGENTS.md` gains a `## Worth doing` section sending a worth-it judgment whose evidence has run out to argue against the ranking, by number. A line that only named the location would have reproduced the state this change exists to end — a ranking that exists and binds nothing — while still spending always-on characters. It is admitted at the admission order's last-resort tier on the ground that a ranking function is exactly the *must bind before any context loads* class that tier is reserved for: a session deciding whether work is worth doing has, at that moment, loaded nothing. **Per-cell copies were never available**: cells are self-contained and may not reference `docs/`, so the shape was one home plus one always-on line or nothing.

**The both-ways guard gains its recognition half, in the values document.** The guard's existing sentence — *a claim names the value it serves, or it isn't one* — reaches a **claim**, which carries its value because somebody is arguing worth as it is read. An **open item** is read at rest, by a ranking pass or a staleness sweep or any evidence-shaped filter, with nobody present to argue it; a values-funded item has no measurable payoff by construction, so it reads as weak and the guard never fires. That is the document's own tilt operating on the board rather than in an argument. An item whose case for pickup rests on unmeasurable value now names that value where its case is made.

**Absence of a name means untagged, never *measurable*, and that clause is load-bearing rather than decorative.** A tag whose absence carries meaning must be applied reliably; applied patchily — and it will be, since it depends on each filer classifying their own rationale — a missing tag reads as a measurability claim, and the sweep culls precisely the items the guard protects. The guard fires backwards. Stating that an untagged item gets what it would have got anyway is what keeps a partial mechanism honest instead of harmful.

**The ranking is adopted as written**, five values in their stated order. Adoption is the cheapest moment to amend it and the owner declined to; amendment stays his alone and now costs a ceremony.

## The outflow: two moves and one refusal

Owed on any edit of an always-on surface, at the budget or nowhere near it, and never sized to what is added [D-184].

- **Moved: the flow's fix-batch clause.** *"A fix batch that rewrites what the material instructs buys one more session on the last tree the review's fixes produce, or the one line declining it, before the review closes."* `experience-session` carries that rule in its body in more detail, **and its description carries the trigger** — *"and again once a review's fix batch has rewritten what that material instructs, before the review closes"* — which is the surface that always loads and therefore the one D-184's own correction says the trigger has to be on. The doctrine was holding a duplicate, not a rule. Nothing left the always-on surface that was not already on it twice, which is why this is a move and not the artifact-to-artifact shuffle D-184 warns reads as a reduction.
- **Compressed: the Release line's CODEOWNERS contrast.** *"— not `CODEOWNERS`, which cannot reach a PR's own author, today always him"* goes to the `[D-81]` already cited beside it, whose title is that contrast and whose body carries both the mechanism and the single-author caveat. What survives is what a reader needs in order to know the rule exists: CI labels and comments on a PR touching these files.
- **Refused: the CRLF clause under Structure and substrate**, despite `[D-186]` sitting on it and the reason looking like a textbook compression. **D-186 §5 records that placement as the deliberate choice** — *"It sits in `AGENTS.md` rather than in a cell or in this entry because the moment it must be found is mid-task, when a session notices CRLF and has no reason to open the decision log"* — and separates the recorded fact from the rule on exactly that ground: the rule stops harnesses from lying, the fact stops sessions from investigating. The outflow's first move is to read the citation, and here the citation said don't. This is the same shape as D-184's refusal on the zone wall's consumer-cache clause, and it is recorded so a later audit reaching the same passage does not re-derive it.

## Rejected

- **Putting the recognition obligation in the `filing` cell** — that an issue whose pickup rests on unmeasurable value must name that value by number. It is the stronger mechanism and it was declined for four reasons, the last decisive. (a) The absence-as-false-signal failure above, which a mandatory-but-patchy tag creates and a document-stated one does not. (b) It binds new filings only; issues here are not retro-maintained, so every currently-open item stays untagged permanently and the mechanism protects the board as it turns over rather than the board that exists. (c) The charter admits an agent-proposed rule on an incident from real work or the owner's specific approval, and states that **a review finding about governing prose is not an incident** — which is exactly what [#221](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/pull/221)'s cold read is. (d) `filing` is shipped, and the zone wall forbids a cell referencing `docs/`; it could state *name the value by number* but never say where the numbers resolve. Here the `AGENTS.md` line supplies that referent — but an adopter never loads `AGENTS.md`, and the charter export is deferred out of this change, so they would receive an instruction to cite a numbered value against no ranking at all. **It stays available on an actual sighting**, at which point the values document's sentence is there to promote and the charter-export question will likely have been settled too.
- **Deferring recognition entirely and re-filing it.** #221's finding was routed to this pickup by name, so deferring re-files a routed finding and the next pickup re-derives the same three options; and it would adopt the guard as binding in the same change that knowingly left it unfireable on the board.
- **A shipped `skills/values/` cell as the home.** A cell's name and description are always-on and cost more surface than the pointer line does, and it would ship this repository's ranking to adopters — the thing the deferred charter-export question exists to get right first.
- **Carrying the ranking in `AGENTS.md` itself.** Five values with their rationale is a large multiple of what a pointer costs, against a ceiling whose headroom at the merge base was small.
- **Leaving the file at `docs/north-star/values.md` at `4c4805c` and amending only its status line.** Cheapest diff and no cross-reference repair, but it leaves one binding document and one non-binding one in a directory named for the second, legible only for as long as readers read labels rather than paths.
- **Exporting the mechanism to the charter** — *judgments cite the repository's declared values* — for adopting repositories. Thin headroom, and speculative before this repository has run the mechanism even once. Left open on #215's terms, revisitable on a sighting.
- **Allocation-lane mechanics**, and **the worth-it check itself**. The first needs a board that can hold lanes, which is [#83](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/83)'s; the second is [#118](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/118)'s, and this change exists to give its pickup something to judge against.
