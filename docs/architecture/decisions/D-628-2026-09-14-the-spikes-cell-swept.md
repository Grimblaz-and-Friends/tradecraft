## What was decided

The `spikes` cell keeps every concept it carried. What leaves is the enumerated case a session could decide from the concept, the armour clause, the second statement of something already said, and the field list a session copies at the moment of writing. Forty-six passages were classed sentence by sentence in the pre-implementation artifact before anything was built: **17 kept** — fifteen rows byte-for-byte and two gaining only a citation — **26 sharpened**, **1 compressed to a decision entry's citation**, **1 cut unwritten**, and **1 row of new depth-index entries** for the two templates.

**Why this cell's cut runs along one seam, and the builder had to know it.** This cell's instrument rules are here *because of* an incident rather than despite one. [D-337] records that D-320's fence run wrote four rules only into its entry — *"a decision entry informs and never binds, so a session opening `skills/spikes/SKILL.md` to run one found none of them"* — and closed [#325](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/325) by moving them into the cell. ## The deletion licence governs less than it did, and the file now says so

`skills/spikes/references/licensing-a-deletion.md` is the instrument of the *evidence-gated* bar for deleting a rule — a per-sentence run, three seats an arm, no citation in either arm. **The brief reverses that bar**: *"today a rule stays unless there is evidence it does not bind; under this, a sentence that is not a concept, a check, a script's message or an incident goes, and the change that removes it says which it was."* `skills/authoring/references/routing.md` records that reversal against [D-261].

So after #571 this file governs **a deletion a run licenses, and a sweep's deletions are not that.** They are on the charter's form test, they name their class in the change that removes them — which is what the tables above are — and they never load this file, whose load condition is scoring a plant or a run whose output would be a removal. **No cut in this change was licensed by an A/B, and none was run.**

One wording followed from the narrowing and is the only thing added on its account. Line 9 opened *"Deletion requires a run whose arms differ…"*, which a reader arriving after #571 would take as the only door to a deletion; it now opens *"A run licenses a deletion where its arms differ…"*, the licence's actual subject. The cost asymmetry at line 11 stays as the reason the run's licence has the shape it has — it is not the practice's deletion bar any more, and nothing was added to say so, because the charter is always-on and the sweep's route never opens this file. **The dispatch that commissioned the artifact called this file *"what every sweep's deletions are measured against"*; that is contrary to the brief's own sentence and was not followed.**

## Two templates, flat, and the dispatch template is whole

**Item 3 of the reading — an output's fields go to one template per output and the prose keeps what the output is for — was taken as meaning two templates here.** This cell emits two things a session copies at the moment of writing, and each had its fields spread across prose in two places: the **spike report**, whose fields were in `skills/spikes/SKILL.md:37` *and* `skills/spikes/references/licensing-a-deletion.md:5,15,19,23`, so no session assembling one ever saw them together; and the **A/B seat's dispatch**, whose fields were at `skills/spikes/references/cold-seat-ab.md:12,14,16`.

**Both files sit directly in `skills/spikes/references/`, not in a subdirectory, and that is forced.** `tools/lint.py` defines `REFERENCES_REF` as `re.compile(r"(references[\\/][\w.-]+\.md)")`, whose filename class admits no slash, so a pointer into a subdirectory is invisible to `check_cell_references` while `check_depth_index`, which walks `rglob("*.md")`, would still demand an index entry — an orphan by construction. Each template therefore gets one index entry in the body, which `skills/authoring/references/cell-structure.md`'s third bullet requires and `check_depth_index` enforces.

**The dispatch template is this cell's whole and points at nothing for its bounds.** `skills/engagement/references/cold-seat.md:9` reads: *"This section is the whole of what every cold dispatch takes from this file. Every section below it is the artifact check's own procedure — what that dispatch carries, its bar, its verdicts, its cap — and an instrument dispatching a cold seat for anything else takes its dispatch contract from its own cell."* What `engagement` owns is the cold definition ([D-491]`:13`), which `cold-seat-ab.md:7` already points at. The bounds an A/B seat is given — the stop, the disposal rule, and that it dispatches nothing of its own — are this instrument's dispatch contract, stated once here, with the template carrying them as fields. Two instruments' contracts stating one bound is not a shared standard with two owners: each reaches only its own seat.

## The review put five reasons back, and that is the sweep's own lesson

The routine lane's two seats and its terminal stage bought twelve findings. **Six were shipped prose, and five of those were restorations of clauses this sweep had removed** — the record's scope and the substitution's *neither keeps nor stops* half at `skills/spikes/references/licensing-a-deletion.md:7`, the asymmetric-item reason at `skills/spikes/references/cold-seat-ab.md:9`, the plan-stop's premise at `:12`, and the comparability clause at `:16` that this entry had classed *to template* and that the template did not hold. The sixth removed the word `whole` from `cold-seat-ab.md:3`, the surviving half of a leak two experience-session consumers had already found in the template itself.

**Every one of the five took a concept's *reason* rather than a decided case.** [D-575]'s principle is that governing prose states the concept **and its reason**; the brief's form test licenses removing a sentence that is *"not a concept, a check, a script's message or an incident"*, and a reason is part of the concept. So none of the five was ever licensed by the form test, and putting them back corrects this sweep's compliance rather than deciding anything new — which is why the terminal stage ruled restoration outside the bound that keeps a fix batch from adding governing sentences on a finding alone. **The lesson for the sweeps that follow: under this principle the easiest thing to cut wrongly is the *because* clause**, and a row that keeps "the concept" while dropping the sentence that says why is the shape to look for.

One finding was lapsed on that bound rather than on its merits: `spike-report-template.md` nowhere says the copied block is the whole of the report, which is a real gap, and no prose states it, so the batch said so rather than added it.
