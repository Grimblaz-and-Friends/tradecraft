# D-366: A substitution measures two things and licenses adoption by neither, three seats an arm is a floor, and the plant may not be a second route to the behaviour under test

**Status:** Accepted 2026-09-04 (PR #366)

**Evidence.** The gaps as filed: [#358](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/358) and [#363](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/363), worked as one change. The brief settled in conversation and posted at [issuecomment-5535871364](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/358#issuecomment-5535871364); the owner's affirmation, ruling both forks, at [issuecomment-5535879978](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/358#issuecomment-5535879978); the artifact, settled `would` on its **second** cold seat, at [issuecomment-5536067066](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/358#issuecomment-5536067066), with a reading amendment at [issuecomment-5536091665](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/358#issuecomment-5536091665). The shape this completes was built by [D-352](D-352-2026-09-03-the-instrument-covers-a-substitution.md), which named a substitution as a third run shape and sent exactly these two questions to the board.

## The condition

D-352 shipped a substitution's **default** and neither a **threshold** nor a **width**, and its citation test counts only substance one wording carries and the other does not. Two seats on that change, designing against the shipped text, independently invented the missing two; its `operational` seat named its width choice as *"exactly the translation AC1 says a consumer should not have to make"* and its threshold as *"any nonzero split in the candidate's favour"*, naming two an arm and a majority rule as things another session could equally have picked. Separately, [PR #352](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/pull/352)'s second experience session, using the shipped cell, reported supplying a rule the cell does not carry — *"the plant must not be a second route to the behaviour the text under test produces"* — and flagged the invention as its own.

D-352 declined to invent a number and said why: *"Every available answer is a number with no evidence behind it, and this repository has twice refused a ruling arriving as a constant; it went to the board so its pickup can put the fork to the owner with data."*

## The data the forks were put on

The complete local corpus of this repository's cold-seat A/B runs, extracted rather than recalled: every run reachable from the repository's issue and pull request comments between 2026-08-16 and 2026-09-04, cross-checked against the Evidence sections of D-320, D-336, D-337, D-338, D-345, D-347, D-352 and D-353. **Twenty-two runs.** Two results are load-bearing and both are re-derivable.

**Three seats an arm is the ceiling of the whole record, per docket case, without exception.** [#255](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/255#issuecomment-5486527919) is the apparent counter-example and reconciles rather than contradicting: its *"n=6 per arm"* is two cases pooled at three per case per arm, seven per arm over its three cases, which matches its stated 35 runs across five arms. The number has a single origin at [#272](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/272#issuecomment-5500944347), where it is listed as a limit and never derived, and [the fence run](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/260#issuecomment-5503370780) inherited it saying so — *"This run widens the clause count but not the seat count."*

**No disposition this instrument has ever reached turned on the size of a gap.** Every one turned on whether any seat cited. The fence run kept clauses at 1/3 vs 0/3 three times (B3, C3, E1) and kept C1 at 3/3 vs 3/3.

**That second result is what made fork 1 real, and it is this entry's main contribution to a later session.** Presence-of-a-citation is not a neutral test; it is calibrated by the deletion's cost asymmetry, *keeping is cheap*, which is why one citation keeps. Adoption reverses that asymmetry — the default is the incumbent, so adopting is the change — and carrying the presence test across would license switching a shipped rule on one seat out of three. **So retaining the exclusivity citation test does not avoid the threshold question; it hides it.** Every route needs a bar except the route that licenses nothing. A session re-deriving this ruling from the shipped text alone will not find that argument there, which is why it is here.

## The decision

**Fork 1, ruled by the owner: the run measures two things and licenses adoption by neither.** A substitution is scored on substance one wording carries and the other does not, **and** on reliance on substance both carry, attributed to the arm the seat sat in and read as a rate across arms. Both are scored and both reported. **The instrument sets no adoption bar**: the run reports both measures, the plant's seat-by-seat overlap and what its null bounds, and the call to adopt or leave the current wording standing is the proposer's, argued on that report.

Rejected: **an exclusivity-only test with a shipped adoption bar**, because the number has nothing behind it and the record shows what presence tests produce; and **an exclusivity-only test that refuses to dispatch a clarity rewrite**, because the cell's own description names *"a candidate wording for a rule"* first and clarity rewrites are most of those, so that option converts a defect into a documented incapacity.

The second measure answers an argument an external reviewer made on PR #352 — that requiring candidate-exclusive substance makes a semantically equivalent rewrite unmeasurable by construction. That argument was right and could not be admitted there: the charter's bar does not let a review finding change what an instrument measures. It is admitted here by the owner's specific approval, which is the bar's other path [D-77].

**Fork 2, ruled by the owner: three seats an arm is a floor, and the shipped text says a floor is all it is.** A substitution strips nothing, so both arms keep everything around the sentence, its signal is weaker by construction, and a null at three here bounds less than a null at three does in an ablation. Rejected: **inheriting three silently**, as an overclaim; and **stating no width**, which would leave a null meaning nothing.

**The plant gains a subject constraint, at design time, for every run shape.** A plant must not be a second route to the behaviour the text under test produces — stated of the plant against this run's docket and this text, never of the plant alone, because the property is relational. It sits beside *built to the same near-miss shape as the real items* rather than replacing it: **shape is difficulty calibration and subject is what the plant licenses**, and the two are separable. This is #363's rule, admitted on the incident above.

**Scoring-side, that rule is stated of a substitution alone, and the reason is the whole of why this entry exists.** The first cold seat returned `would not` on a draft that stated it generally, and it was right. On a **confirm**, a redundant plant does not merely spoil the measurement — it manufactures the deletion licence. Seats reach the behaviour through the plant in both arms, so no seat cites the text in either; the void gate passes **because the plant was cited**; three seats an arm are present; and `skills/spikes/references/licensing-a-deletion.md`'s three conjuncts fire on text that was live. A remedy expressed in nulls cannot reach a rule expressed in citations. Any remedy that *would* reach it changes what a deletion run licenses, which the affirmed brief excludes in terms.

**So the residual is carried rather than closed, and named in the shipped text rather than left to inference.** The wording reads *"not because a confirm is immune, but because it is worse there"*. Its promoter: a confirm is scored whose plant independently licensed the behaviour, or a scorer asks what to do with one.

## What is unresolved, and goes to the review

**Scoping the redundant-plant reading to a substitution runs against D-352's own owner ruling on that same paragraph**, which chose *option A, stated generally* over *option B, scoping it to a substitution*, because *"the confound is not substitution-specific and scoping it would read as if an ablation were immune."* The worry transfers and the shipped sentence mitigates rather than resolves it. The session's recommendation, carried to the review and to release: **drop the scoring-side edit entirely**, leaving the design-time rule to close the harm where the incident actually arose, with the confirm-side residual carried as one finding across all shapes. Its scoring-side witness is fabricated material — see the retraction below — where the design-side rule has a real incident. It is left standing here because the artifact settled with it in, and the second cold seat, having weighed exactly this point, ruled it belonged in front of the review rather than in another convergence round.

- **The void gate's arms-agreed condition**, and the recorded finding that a substitution whose arms both cited their own candidates is voided by it. Only the confound sentences in that paragraph moved.
