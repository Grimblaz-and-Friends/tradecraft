## The data the forks were put on, and what its first statement got wrong

**Three seats an arm is the ceiling of every run of this instrument dispatched for a spike, per docket case.** The corpus also holds runs commissioned **inside a review**, and one of those exceeds it: [PR #232's defense](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/pull/232#issuecomment-5465499409) seated **six per docket case** in its intact arm — five of six seats loading the cell, ten of twelve task-instances over two docket cases. That is why the claim is stated of spike dispatches rather than of the whole record.

**[#255](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/255#issuecomment-5486527919) is not a counter-example and reconciles**: its *"n=6 per arm"* is two cases pooled at three per case per arm, seven per arm over its three cases, matching its stated 35 runs across five arms. The number is **stated as a limit and never derived** at [#272](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/272#issuecomment-5500944347), and [the fence run](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/260#issuecomment-5503271800) inherited it saying so — *"This run widens the clause count but not the seat count."* (That sentence is in the fence run's **artifact** comment, not its record comment, which states the same substance as *"n=3 per arm. Unchanged from #272."*)

**No keep-or-delete disposition of the deletion instrument has turned on the size of a gap.** Every one turned on whether any seat cited. The fence run kept clauses at 1/3 vs 0/3 three times (B3, C3, E1) and kept C1 at 3/3 vs 3/3. **The claim is narrowed to that instrument deliberately**: PR #232's review-commissioned run *did* dispose on gap size — *"1/6 against 0/4 is not separable from zero at these n. I record it as a reproduced instance, not a measured frequency."*

**This section's first draft asserted both claims unrestricted — *"the ceiling of the whole record, per docket case, without exception"* and *"no disposition this instrument has ever reached"* — and PR #232 falsifies both as written.** They are corrected here rather than adjusted, and this entry records the error because the corpus argument is what decided both owner forks and a later session must be able to see how far it actually reaches.

**Why the narrowed second claim still made fork 1 real, and it is this entry's main contribution to a later session.** Presence-of-a-citation is not a neutral test; it is calibrated by the deletion's cost asymmetry, *keeping is cheap*, which is why one citation keeps. Adoption reverses that asymmetry — the default is the incumbent, so adopting is the change — and carrying the presence test across would license switching a shipped rule on one seat out of three. **So retaining the exclusivity citation test does not avoid the threshold question; it hides it.** Every route needs a bar except the route that licenses nothing. A session re-deriving this ruling from the shipped text alone will not find that argument there.

## The decision

**Fork 1, ruled by the owner: the run measures two things and licenses adoption by neither.** A substitution is scored on substance one wording carries and the other does not, **and** on reliance on substance both carry, attributed to the arm the seat sat in and read as a rate across arms. Both are scored and both reported. **The instrument sets no adoption bar**: the run reports both measures, the plant's seat-by-seat overlap, whether the plant was a second route, and what its null bounds, and the call is the proposer's, argued on that report — **except where the two wordings would have a reader do different things, which is a genuine fork and reaches the owner argued.** That carve-out was not in the first draft; an external reviewer pointed out that assigning the call categorically to the proposer conflicts with the charter's authority rule, and it does.

Rejected: **an exclusivity-only test with a shipped adoption bar**, because the number has nothing behind it and the record shows what presence tests produce; and **an exclusivity-only test that refuses to dispatch a clarity rewrite**, because the cell's own description names *a candidate wording for a rule* among the four situations it fires on, and clarity rewrites are most of those, so that option converts a defect into a documented incapacity.

The second measure answers an argument an external reviewer made on PR #352 — that requiring candidate-exclusive substance makes a semantically equivalent rewrite unmeasurable by construction. That argument was right and could not be admitted there: the charter's bar does not let a review finding change what an instrument measures. It is admitted here by the owner's specific approval, which is the bar's other path [D-77].

**Fork 2, ruled by the owner: three seats an arm is a floor, and the shipped text says a floor is all it is.** A substitution strips nothing, so both arms keep everything around the sentence, its signal is weaker by construction, and a null at three here bounds less than a null at three does in an ablation. Rejected: **inheriting three silently**, as an overclaim; and **stating no width**, which would leave a null meaning nothing. **The shipped line claims no empirical superlative** — a first draft added *"and the widest this repository's record has seated"*, which is false without the per-docket-case unit it did not carry and unverifiable by an adopter, whose own record has seated nothing. The floor rests on the width the deletion licence already names.

**The plant gains a subject constraint, at design time, for every run shape.** A plant must not be a second route to the behaviour the text under test produces — stated of the plant against this run's docket and this text, never of the plant alone, because the property is relational. It sits beside *built to the same near-miss shape as the real items*: **shape is difficulty calibration and subject is what the plant licenses**, and the two are separable. This is #363's rule, admitted on the incident above.

**Reported call 3 is superseded by the build, and the merits are why.** The affirmation recorded, under *"Calls reported as the session's rather than asked"*, that the rule *"reaches a confirm and a substitution, never a screen"*, on the ground that a screen's plant rides with the ablated class so *"redundancy cannot mask there"*. **That ground is wrong.** A class-member plant that is a second route is cited in the intact arm and not in the ablated one, so the arms **separate** and the class reads as live when only the invented plant was — redundancy masks on a screen too, in the opposite polarity. The shipped constraint therefore binds every shape, and this entry supersedes the reported call rather than leaving the record to be reconciled by a later reader.

**Scoring-side, that rule is stated of a substitution alone.** On a **confirm**, a redundant plant does not merely spoil the measurement — it manufactures the deletion licence. Seats reach the behaviour through the plant in both arms, so no seat cites the text in either; the void gate passes **because the plant was cited**; three seats an arm are present; and `skills/spikes/references/licensing-a-deletion.md`'s three conjuncts fire on text that was live. A remedy expressed in nulls cannot reach a rule expressed in citations, and any remedy that *would* reach it changes what a deletion run licenses, which the affirmed brief excludes in terms. **The residual is carried rather than closed**, named in the shipped text as *"not because a confirm is immune, but because it is worse there"*. Its promoter: a confirm is scored whose plant independently licensed the behaviour, or a scorer asks what to do with one.

## What the review changed

Five seats, a defense and a judge, on a change of two shipped files. **The terminal ruling is at [issuecomment-5540864041](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/pull/366#issuecomment-5540864041)**, with the merged findings, the defense and the seat reports posted beside it.

**The review kept the scoring-side reading against the authoring session's own recommendation to drop it.** That session recommended dropping on the ground that the design-time rule carries the incident; the review found that a design-time argument does not answer a scoring-time harm, and that the clause is **the only place in shipped material** where any scorer learns a redundant plant manufactures a deletion licence — everything else saying so is repo-only prose the zone wall keeps from every adopter. The keep was made conditional on closing the check it depended on, and that condition is met: the reference's *“Neither measure licenses adopting the candidate”* paragraph (`:19` after this change's own renumbering) now obliges the run to report **whether the plant was a second route**, which nothing previously instructed anyone to establish.

**One high the review raised above the panel's own severity, and it was the change's most serious defect.** Making shared-substance reliance a scored citation moved what *cited* meant, and the first draft updated the seat-by-seat confound while leaving the void gate — the sentence immediately above it — reading the moved word. A substitution separating only on the second measure, with no plant citation in either arm, took the gate's *arms separated* escape and stood, where at base it was void twice over: **the instrument's only validity gate was silently escapable on precisely the case this change exists to enable.** The clause that licensed that reading is deleted and the gate's scope now ships once, in the reference — **in wording a confirm's or a screen's arms cannot satisfy**, since its antecedent is *“arms sharing all their substance”* and those shapes' arms differ by a removal. **Not by its siting**: that file is loaded whenever a plant is scored, on every shape, and this change's own recorded row says so.

**Two claims about this change's own record were retracted before landing**, both of the class this repository keeps meeting: a figure inherited rather than derived. The first draft said *seven* `docs/recorded-findings.jsonl` rows cite the reference path, carried from D-352 which measured it at `453b808`; `grep -c "skills/spikes/references/licensing-a-deletion\.md" docs/recorded-findings.jsonl` returns **11** at this change's merge base. It also said *four* decision-log entry sites cite the path, likewise inherited, in the same sentence that claimed the row count was derived; `git grep -l "skills/spikes/references/licensing-a-deletion" -- docs/architecture/decisions/` returns four files before this entry and **five** after, counting the index README as D-352's own convention does; unscoped it returns more, the path being cited outside the log too.

**And the review's own figure work disagreed with itself for a reason worth recording.** Two seats measured paragraph lengths in bytes and two in characters; the em-dashes this prose is full of are three bytes each, so the sets differed by ten to fourteen and the terminal ruling read one as failing to reproduce. **Both reproduced.
