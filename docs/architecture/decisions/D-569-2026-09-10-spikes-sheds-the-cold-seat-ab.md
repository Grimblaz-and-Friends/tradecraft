## What was decided

`skills/spikes/SKILL.md` at `686a3bc` was 17,224 characters of body against a ceiling of 17,136, with one depth file already beside it. **One section is 9,701 of that — 56% of the body.** `## Cold-seat A/B, for a premise about what a reader does` is the procedure for a spike whose executor is a reader: the six properties, the planted control and the three run shapes it rides differently in, the two questions the seats are asked, how a silence is read, and the two-arms rule. The cell's own trigger has two limbs, and `SKILL.md:23` names them — *reading a design settles neither what is in the material nor whether a mechanism fires*. A session arriving on either of those limbs loaded every character of that section and used none of it. It becomes `skills/spikes/references/cold-seat-ab.md`. The body is **8,358** characters, and `tools/lint.py`'s ceiling for it is rebaselined to that, as that map's own comment requires of a cell that sheds depth.

**Nothing is cut.** Of the non-blank lines below the frontmatter at `686a3bc`, every one appears verbatim in exactly one of the two files and none in both, with three exceptions: the section heading, which loses one `#` on becoming the new file's title; a clause that said *the disposal below* about a section that stays in the body; and the pointer at the moved section's end, which now names `skills/spikes/references/licensing-a-deletion.md` in the `../references/` form a depth file must use for a sibling — this cell's first sibling pointer and the one form `tools/lint.py`'s pointer check accepts.

## `## Exploring without a premise` stays in the body, and that is this change's one judgment call

The disqualifier in `skills/authoring/references/cell-structure.md` — *a cell is too big when a session loads prose it had no use for* — is what decides every paragraph here, and it is arguable against this one: a session that arrived with a named premise has no use for a section about having none. It stays, and the reasoning is recorded here so a later session finishing the split does not read it as a paragraph somebody missed.

- **Its trigger is the negative branch of the cell's own central test, which every firing runs.** A session reads `## When a spike fires`, finds its situation does not fire it, and this is what tells it what it may do instead — *that is yours to do, unprompted, and nothing here needs to authorise it* — and that the disposal rule still binds what it does. Prose answering the *no* arm of a test every reader takes is reached by the same read that decides the trigger, not by a second job.
- **The arithmetic runs the wrong way.** The section is 662 characters with the newline that would leave the body, 661 without. A title, a load condition, an index entry and a pointer sentence cost most of that back; this cell's other index entry is 288 characters and the one this change writes is 547.

**What would change the call:** the section growing to where an index entry is materially cheaper than it, or a second trigger's worth of unpremised-exploration material arriving beside it.

## This reverses D-337's placement, and the reversal is the part worth recording

[D-337](D-337-2026-09-03-the-instrument-rules-move-to-the-cell.md) is the entry that put these rules where this change finds them, and its `:17` states the cut it made: *"Rules every run of that kind needs sit in the cell body; the deletion conditions shed behind a pointer into `skills/spikes/references/licensing-a-deletion.md`, the cell's first `references/` file."* **Placement no longer encodes that cut.** Both classes now sit in `references/`, and what survives of D-337's distinction is the two load conditions — `cold-seat-ab.md` opens on designing, dispatching and reading a run, `licensing-a-deletion.md` on scoring a plant or licensing a deletion.

The cut itself is untouched; what changed is the premise under it. D-337 read *rules every run of that kind needs* as body material because the reader was taken to be a session running an A/B. The brief this change serves reads the population differently: the body's readers are every session the cell's two trigger limbs reach, and a run of that kind is one of them. Both readings are defensible and the second is the affirmed brief's. A session that wants D-337's shape back should know it is arguing against a brief rather than restoring an oversight.

**The change's review found this unrecorded and ordered it here.** The pull request argued the split without naming D-337 anywhere.

## What was rejected

**Splitting `## Exploring without a premise` out with the A/B** — the section above.

**Moving the A/B-specific clauses out of the spike-report duty at `SKILL.md:37`.** The report rule is a body-level obligation on every spike, and three of its clauses ask for things only an A/B produces: where each seat was stopped, what a planted control returned, and which disposition a scored run reached. The review measured the residue at roughly 305 characters once the clause about dispatched seats is excluded — [D-347](D-347-2026-09-03-whose-leaning-counts.md) put that one there deliberately, and the incident behind the recorded entry it answers was not an A/B. Each clause is conditionally gated on a run that carried the thing, so a session outside that scope reads past it. Weighed against a body at zero headroom, the edit was not worth its characters.

**Repointing the decision-log references this split strands.** [D-561](D-561-2026-09-10-records-sheds-three-sections.md) records the owner's ruling of 2026-09-10 that a split moving prose into a cell's own `references/` does not trigger the log's mover-pays repoint permission, and that the ruling **binds the programme's seven remaining splits**. This is one of them. Three file-level credits into this body now characterise it by content that left it — `D-314:39`, `D-491:40` and `D-337:17`, the last repaired here in substance rather than by repointing — and they stay as they are, at the cost that ruling accepted.

**Repairing the recorded findings this split strands.** `docs/recorded-findings.jsonl` keys 16 entries on `skills/spikes/SKILL.md`; **fourteen of them name material that is now in `cold-seat-ab.md`**, five of those by line number. That record carries no repair permission — nothing maintains it — so this is stated rather than repaired, and what keeps the entries findable is that a later review's dispatch matches an entry by the cell that holds its subject and not by the file. The widening from line-number rot to path rot is filed under [#412](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/412), which is the open cause.

**Giving the new depth file a ceiling of its own.** The lint prices a cell's `SKILL.md` and not its depth, so a cell can shed its way under a constant without shedding anything a reader loads. That asymmetry is [#457](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/457)'s subject, and resolving it here would decide the ceiling mechanism, which the affirmed brief puts out of scope.
