## The condition

[D-345] widened the routing-criterion bullet to cover text that keeps a reader *away* from a destination — its subject, its two quantities, its enumeration lead-in and route 1's predicate all moved. **The bolded sentence a criterion-writer applies as the test did not move with them,** and was byte-identical at `ed5a524` and at this change's base `453b808`:

> **Reach is falsifiable only where nothing else the reader loads routes that same situation to the same place, or where what does routes it too weakly to mask the difference.**

*The same place* is where the text under test sends the reader. For a **bare exclusion** — a clause that fences a reader off without naming where to go instead — there is no such place, so the phrase has no referent at all. For a fence that does name one, the question is destination-matched where the masking is not.

**[D-338] is the entry that records this gap as sustained and not fixed**, in its *What the review changed* section: *"The bullet is silent on routing away … which needs a design call and goes to the board."* [D-345] inherits that sentence and points at it; it does not itself record a ruling on the condition. **The ruling that made this a decision rather than a fix — that amending the clause re-opens a sentence D-338's judge recorded as having survived every attack unamended — was PR #345's, and lives on that thread.** It is not an in-repository record, and an earlier draft of this entry cited D-345 for it wrongly.

**The charter is what makes D-338's certification not an objection**: *"Decisions inform, never bind … it is never a citation against change, because if current behavior is wrong, the original reasoning probably was too."* D-338 pins its quotations to `skills/` at `70d59b7` and its certification claim is historical, so neither entry is falsified by this repair.

## The decision

**One replacement, in the condition and nowhere else.**

> **Reach is falsifiable only where nothing else the reader loads routes that same situation anyway, or where what does routes it too weakly to mask the difference.**

**The confound is independence, not direction, and that is the whole of the change's thesis.** An ablation separates unless something else decides the outcome **without the text under test**. Which way that something else pushes is irrelevant: anything resolving the situation *away from* the destination leaves both arms declining it, and anything pulling hard enough *toward* the destination leaves both arms opening it. Both mask. So the condition is one question rather than two facing opposite ways, and stating it as one is what makes it read in either polarity.

**`anyway` is the independence operator, and that is why it and not another word.** It says *regardless of the text under test*, and in this position — inside the restrictor of an *only where* clause, with *nothing **else** the reader loads* supplying the contrast two words upstream — it carries no other reading. That is a survey rather than an ear: every occurrence of the word in this repository is the independence or concessive operator, none the discourse particle *besides* and none a manner reading, which is the same instrument applied to `on its own` in the paragraph below and run symmetrically. It needs no destination, so the bare exclusion is repaired in the same stroke. And the verb `routes` is **kept**, which is load-bearing in two ways this entry's first draft got wrong and a review corrected — see below.

**Route 3 stays outside the condition, exactly as before.** The reader's own indifference is not something the reader *loads*, so no condition phrased over what the reader loads reaches it. [#348](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/348)'s first limb argued this was a defect; PR #345's defense had already disproved the framing, and this change neither relies on that limb nor alters the position. **No wording of this condition repairs or breaks route 3**, and a claim that any of them does is a category error.

## The verb is what the review turned on

**The first draft of this change shipped `settles that same situation on its own`.** It was measured at −5 characters, it passed every acceptance criterion, and it was wrong in two ways that a five-seat panel, a defense and a judge each verified on the tree without a single behaviour claim.

**`settles` is a completion predicate and it guts the second limb.** *"What does settle that same situation on its own"* that *"settles it too weakly to mask the difference"* describes an empty set: if the outcome is settled without the text under test, both arms produce it and the difference **is** masked. `routes` is gradable, so *"routes it too weakly"* draws a real line between a competing route strong enough to mask and a nudge that is not. **That limb is not decoration.** [D-338] records it as bought from an external reviewer on [D-210]'s measurement — a route that does not saturate still leaves an ablation able to separate — and it is what took that bullet from a net reduction to a net growth. The `settles` draft retired it silently while banking its own saving.

**`on its own` teaches sufficiency, and the gloss that would fix it ships nowhere.** Every adverbial use of the phrase in the shipped tree means *unaided* — `skills/adversarial-review/references/dispatch.md` at `453b808`'s *"Naming the working root is not enough on its own"*, `skills/engagement/references/cold-seat.md` at `453b808`'s *"Naming the root is not enough on its own"*, `skills/spikes/SKILL.md` at `453b808`'s *"its compliance certifies nothing on its own"* — and `AGENTS.md`, which is doctrine rather than shipped material, carries the same sense in its own words (*"the names route on their own"*) on the always-on surface every session here loads first. Read that way, a criterion-writer facing several partial confounds, none decisive alone, satisfies the condition and declares reach falsifiable, which is the failure the bullet exists to prevent. The intended sense — *independently of the text under test* — existed only in this repo-only entry, which an adopter never loads. *Receives* would be wrong: `docs/cells/siting/SKILL.md` at `453b808` records that the plugin's source is the repository root, so a git-source install clones everything and repo-only files do reach an adopter's cache as inert content. What an adopter never does is load them.

**Keeping `routes` also repairs an asymmetry the `settles` draft created.** The enumeration's route 1 carries the predicate *settling the situation* and route 2 carries *naming the destination*. A condition phrased on `settles` matches route 1's verb and not route 2's, so a reader can conclude a surface that merely *names* a destination is not reached — an asymmetry the base wording did not have, since `routes` matched neither. **`AGENTS.md` closes it in the practice's own terms:** *"Every cell's own description loads in every session here and states what it covers, so **the names route on their own**."* Naming is routing here, so route 2 is an instance of the condition in one step. The lead-in's own noun — *"Two **routes** have defeated it"* — keeps its lexical tie to the verb above it, which the `settles` draft severed while touching neither sentence.

**And the practice had already compressed this rule the right way.** **[D-338]'s frozen index row states the rule twice** — as-landed in its body, *"to the same place"* and all, and compressed in its bolded headline as *"reach is falsifiable only where nothing else **routes the situation**, or **routes it too weakly to mask the difference**"*. The headline drops the destination, keeps `routes`, and keeps limb 2 working. **What this change adds to that shape is `anyway`** — the shipped sentence is not the headline plus one word, since the headline also elides *the reader loads*, *that same* and *where what does*, all of which the base already carried; it is the headline's shape with the independence operator supplied. Nobody noticed the row until the judge.

## What was rejected

Six candidate wordings were measured on criterion 3's own instrument — `python tools/lint.py`, the `engagement` cell-body row, run from the repository root against this change's base and against each candidate, with the tree restored between them. The base row is 22,627.

- **`settles that same situation on its own`** (−5) — what the first draft shipped. Rejected on both grounds above.
- **`settles that same situation anyway`** (−9) — the `operational` seat's remedy. Answers the sufficiency reading; `settles` still guts limb 2 and still creates the route-2 asymmetry.
- **`settles that same situation`** (−16) — the `cold-read` seat's, and the largest saving available. Independence then rests on the ablation frame rather than on any word, which may carry it, but it leaves both `settles` defects and is the largest unargued semantic change of the set.
- **`routes that same situation either way`** (−7) — the dispatching session's, offered to the defense as a claim to attack rather than a proposal. Rejected: *either way* has no antecedent for what the two ways are, since the destination noun is what the change deletes, so it reinstates the referent defect one level up; and the same line already carries *"it fails the same way"* and *"either polarity"* while `engagement`'s own body carries *"decides it either way"*, putting a fourth way-or-polarity phrase into the paragraph three consecutive experience-session consumers report reading three times.
- **`bears on that same situation`** (−14) — not proposed as a remedy but as the `cold-read` seat's demonstration that the acceptance criteria could not fail an over-broad wording. It passes all three criteria and makes reach essentially never falsifiable, which is the flat ban on the reach form [D-338] expressly declined and the owner never ruled.
- **Repairing the enumeration's lead-in instead of the condition.** The documented failure happens *at* the bolded sentence, before a reader reaches the enumeration, and it costs characters where this saves them. Put to the owner as option 2 of three; not ruled — the owner affirmed option 1.
- **Recording it and changing nothing.** Put as option 3; not ruled.
- **Folding it into [#303](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/303).** Rejected: #303 ablates the charter body, and hanging an eleven-character prose repair behind a far larger change delays it and muddies what that change's review judges.
- **Giving the bare-exclusion case its own words.** Unnecessary — *that same situation* has a referent whether or not the text names a destination.
- **Touching the paragraph's density.** The affirmed brief put it out of scope in terms; it is tracked at [#328](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/328), where this change's own experience session attached a third sighting.

## What this widens in the polarity that already worked, and why that is not smuggled scope

**The condition's width changes in the positive polarity, and this section is here because it must be recorded rather than because it is contested.** Under `to the same place`, a competitor that resolved the situation toward a **different** destination was not a confound — not the same place — so reach was declared falsifiable there. Under `anyway` it is a confound and reach is not. **The new answer is the right one**: if something else genuinely decides the outcome, it decides it in both arms, the ablation is null, and direction has nothing to do with it. A competitor that merely *competes* without resolving does not satisfy the predicate at either wording, and its correct treatment sits in limb 2 — which is precisely why limb 2 had to keep working.

**This does not bar the measurement [D-338] declined to outlaw.** #255's port run separated 5 of 6 against 0 of 6, and a run that separates is by construction one in which nothing settled the situation independently. No wording keyed to independent settlers reaches it. **The warrant sentence's own region grows with the condition it complements**, on inherited evidence and with its characters unmoved; two candidate counterexamples were constructed in the newly reached region and both survived, so this is recorded scope drift rather than a demonstrated falsity.

## The placement call, and what it cost

**The bullet stays in the body**, on [D-338]'s and [D-345]'s reasoning, unchanged: the list's other items are all in the body, and an instance separated from the rule it instantiates is one a reader meets without the rule that explains it.

**What it cost.** The `engagement` cell-body row of `python tools/lint.py`, run from the repository root at this change's base `453b808` and at its head: **22,627 → 22,616 characters, −11.** The bullet itself, 1,542 → 1,531. Still first of thirteen and still carrying no body budget. **This is the first change to shrink this bullet.** [D-338] **created** it — it is absent at `70d59b7` and 821 characters at `ed5a524` — and [D-345] grew it by 721. Both of those entries are on the record as having grown an unbudgeted body deliberately; this one is on the record as the first to give characters back, which a later size pass reading these rows should be able to tell apart. **No always-on row moved** — the edit is in a cell body, and the `.claude/skills/` and `.agents/skills/` copies `tools/roster.py` at `453b808` generates are name-and-description stubs carrying none of it, verified by regenerating them against the head tree and getting a byte-identical result.

**No decision citation is added.** The bullet keeps `[D-338] [D-320] [D-345]`. `docs/cells/records/SKILL.md` at `453b808` makes the marker permissive — *"may cite"* — and a fourth would cost about eight characters against an eleven-character saving on the one cell under standing pressure to shed them. **This entry is therefore not reachable from the bullet at all** — a reader following `[D-338]` lands on a frozen row that states the superseded wording with nothing marking it stale, and `docs/cells/records/SKILL.md` bars that row from ever acquiring a pointer forward. Reachability is through the **index** those predecessors live in, which is why the row below names [D-338] and states outright that this supersedes the wording D-338's own row still carries.

## What the review changed

**A five-seat panel, a defense and a judge ran on `97be2bf`.** All seven stages, and all three cold seats before them, came up on the default branch and each ran `git checkout --detach` and re-confirmed before working — ten for ten, on a mechanism that had silently handed six of six dispatches the wrong tree on an earlier change here.

**The wording this change ships is the review's, not the drafting session's.** The panel produced the diagnosis in three independent pieces — `revision-diff` found the inert limb, `revision-diff` and `cold-read` and `operational` and `wiring-falsifier` converged on the sufficiency reading, `cold-read` found the route-2 verb asymmetry — the defense originated the surviving candidate and the `AGENTS.md` sentence that decides it, and the judge found D-338's own index row, re-ran the measurement on all six candidates, and ruled. **The acceptance criteria could not have caught any of it:** criterion 2's four-cell walk asks only whether each route is *caught*, so it passes every wording strictly broader than the shipped one, and the judge established it had passed the `settles` draft only because it was executed against a gloss that ships nowhere. That is recorded as a finding against this change's own criteria rather than smoothed.

**Eight statements in the first draft of this entry were false and are corrected here.** It attributed to [D-345] a record that entry does not carry, the gap having been recorded by [D-338]. It said `the same way` *"repairs routes 1 and 3"*, of a condition the same draft said route 3 lies outside. It quoted the enumeration's *"apply in either polarity"* with the qualifier **"though only the first has been measured where the text keeps a reader away"** silently dropped — a qualifier PR #345's own review added on [D-320]'s contrary record — and then asserted route 2's mirror masking as fact. It said `away from the same place` failed in the same cell as `the same way`, where the settled criterion puts it in the two positive-polarity cells. It said D-338 *grew* the bullet it created. It named one exposure where there are two. It pinned byte-identity to four commits, two of which exist only on a local branch and are uncheckable by any other reader; those two are dropped and the claim stands on `ed5a524` and `453b808`. And it claimed to have **closed** the standing recorded finding that nothing detects a truncated inline artifact — the dispatches did pair the digest with a readable path, which is a real improvement and is why the third cold seat could verify both, but one seat of three recomputing anything does not close a finding whose own record calls the naive remedy partly unexecutable and names a different one.

**Eight findings were sustained and deliberately not fixed**, each recorded with its promotion condition in rows this change appended to `docs/recorded-findings.jsonl` — they do not exist at the base, so no pin reaches them: the criteria's blindness to over-breadth; the truncation-closure residue; the frequency claim about what a fence most often meets; the divergence this widening opens between the condition and `skills/spikes/SKILL.md` at `453b808`'s docket ban; the warrant sentence's silent region growth; the corner where both quantities are defeated and the bullet names no route; the entry-pin form the guard does not see; and the lint gap that lets a `**Status:**` line disagree with its own filename.
