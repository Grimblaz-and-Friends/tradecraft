# One holding session per change

**Purpose:** record why the unattended stretch has one owner that builds nothing, and what the rules it replaced were for. **Audience:** a future session revising the stretch's ownership, the review's staffing, or the rules retired here. **Success:** they can tell which of the retired rules were made unnecessary by the separation and which were merely moved, and they can find what the first run under this shape actually cost.

The owner affirmed [the brief](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/586#issuecomment-5650589519), including the vendor sentence he added on the second put. The [artifact](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/586#issuecomment-5650854143) was settled before the first commit by a [Claude cold seat's `would`](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/586#issuecomment-5650856099) in one round, with no adverse points and no amendment to the brief.

**The concept lives in `engagement` because it assigns responsibility across the whole unattended stretch**, which is that cell's subject; `adversarial-review`, `experience-session` and this repository's `landing` cell route to it rather than restating it. A second copy of the rule is the thing that drifts.

## The meaning changes

Recorded here because a later session reads the tree, not this pull request.

- `docs/cells/landing/SKILL.md` — *"The stretch from the pull request through the review is run by the session that opened it"* now reads as the holder's, on a change with an affirmed brief, and keeps the opener's obligation for a change owing none. The D-496 failure it was written for — a stretch handed on at the pull request reaching nobody — is unchanged; who is forbidden to hand it on is what moved.
- `skills/adversarial-review/SKILL.md` — the audience clause now names every review role and the implementer taking its fixes, where it named "the session dispatching a review" and its dispatchees. It named the holder too until this change's own review deleted that apposition as net growth on a cell that routes the concept rather than owning it. The purpose and success sentences are untouched.
- `skills/adversarial-review/SKILL.md` — typed-halt wording narrowed from *"no questions mid-run"* to *"no owner questions mid-run"*, which is what lets a stage ask the holder for a probe it cannot run itself.
- `the-stretch.md`, `arbitration.md` and `the-artifact.md` — **this reverses the sentence as first shipped — *"A fallback onto the implementer's vendor cannot supply a qualifying judgment."*** A fallback is a seat, its reason is named in staffing, and truncation is only where neither vendor can supply one. The sentence contradicted [the brief the owner affirmed on #581](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/581#issuecomment-5647604448), an affirmed term this change did not amend.
- `arbitration.md` — **"Merge — parent-owned, before defense"** became **"Merge — before defense"**, the merging party named as the holder. The label went because *parent-owned* was an allocation whose point was that the dispatcher might be the author; the merge's own obligations — the index that never replaces its sources [D-102], the account of every uncarried seat entry [D-453] — are kept in full, and they survive the interest rationale because they were never only about interest.
- `arbitration.md` — the builder/buyer permission paragraph reduced to the routed rule plus the sentence that a lapse outside a review needs an independent ruling. **The review found this went too far and the fix batch restored the missing half**: *"The builder may fix a finding without permission. [D-230]"* now stands beside it. The reduction had kept every prohibition and dropped the permission that made the low fix bar work, which is the opposite of what the artifact licensed.
- `dispatch.md` — the author-as-dispatcher rationale for role separation retired; role separation and judging-seat staffing route to `engagement`. The dispatch's three-part structure, source fidelity and cold carve-outs are unchanged.
- `roster.md` — staffing freshness and vendor assignment route to `engagement`; the tier rule now applies *within* that staffing rather than beside it. The measured retention table and its limits are untouched.
- `the-record.md` — report authorship and return-cost capture attributed to the holder. The fields, their producers, and the rule that cost is read off returns rather than totalled are unchanged.
- `the-artifact.md` and `cold-seat.md` — the artifact's writer resolved to the implementer and the cold dispatch to the holder. The cold definition, the inline carrier with its sha256 and byte count, the three verdicts and the two-round cap are unchanged. **One settlement route did change, in the fix batch**: the truncation now turns on whether a *qualifying* party is in reach rather than any party, and a fallback seat qualifies, so the route is reached only where neither vendor can supply a seat and a transient failure is not an unobtainable check. `arbitration.md` gains the matching route for a required defense or judge that cannot be staffed at all — the review is recorded incomplete and no fitness ruling is claimed, where before only prosecution seats could degrade.
- `running-one.md` — the chartering party and the note's author resolved to the holder on the unattended stretch. The consumer's coldness and isolation are unchanged.

## What the first run under this shape found

The change was built under itself: one Claude session held the brief and dispatched every stage, one Codex implementer was resumed across the artifact, the settlement, the build and the fixes, and every judging seat was a fresh Claude dispatch. Three things came out of running it that argument would not have produced.

**With approvals off, `workspace-write` cannot commit.** All recorded turns used it; git-metadata writes were denied. The rename proved no categorical deny: OpenAI's protected-path policy covers `.git` and `gitdir:` targets alike. The untried `--approve-for-me` route is [#593](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/593)'s first pickup test. The holder committed the implementer's bytes unchanged — 292 files hashed identical before and after — and the gap is pitched there, not patched here.

**The vendor rule leaves four standing orders unperformable.** A Claude seat holds `Read,Glob,Grep`; `roster.md:10` orders `wiring-falsifier` to probe by execution, `roster.md:12` gives `revision-diff` the whole change, and `arbitration.md` orders the defense to re-run every probe. With judging fixed opposite a Codex implementer, no judging party has a shell. The review's own defense could not execute one probe and said so; the fixes name how the order is discharged, and the staffing term itself — the owner's — is untouched.

**Use exercised reading, not operating.** The cold consumer reconstructed the whole division from the material alone and staffed against the implementer's vendor unprompted, which is the strongest evidence on the record that the concept reads correctly. It was a planning run: five of the eight acceptance criteria require observed conduct no run in this review supplied.

## What was rejected, and what is left open

A second copy of the concept in `adversarial-review` was rejected: the review cell routes to `engagement` instead, and the twelve sites the concept touches are the cost of one concept crossing three instruments rather than an outflow. Renaming the roles across the practice was rejected as scope. The holder's context-exhaustion route is unresolved and deliberately unfiled: the rule's own sentence exists, and no incident does, so it fails the filing standard until a holder actually hits the limit.
