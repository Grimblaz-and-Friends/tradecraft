# D-169: The charter is exempt from self-containment in both directions, and `authoring` states what a cell owes the roster

**Status:** Accepted 2026-08-25 (PR #169)

## Context

Self-containment — no skill references another — was written when every skill was a peer elaborating a job, and its reasons are loading cost and multi-site drift, measured against the predecessor's 276 cross-skill references. [D-156](D-156-2026-08-24-installable-plugin-and-shipped-charter.md) moved the practice's binding half into `skills/charter/SKILL.md`. Being a directory under `skills/`, it inherited the rule, and the inheritance was a side effect rather than a decision: `AGENTS.md` had carried three working path references into cells (`skills/engagement` twice, `skills/adversarial-review` once) and the charter that replaced it could restate none of them.

Measured on `b9bcdeb`: the charter named **zero** of the seven other cells. Eight rules it states have their depth owned by a cell — the plain brief, the fork test, the pre-implementation artifact, the purpose/audience/success header, the review's roster and evidence standards, the findings disposition, the routing map, the admission order — and two of the eight reached for periphrasis ("the cell that governs how a session and the owner work together") while six pointed at nothing at all. The owner's ruling, put in his words: without its references the charter's value drops immensely.

The same gap blocked #130 §4. That rule offers two branches for a standard two cells share — route it up to the always-on surface, or give it one owning cell — and the route-up branch had quietly stopped working, because a cell cannot cite the charter either. #130's own §3 had already noticed the older form of this ("the cell cannot cite the doctrine file"); D-156 moved the uncitable target rather than fixing it.

## Decision

**The charter is exempt from self-containment in both directions, in the name form only.** Any cell may name the charter; the charter may name any cell; no cell may name any other. The exemption is one target at depth one, so it cannot grow into a mesh — and the reasons behind self-containment do not reach it: the charter is always-on in every session by construction (imported by `AGENTS.md`, instructed, and emitted by the `SessionStart` hook), so a citation points at prose the reader has already loaded and costs no loading, and a citation cannot fall out of agreement the way a second copy can.

**Paths between cells stay findings even from the charter,** on a ground self-containment never covered: a rooted `skills/…` path does not resolve once installed, because cells sit in a plugin cache rather than beside the reader. This was not the first design — the exemption was written to cover every reference form, and `test_skill_names_its_scripts_relative_to_itself`'s sibling in `test_portability.py` caught it. The narrowing is why the reference form is a **name** rather than a path: a session reaches a skill by invoking it, and the name is the handle that survives relocation.

**The exemption is held by guards, not by prose,** which is the residence principle applied to this change's own rule. `check_sideways_deps` learns the exemption and now reads the name form `` `<name>` cell `` as coupling — it could not before, and its docstring had said so ("not machine-checkable; it is reviewed, not linted"); defining a reference form is what made it checkable. `check_cell_references` fails a reference naming a cell that does not exist, which is the failure a rename causes silently, since prose cannot be resolved the way a path can. The charter's exemption is also universal in target: the `SessionStart` hook's whole job is to emit the charter and `check_delivery` requires that dependency, so a rule forbidding it would have been two guards answering one question differently.

**`authoring` states what a cell owes the roster,** in four standards: the description as the entire always-loaded triggering surface, with the frontmatter guard named as the floor beneath it rather than the standard; depth leaving the body for `references/` on a disqualifier rather than a size [D-104:58]; the standards for code a cell carries; and one owner for a standard two cells share. It **sheds its own depth to stay the size it was** — the tree's first two `references/` files — which is the only evidence that the second standard is applicable rather than aspirational.

**Two duplications inside this change were resolved by the rule it lands, which is how the rule was tested.** `engagement` stopped keeping a synced copy of the fork test and now cites the charter, the route-up branch's first use; and a first draft of the new section had the figures bullet and the scripts bullet both stating the resolve-against-the-naming-file rule, which is exactly the two-half-owners class, cut to one owner.

**The description standard's warrant is anticipatory, and the change says so.** A cold-seat A/B was run because the standard is a claim about what a reader does under a wording. Twelve seats, three arms, two rounds — including an arm with the whole trigger apparatus removed — returned identical answers, so the probe failed its own negative control and licenses no claim either way. At a roster of three or four distinct cells the summary sentence carries the routing and everything below it is redundant; the regime the standard is written against is not reproduced at that width. The standard therefore rests on external evidence and on the width the practice is heading for, and the run is evidence that #89's roster-width condition is doing real work rather than deferring indefinitely.

## Rejected

- **Forbidding the sync note outright (fork B.1), as #130 filed it.** It kills the route-up branch: a cell could never cite the charter, so routing a standard up would force the elaborating cell to go silent about it — stripping the fork test from `engagement`, whose whole job it is, or from the charter, which must stand alone for an adopter.
- **Licensing marked restatement across the ship boundary (fork B.2).** Recommended in an earlier draft and superseded on the merits before it reached the owner: it permits two copies of one sentence, which is the drift §4 exists to kill. Naming a duplication is not removing it.
- **Resolving every existing duplication here (fork A.2).** The owner ruled A.1 — forward-binding — and folded in only the `engagement` sync clause, which B.3 made a one-line change. The purpose/audience/success restatement and the remainder of the fork-test restatement stand, dispositioned by their owning cell's next change.
- **Naming the four cells the charter states no rule for** (`filing`, `spikes`, `experience-session`, `persist-changes`). Owner-ruled out of scope: that adds content rather than restoring lost pointers, and is a roster registry by another name. Discoverability is #89's.
- **Exempting the charter by relaxing the guard generally.** An acceptance criterion was written specifically to fail that shape, and a test requires cell↔cell references to still fire after the exemption lands.
