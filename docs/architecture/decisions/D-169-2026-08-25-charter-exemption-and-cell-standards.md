# D-169: The charter is exempt from self-containment in both directions, and `authoring` states what a cell owes the roster

**Status:** Accepted 2026-08-25 (PR #169)

## Context

Self-containment — no skill references another — was written when every skill was a peer elaborating a job, and its reasons are loading cost and multi-site drift, learned from the predecessor's accumulated mesh of mutual references. No count is stated: the corpus is not in this tree and the figure an earlier draft carried could not be re-derived by any reader of this entry, which is the failure the cell this change lands legislates against. [D-156](D-156-2026-08-24-installable-plugin-and-shipped-charter.md) moved the practice's binding half into `skills/charter/SKILL.md`. Being a directory under `skills/`, it inherited the rule, and the inheritance was a side effect rather than a decision: `AGENTS.md` had carried three working path references into cells (`skills/engagement` twice, `skills/adversarial-review` once) and the charter that replaced it could restate none of them.

Measured on `b9bcdeb`: the charter named **zero** of the seven other cells. Eight rules it states have their depth owned by a cell — the plain brief, the fork test, the pre-implementation artifact, the purpose/audience/success header, the review's roster and evidence standards, the findings disposition, the routing map, the admission order — and two of the eight reached for periphrasis ("the cell that governs how a session and the owner work together") while six pointed at nothing at all. The owner's ruling, put in his words: without its references the charter's value drops immensely.

The same gap blocked #130 §4. That rule offers two branches for a standard two cells share — route it up to the always-on surface, or give it one owning cell — and the route-up branch had quietly stopped working, because a cell cannot cite the charter either. #130's own §3 had already noticed the older form of this ("the cell cannot cite the doctrine file"); D-156 moved the uncitable target rather than fixing it.

## Decision

**The charter is exempt from self-containment in both directions, in the name form only.** Any cell may name the charter; the charter may name any cell; no cell may name any other. The exemption is one target at depth one, so it cannot grow into a mesh — and the reasons behind self-containment do not reach it: the charter is always-on in every session by construction (imported by `AGENTS.md`, instructed, and emitted by the `SessionStart` hook), so a citation points at prose the reader has already loaded and costs no loading, and a citation cannot fall out of agreement the way a second copy can.

**Paths between cells stay findings even from the charter,** on a ground self-containment never covered: a rooted `skills/…` path does not resolve once installed, because cells sit in a plugin cache rather than beside the reader. This was not the first design — the exemption was written to cover every reference form, and `test_sideways_deps_reaches_the_charter_and_the_hooks` in `tools/tests/test_lint.py` caught it, by asserting that a rooted path from the charter still fires. `test_portability.py` is green on that overreach and cannot see it: its `SCRIPT_MENTION` matches `scripts/*.py` and never a cell path. The narrowing is why the reference form is a **name** rather than a path: the name survives relocation where the path does not. It is not a string that resolves bare — Claude Code addresses an installed plugin's skills as `<plugin>:<skill>`, so a runtime may qualify the name on the way in, and two plugins shipping one cell name would collide. What the form buys is that a reader can still follow it; documenting the resolution model is routed to #89.

**The exemption is held by guards, not by prose,** which is the residence principle applied to this change's own rule. `check_sideways_deps` learns the exemption and now reads the name form `` `<name>` cell `` as coupling — it could not before, and its docstring had said so ("not machine-checkable; it is reviewed, not linted"); defining a reference form is what made it checkable. `check_cell_references` fails a reference naming a cell that does not exist, which is the failure a rename causes silently, since prose cannot be resolved the way a path can. The charter's exemption is also universal in target: the `SessionStart` hook's whole job is to emit the charter and `check_delivery` requires that dependency, so a rule forbidding it would have been two guards answering one question differently.

**Two guard boundaries moved with it, named here because a later reader would otherwise re-derive the old ones.** The portability guard stopped enumerating lawful prefixes and now resolves instead, so `../` is lawful wherever it lands inside the cell — its deleted comment had called `../` unlawful for "reaching outside the cell", which is true from `SKILL.md` and false from `references/` — and its read scope widened from `SKILL.md` to every markdown file in the cell, because depth-shedding lets a calling contract be written one directory down. And the reference checks split on form rather than on check: a name is read outside fenced blocks only, because inside one it is a spelling being shown, while every path form — rooted, relative, and a `references/` pointer — is read everywhere, because a path that does not resolve is broken whatever encloses it. Both also read one line wrap, a reflow being a formatting edit nobody inspects that was shown under review to remove a reference from both checks silently. The fence rule itself is CommonMark's, marker and length and info string, because a naive toggle fails in both directions on what a cell documenting markdown writes.

**The self-containment rule narrowed in two ways, neither of them silent.** Its prohibition went from *depends on no other skill* to *names no sibling cell* — a strictly smaller class, and the one a guard can read. Its reason clause kept drift and dropped loading cost; the dropped half is still live, and is what the exemption's own argument turns on, so it is recorded here rather than carried in the shipped rule.

**`authoring` states what a cell owes the roster,** in four standards: the description as the entire always-loaded triggering surface, with the frontmatter guard named as the floor beneath it rather than the standard; depth leaving the body for `references/` on a disqualifier rather than a size [D-104:58]; the standards for code a cell carries; and one owner for a standard two cells share. It **sheds its own depth so its body stays under what it was** — the tree's first three `references/` files — which is the only evidence that the second standard is applicable rather than aspirational. The claim is about the body and not the cell: the description grew from 551 to 698 characters to carry the standard's own requirements, so a session that never invokes `authoring` pays more than it did, and the roster's always-on total moves from 4,149 to 4,296. `python skills/authoring/scripts/figures.py --cell skills/authoring/SKILL.md --budget 7359` derives the body and `tools/figures.py --cell skills/authoring/SKILL.md --cell-budget 7359` adds the description. The roster totals are **each cell's `name` plus its `description`, summed over `skills/*/SKILL.md`** — stated because descriptions alone read 4,060 and 4,207, and a successor inheriting the wrong basis is off by 89 in each direction. The total and what to do about it are #140's.

**Five duplications inside this change were resolved by the rule it lands, which is how the rule was tested.** `engagement` stopped keeping a synced copy of the fork test and now cites the charter, the route-up branch's first use; a first draft of the new section had the figures bullet and the scripts bullet both stating the resolve-against-the-naming-file rule; and the review found three more the change had created against root doctrine — self-containment itself at `AGENTS.md:26`, which is the review's only high, plus the calling-contract and substrate rules, each restated there with its reasons. All five took the same remedy: one owning cell, the other site carrying none of it. `authoring` owns all three standards now and `AGENTS.md` keeps only what is local here — the enforcing mechanisms and the runtimes the rules are written against. The doctrine is 299 characters shorter for it on balance: the two rules it ceded are −380, and the high's own remedy spent +81 of that buying the citation that replaced the restatement.

None of the five is an instance of the duplications the owner's A.1 ruling leaves standing. `AGENTS.md:26` in particular was untouched from the reset until this change gave `authoring` the standard it had been restating — so the change created that duplication and the change resolved it, which is the same bucket as the other four and not the forward-binding one.

**What the reference guard actually covers, stated because the criteria overclaimed it.** It reads the reserved form on one line, and the same form split by one wrap, outside fenced blocks. It does not read a synonym (`skill` for `cell`), an emphasised name, or an unbackticked one. Widening it was priced out rather than dismissed: each widening enlarges the class the existence check over-fires on, and a general multiline match breaks the line numbers both guards report. The prose carries what the guard cannot — the form is stated prescriptively and named as reserved — so a spelling outside it is a defect a reviewer catches rather than one that silently passes.

**The description standard's warrant is anticipatory, and the change says so.** A cold-seat A/B was run because the standard is a claim about what a reader does under a wording. Twelve seats, three arms, two rounds — including an arm with the whole trigger apparatus removed — returned identical answers, so the probe failed its own negative control and licenses no claim either way. At a roster of three or four distinct cells the summary sentence carries the routing and everything below it is redundant; the regime the standard is written against is not reproduced at that width. The standard therefore rests on external evidence and on the width the practice is heading for, and the run is evidence that #89's roster-width condition is doing real work rather than deferring indefinitely.

## Rejected

- **Forbidding the sync note outright (fork B.1), as #130 filed it.** It kills the route-up branch: a cell could never cite the charter, so routing a standard up would force the elaborating cell to go silent about it — stripping the fork test from `engagement`, whose whole job it is, or from the charter, which must stand alone for an adopter.
- **Licensing marked restatement across the ship boundary (fork B.2).** Recommended in an earlier draft and superseded on the merits before it reached the owner: it permits two copies of one sentence, which is the drift §4 exists to kill. Naming a duplication is not removing it.
- **Resolving every existing duplication here (fork A.2).** The owner ruled A.1 — forward-binding — and folded in only the `engagement` sync clause, which B.3 made a one-line change. The purpose/audience/success restatement and the remainder of the fork-test restatement stand, dispositioned by their owning cell's next change.
- **Naming the four cells the charter states no rule for** (`filing`, `spikes`, `experience-session`, `persist-changes`). Owner-ruled out of scope: that adds content rather than restoring lost pointers, and is a roster registry by another name. Discoverability is #89's.
- **Widening the reference guard to every spelling of a name.** Priced out under review, not dismissed: the synonym and emphasis forms evade both checks today, and catching them costs more false positives on ordinary prose and the line numbers the findings depend on. The wrap case was fixed instead, because it is the prescribed spelling merely reflowed.
- **Correcting the spike report's docket enumeration.** Sustained and priced out: the report is a posted record, and records here are append-only — a remedy that requires breaking a binding rule to apply is declined on the record instead.
- **Exempting the charter by relaxing the guard generally.** An acceptance criterion was written specifically to fail that shape, and a test requires cell↔cell references to still fire after the exemption lands.
