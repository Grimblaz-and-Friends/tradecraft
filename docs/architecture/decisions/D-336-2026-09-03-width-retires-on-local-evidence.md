# D-336: The width claim retires on this repository's own record, and five becomes the written default for the modal change

**Status:** Accepted 2026-09-03 (PR #336)

## Context

[D-185](D-185-2026-08-25-finders-of-each-sustained-high.md) landed the clause obliging a report to name each sustained high's finders, and set a trigger: *"after six reviews have published finder data — roughly fifteen to twenty newly attributed highs — a session recomputes and brings the owner an argued call."* (The narrower wording *"recomputes the retention figures … an argued call on width"* is the affirmed artifact's, not this entry's.) It recorded, in as many words, **"Width is not decided."** That entry is frozen and will say so forever; this entry supersedes it on that point.

**The trigger fired and was met.** Twenty-five panel-lane reviews booking sustained highs ran between 2026-08-28 and 2026-09-02, selected from 2026-08-26 onward, against the six the condition asked for. Five read-only extraction passes over their reports recovered 81 panel-originated sustained highs, 55 attributed, 50 naming a primary finder. [The recomputation](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/138#issuecomment-5519548671) carries the corpus, the per-seat table, the sensitivity bound and the re-run recipe, and [a correction](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/138#issuecomment-5519836260) narrows what its width section claimed.

**The August reading reversed.** The archaeology that preceded D-185 could answer only to within a bracket — 62% to 91% — because 61% of panel highs named no finder. On 23 attributed highs it concluded that three seats retained 100% and *"the fourth and fifth seats add nothing on this sample."* On 55 attributed highs that does not survive: it was a small-sample artifact, and 23 highs could not distinguish a seat that finds nothing unique from one whose unique findings had not yet occurred. In August `revision-diff` and `wiring-falsifier` sole-found zero highs; both sole-find now, and every seat does.

**Two narrower panels this practice had floated are refuted by the same data.** The pair [#138](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/138) itself proposed — an executing look plus a claims-vs-sources look — retains 62% of attributed highs counting every credited finder and 30% counting primaries alone, the weakest of every named option. The pair August recommended instead, `claims-vs-evidence` with `cold-read`, measures 73% and 54%.

## Decision

**One: the predecessor's figures retire; the shipped prose states the local curve.** This was the one fork put to the owner, and he settled it at the recommendation on 2026-09-02 ([brief](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/138#issuecomment-5519865279), [affirmation](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/138#issuecomment-5519866880)).

**The rejected alternative was to carry both sets side by side, labelled by what they measure.** It was rejected because the two measure different quantities — the predecessor's 12% is retention of sustained yield across every severity, the local curve is retention of sustained *highs* — and two denominators in one paragraph is how the claim became confusing in the first place. What survives of the predecessor's record is the single-pass 12%, kept because the owner's affirmed recommendation requires naming the figure a local spike separately landed near; the best-hindsight-seat 46% is dropped as surplus to that comparison.

**Two: `SKILL.md` states five seats for an artifact that is both substantially new prose and an amendment to governing prose.** The roster already authorised this at its shape-seat bullet, and five is what 15 of the 20 post-clause panels whose seat table parses actually seated — 15 known of 25; the summary surface said four, and most sessions read only the summary. This is the recomputation's **Direction 3**, resolved in favour of blessing five as the written default rather than tightening the both-shapes clause so the fifth seat becomes a declared-risk choice.

**The con recorded against that option, and not priced:** blessing five *raises the written cost of the modal review*. Concretely, the modal round's recorded-finding rulings rise from 68 to 85 with the seventeen entries this cell now carries. That cost was named in the source and is not quantified anywhere else; a later session revisiting width should treat it as the standing argument on the other side rather than assume the question was costed.

**What the affirmed brief does and does not cover.** Its sentence *"Nothing about how wide our panels actually are changes"* is true of practice — five-seat panels were already lawful and were staffed correctly — and **false for a session reading `SKILL.md` alone**, which is the majority the same brief invokes. A review seat filed this as the change exceeding its brief; the defense and the terminal stage both dropped the finding, on the ground that the brief separately names the disagreement, names the summary page as the surface that is wrong, and tells the owner most sessions are being given the wrong number. The authorisation holds. The inaccuracy of that one sentence does not, and is recorded here so no later session re-derives this change's effect from it.

**Three: what the shipped figures may and may not be.** The review found the first attempt at this paragraph printing the recomputation's hindsight-optimal subsets, whose interior points describe panels this roster cannot staff — the measured best-four excludes `wiring-falsifier`, a standing seat. The shipped table therefore states retention for **the panels the roster actually permits**, both estimators, with the denominators explicit (55 attributed; 50 naming a primary, since five attributed highs name none). Any later recomputation must state which panels it scores; a subset curve and a roster panel are not the same object, and reusing the roster's ordinal seat terms for subset positions inverts which seat the evidence supports.

**This supersedes D-185's "Width is not decided."** The recomputation it scheduled has run, its argued call reached the owner, and the width claim now rests on this repository's own record. What remains open is not the measurement but a values judgement the measurement cannot settle: whether the second shape seat's yield — sole finder, with its partner, of 12 of the 20 sole-found highs — is worth its dispatch. **The finder-attribution clause that made all of this possible is decaying**, from 15 of 15 panel highs attributed across its first six reviews to 3 of the 6 booked on 2026-09-02, 26 of the whole window's 81 unattributed, and six reviews publishing nothing at all; repairing it is sequenced next and is what decides whether the next recomputation is better than this one.

**Two findings this change's own review produced, recorded here because the index row cites them.** The defense discovered that `references/` is **unbudgeted** — `CELL_BODY_BUDGET_CHARS` holds two `SKILL.md` keys — which re-priced a fix docket every stage had treated as budget-blocked; recorded finding #15's *"any remedy landing in that cell must be net-neutral or shorter"* is true of the cell body and false of its reference files, and that entry is frozen and cannot be corrected. Recorded finding #13's promoter fired at 17 entries against 5 seats — 85 rulings before any seat opened the diff, a load this change itself raised by a quarter — and the terminal stage ruled it should be filed rather than recorded a third time.
