# D-590 — Work is bought, never owed

**Purpose:** record why #572 replaces finding debt with offers and how its migration is bounded. **Audience:** a session revising review, filing, or pool behavior. **Success:** it can distinguish the owner's purchased change from the machinery deliberately retired and the transition the owner performs separately.

The [affirmed brief](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/572#issuecomment-5640662649) makes work an offer. The [affirmation](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/572#issuecomment-5640662832) extends the initial hearing to the entire existing pool. On pickup the owner moved that hearing from before build to before merge and undertook it in parallel; the [pickup record](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/572#issuecomment-5649945955) carries that sequencing. This change implements the ordinary rules. It neither makes the hearing's purchases nor certifies its completion.

## What changes meaning

The independent terminal stage gives each finding one fate and one reason: bought, pitched, or lapsed. A real failure of the affirmed brief is already bought. Work beyond that undertaking but not excluded is sold to the terminal stage on value and remedy price. Explicitly excluded work, or work changing what the change is for, is pitched to the owner. The builder can fix or offer work and cannot lapse it. Unsupported findings lapse through an independent ruling. Severity remains harm-if-unfixed; it no longer imposes a placement by itself. A sustained finding is verified as real independently of purchase, including a real offer lapsed on value or price; unsupported offers are not sustained. This keeps the report population and executable floor tied to truth rather than expenditure.

The full current review docket, independent defense and judge, evidence standards, executable floor, lane choice, panel gate, and consequence-shape split remain. The held-findings store is preserved byte-for-byte as closed history: no fetch, append, entry format, or successor re-ruling. The report accounts for each offer and each uncarried seat entry, with like entries grouped; it creates no fourth fate or obligation on another review. External comments may lapse on evidence or value, but a lapse always needs an independent ruling, including comments arriving after the terminal stage or a fix-batch push. A late consumer offer can go to an independent stage outside the review or become a pitch under the filing standard. The tranche rule, weight override, and mandatory placement law go. This supersedes the held-finding machinery recorded in D-441 and D-545 without amending their history.

A filing sells whether work is worth buying in engagement's argued form; it leaves solution design for pickup. The incident-or-run evidence bar for governing prose and its measurement exception remain. Search covers open and closed issues. New evidence for an open pitch extends that pitch as a comment, without a symptom issue or a duty to re-file an uncorrected instance. A closed pitch remains searchable evidence for a fresh argued pitch. Existing numbered cause links are preserved; landing still disposes of linked issues a fix actually discharges.

The pool holds unframed open pitches and the board holds purchased work. The owner buys from the few strongest offers when there is room, with no rating confirmation or assessment gate. Ratings remain the filer's static advisory input. Accrual, rating decay, assessment state, assessment cycle, and rating-triggered push/raise/unraise are retired, superseding those parts of D-438, D-441, and the refresh sequence recorded in D-522. Symptom-count tie-breaking from D-523 is retired; recent activity and the issue number still settle rating ties. Board transport, framed reconciliation, order, and already-purchased cause groups remain.

The explicit `fade` command closes an open unframed pitch after one quiet window, independently of ratings. The shipped window is 30 days and `fade.closes` is true. `updatedAt` supplies activity; unknown, malformed, offsetless, future, framed, and already-closed candidates do not authorize closure. The command rereads each candidate's current state, labels, and activity immediately before closing as not planned, with its reason in a comment. This narrows concurrent-change exposure but is not an atomic compare-and-close transaction. Preview and a policy with closing disabled write nothing. Body and comments are retained. Read surfaces expose activity and eligibility; the refresh explicitly previews and invokes lapse.

The CLI retains list, show, shortlist, framed, policy, labels, rate, frame, and unframe. Removed commands fail through argument parsing. Whole-file policies may still contain retired keys: the loader validates the retained contract and ignores those extras; an old symptoms tie-break is named as retired and ignored. Existing labels are not migrated or removed. Cause-label provisioning stays because the board still uses that label for its existing cause groups. The generic repository lookup stays because the rot reader imports it. Board hints and refresh prose now name commands that exist. The plugin advances to 0.117.0.

## Alternatives and boundary

Keeping held findings, tranches, or a gradual drain for the existing pool would preserve the debt the owner asked to remove. Deleting the historical file would violate the affirmed preservation term. Rejecting every old policy or removing old labels would turn a behavioral simplification into a mandatory migration. Keeping the cause label does not keep the retired symptom-count priority or assessment bookkeeping; it preserves the unchanged board contract.

The [settled artifact](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/572#issuecomment-5649954111) received one [Claude Opus/high cold verdict of would](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/572#issuecomment-5649954196). The settlement block alone changed afterward. Its acceptance criteria govern validation and review. Pool tests fence the wire and exercise closing and protection, including a deliberately broken eligibility check; board and rot tests cover retained callers. The implementation does not run a scheduler or touch live pitches. The one-time hearing remains a separate owner-run transition before merge.
