# D-429: The cause relationship stops being prose and becomes GitHub's sub-issue link

**Status:** Accepted 2026-09-05 (PR #429)

## Context

[#416](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/416), [#417](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/417) and [#425](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/425), worked as one change because the board already bundled them and because one fact sits under all three.

**What the why-chain reached at pickup.** The three read as separate defects — a missing guard, an unbounded cardinality, an unwritten reverse direction — and they are one: **the cause relationship was asserted as a line in one issue's body, in one direction, with no representation any tool could read.** #416 is that seen at the plan, where `parse_plan` carries four columns and none says which row is the cause. #417 is that seen at cardinality, where nothing bounds a relationship nothing holds. #425 is that seen at reciprocity, where the reverse is one hand edit per symptom and so is not performed.

**What the tree held at the base.** At `bab184c`, which is [D-415]'s own landing commit: `skills/filing/SKILL.md`'s tie table carrying `causes #N` / `caused-by #N`; that cell's *"Each paired relationship is written in both directions"*; `docs/cells/board/SKILL.md`'s cause-led grouping rule and its `gh issue list --json number,body` tie read; `docs/cells/landing/SKILL.md`'s walk over every open body; and `tools/board.py` refusing `Standing` + `Queued` while accepting the structurally identical cause pairing thirteen lines later. Across every issue in every state, exactly two carried a cause tie — #404 and #418 — with #404's other fourteen symptoms in the pre-verb prose form.

**Why now.** [D-415] recorded the asymmetry as filed rather than settled, and recorded that this board already carries GitHub's native `Parent issue` and `Sub-issues progress` fields, put out of reach by that change's affirmed brief **"on scope rather than on capability"** and left as the owner's to reopen. He reopened them on 2026-09-05.

## Decision

**1. The relationship is GitHub's sub-issue link, not a tie verb — which reverses [D-415]'s decision 1 by removing its premise rather than by disagreeing with it.** That entry admitted `causes` / `caused-by` after reasoning the relationship against the four existing consequence columns and finding that **none of the four produces a group with a head**. That reasoning was correct and is untouched: the defect was never the row's placement in the set, it was that *any* prose row leaves the relationship unreadable by anything but a person. [D-415]'s decision 3 rejected a check in `tools/board.py` because one **"would have to parse tie prose out of issue bodies to tell a cause from a symptom"** — true of the tree it was written on, and no longer true of anything, because the relationship stopped being prose. **A reversal by premise removal is worth naming as such:** a later session reading only the two headlines would see a decision undone in a day, and the thing that changed in between is what the relationship is made of.

**2. A label marks the cause, not GitHub's issue-type field — the owner's question, and the right answer for three reasons.** He asked *"Would it be better just to use a label instead?"* against a session recommendation of a custom `Cause` issue type. **The decisive reason is cardinality of the field itself:** `Issue.issueType` is a single `IssueType` object, so typing an issue `Cause` spends the only type slot it will ever have and forecloses `Bug` or `Feature` forever; `Issue.labels` is a connection and costs nothing. **The second is portability:** labels are repository-level and travel with `skills/filing/SKILL.md` to an adopter, while issue types are org-level configuration an adopter would have to replicate — a cost that would have landed on the one cell here that ships. **The third is that this repository already does exactly this**: `doctrine` is a repository-level, tool-read label that `ci.yml` keys on, so the pattern is established rather than invented. What the type had over the label is mutual exclusion, which makes it harder to apply sloppily; the guard is what enforces correctness either way.

**3. The label is on the parent only, and there is no `symptom` label, because symptom-hood is a property of an edge.** On a chain — A causes B, B causes C — B is a symptom of A and the cause of C. A `symptom` label contradicts itself on B; `cause` stays true of B, because B genuinely has children. The chain case [#417](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/417) raises is therefore expressible rather than excluded, and the label's definition is what makes it so.

**4. #417's cardinality is enforced by the substrate, and this was measured rather than assumed before the brief locked.** `addSubIssue` for a second parent returns a `VALIDATION` error — *"Failed to add sub-issue #418 to parent #417. Sub issue may only have one parent"* — and, critically, **errors rather than silently re-parenting**, so nothing is destroyed and the guard need not detect a lost tie. The existing link survived the rejected attempt, which was checked. [D-415]'s decision 8 secured single-parentage only for the duration of a dispute, through the disputed-case rule; it is now unconditional and enforced by GitHub, which is why that entry's careful narrowness is no longer load-bearing.

**5. The guard keys on the cause being *open* and on the parent being *labelled*, and both choices do work.** *Open*, so a cause closing lifts the block with no cleanup step for anyone to forget — which is also why **the link is never removed when a cause closes**: it records something that was observed, and the `open` test already makes it harmless. *Labelled*, so an ordinary task-decomposition sub-issue never reaches the board's rules. The second was not in the artifact's first draft and a cold seat named it: an implementation keyed on the bare link passes every other criterion while blocking on decomposition, and nothing in the tree today would have surfaced it.

**6. The owner's exception is expressible in the plan, on a line the parser already discarded.** `docs/cells/board/SKILL.md` reserves to him the ruling that a symptom is worked while its cause is open. **A guard with no way to express that refuses a board filled in correctly** — it would have made the tool reject the one plan he is entitled to. The plan carries `# owner-exception: #N <his words>`, which `parse_plan` already skipped as a comment, so the format does not move and every existing plan still parses. **His words are required and a bare flag is refused**, because a flag lets the exception be taken silently, and the same cell already obliges the refresh note to carry his words. This too came out of a cold seat rather than the drafting.

**7. The verbs are removed rather than kept as a readable mirror.** A prose row beside the link would re-create the two-vocabularies gap [D-415] was written to close, in the same change that closes it. The consequence is accepted and stated: the tie table's cause row now names a mechanism rather than a verb, and a causal relationship that *cannot* be linked — the cause in another repository, or no access — goes in the tie block as flagged prose, the route the cell already has for a relationship no name expresses.

**8. The migration is the whole population, which is what makes it not a judgement call.** #404 is labelled and its fifteen symptoms linked — the fourteen its `cause-of` line named plus #418 — and those two prose lines came out of its body. This was put to the owner as *"why deal with 404's symptoms, but not others?"*, and the answer is that there are no others: the probe returns #404 and #418 alone, #417 having matched a grep only because it quotes the vocabulary while documenting it. **The two closed symptoms are linked too**, so the group stays whole and a later reader is not left wondering which fourteen the retired line meant.

**9. When an issue is ever *asked* whether it has a cause is out of scope, and that boundary is his.** He named the gap the session's own framing had collapsed — *"most of the 105 have never run through the process to consider a cause... not that they don't have a cause. So the questions is when to do it"* — and ruled it separate work, filed as [#427](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/427). **He also ruled against carrying a marker for it here**, choosing *"File "when" separately, no marker now"*, so `parent: null` continues to mean both *asked, no cause* and *never asked*. That is deliberate and is the one thing this change could have made cheaper later and did not: the argument that lost was that a marker nothing consumes is bookkeeping until #427 exists.

## What this change cost, and what it did not touch

**Net growth, argued because `skills/authoring/SKILL.md` requires it.** `python tools/lint.py` at `HEAD~1` and at `HEAD`: `board` 12,576 → 13,301, `filing` 11,197 → 12,420, `landing` 3,573 → 3,683 — **+2,058 characters**, `engagement` unmoved. All report *no body budget*, so no ceiling is in play and no admission row is owed; the always-on row is unmoved, no description having changed. **The growth is the mechanism's operating detail**, which the prose route did not need because it did nothing: the two GraphQL reads the cells now name, the exception directive, and what the label means. The landing cell is the one place the change *simplified* a rule — its warning never to read symptoms off the cause's own issue is deleted, because one link is carried by both issues and the filing order no longer matters.

**Two cold seats, both `would`, no adverse verdict.** The first judged sha256 `32ef0cea…` (9,280 bytes) and returned `would` with four observations. The artifact was revised on all four rather than settled there — adding decisions 5 and 6 above — which added claims and so made it a fresh draft; a second, fresh seat judged sha256 `2962797b…` (11,144 bytes) and returned `would`. **Two of this entry's nine decisions came from a seat that was not obliged to produce them**, which is the argument for spending the second round.

**The guard was mutation-tested rather than merely covered.** With `check_causes` neutered, exactly the four refusal arms fail and no acceptance arm does. It was then run against the live 105-row board: the real plan is accepted with 0 moves and 0 relabels, the same plan with one symptom flipped to `Queued` is refused naming symptom, cause and remedy, and the exception directive makes that same plan lawful again while a bare flag does not.

**What an adopter receives.** The link and the label reach them through `skills/filing/SKILL.md`; the guard and both cell-level reads do not, `tools/board.py` and the two repo-only cells being this repository's. That asymmetry is [D-415]'s decision 6 unchanged, and is recorded again only because the shape of it moved: an adopter previously got a verb their ranking consumed, and now gets a mechanism their ranking consumes and their transport does not check.

## Rejected

**GitHub's issue-type field carrying the marker.** Decision 2. It was the session's own first recommendation and the owner's question overturned it; the decisive objection is that the type is a single slot.

**Keeping `causes` / `caused-by` as a prose mirror beside the link.** Decision 7. It reads as the safe option and is the exact defect being removed.

**A `symptom` label on each child.** Decision 3. Rejected because it is false on any chain.

**Removing the link when a cause closes.** Decision 5. Rejected because the relationship is a fact that was observed, and the `open` test already makes a closed cause harmless.

**Carrying a marker that an issue was assessed for a cause.** Decision 9. Put to the owner argued, with the cost of deferring named — a later sweep re-walks the board — and he ruled it out.

**Widening this change to answer when the why-chain runs.** Decision 9. Rejected as a different set of cells and a different brief; it would have reopened `engagement`'s pickup rule and the refresh's explicit refusal to re-derive bundling.

**Settling the artifact on the first `would`.** Rejected in favour of one more seat, on the reasoning that the observation about the owner's exception would otherwise arrive as a review finding and a fix batch at higher cost.
