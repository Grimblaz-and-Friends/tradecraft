# D-392: One isolation contract for every dispatched recipient, a close event that is a condition on the tree, and the report's field list reaching the stage that must elicit it

**Status:** Accepted 2026-09-05 (PR #392)

## Context

Three defects in `skills/adversarial-review`, all found by use of the instruments [PR #382](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/pull/382) landed, and all in the file that dispatches most of this practice's sessions.

**The isolation clause tested when isolation is needed rather than what it must be.** `dispatch.md` opened it *"Seats that mutate the tree get their own worktree, cut detached from a base the dispatch names, and their write boundary there."* Every obligation in the paragraph hung off that condition, which is false for most dispatched recipients — non-mutating seats, the defense and the judge are its majority population. PR #382 rewrote the sentence and left the gate byte-identical. `docs/recorded-findings.jsonl` carries the entry, promoted by *"a non-mutating dispatched seat leaving a branch or worktree"*, and every non-mutating seat on that review met it; the defense and the judge each reported it from inside.

**Nothing said when such a tree may be removed.** [PR #307](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/pull/307) added a clause keying removal to resumability, which in this harness never goes false, so it licensed no cleanup at all and was withdrawn. The second recorded entry measured 61 worktrees at the time; this change measured 85 at drafting, 88 an hour later, with 33 `worktree-agent-*` branches outliving their trees. The motivating incident is that review's own dispatching session removing a cold seat's tree while the seat was still verifying.

**The stage that must ask for the report's fields was told not to read the list.** `the-record.md` enumerates what the final report carries; its load condition said *"Nothing before the terminal ruling needs it."* PR #382's experience-session consumer, given a dispatcher's job, read the file anyway, back-derived the seat output format, and reported plainly that *"that derivation is mine, not the skill's, and a dispatcher who read only `roster.md` and `dispatch.md` would not produce it."* The same consumer read `dispatch.md:5`'s *"Its output format and working location where it differs are the only other per-recipient content"* three times, reported both readings compliant, resolved it the way that produced byte-identical Output sections outside the byte-identical block, and called that odd.

## Decision

**Every dispatched recipient works in its own tree, cut detached at the revision the dispatch names — and a recipient dispatched to write owes its write boundary there beyond that.** *"and carrying no branch"* stood in the first commit and the review's fix batch removed it: *cut detached* already says it, and as a separate requirement it was unreachable on the tool-cut route, whose recipient runs `git checkout --detach` and leaves the branch standing. The gate now states what isolation *is*, which is the framing the recorded entry itself asked for. Reading and writing seats are deliberately **not** collapsed: the property is uniform and the write boundary is the one clause the writing minority owes on top. The two creation routes were already distinguished at `dispatch.md:13` — the dispatcher positions a root it creates, a recipient launched into a tool-cut root detaches itself — so the widened gate states the property and those sentences keep saying how each route reaches it.

**A dispatched recipient's tree becomes removable when the review that dispatched it closes — its final report posted — and not before.** Three properties were required of the event and are why this one was chosen. It **goes false and then true**, which is precisely what PR #307's version failed. It sits **on the safe side of the incident**: `arbitration.md:5` re-dispatches once a seat that returned unusable output, so any earlier event is true while the tree may still be needed. **That support is falsified by this change's own `:17`** — a re-dispatched recipient gets its own tree, so the failed seat's is not still needed; what carries the choice is the incident and the criterion the event was chosen against. And **removal covers the branch the tree's creation made**, since a branch nothing removes is what outlives its tree. **The qualifier on that clause was first written as *where it was cut without `--detach`* and the review found it stated on the wrong git variable** — a branch is made only where no committish is given and the flag is absent, and after the recipient detaches, a tree cut either way is byte-indistinguishable in `git worktree list --porcelain`. It now reads *where one still stands*, which is a fact about the present that can be checked.

**It is written as a condition on the tree rather than a duty owed by a live session, and that wording is the whole of whether the rule reaches anything.** Trees outlive the sessions that cut them: of the 85 measured at drafting, essentially none had a live dispatcher. **The 68 this entry first attributed wholly to the dispatching tool is two populations.** Re-derived by the review at 98 trees: 47 are `agent-*`, cut for a dispatched recipient, and **34 are ordinary session worktrees** — `start-383-389-69ac71`, this change's own working root, among them — dispatched by no review and reached by the close event's subject not at all. **Criterion 6 was run by the review's judge rather than by this change**: the subject reaches the 15 dispatcher-positioned trees plus the review-dispatched subset of the 47, a ceiling of 62 of 98 against the criterion's floor of 15, so the bar is cleared while a per-tree count stays underivable. A rule worded *the dispatching session removes its own trees* makes none of the standing population removable while reading as though it had addressed them. **The cold seat caught this**, finding the artifact's own Call 2 and Call 3 stating the clause in the two incompatible forms; the shipped sentence takes the condition form.

**The report's field list reaches the dispatcher by widening both excluding hops, and nothing is copied.** [D-388](D-388-2026-09-04-a-move-must-reach-the-reader.md) landed the ladder **sixteen minutes** before this change began — PR #388 merged `2026-09-05T04:00:32Z`, the brief was posted `04:16:31Z`, the first commit made `04:42:32Z`, so the two overlapped — *"what widens is the cheapest surface that closes it — a load condition, then a pointer, then a description; returning the rule to its source restores the second copy"* — checked by reading every hop rather than by finding the source's words in the destination. Read: `SKILL.md`'s pointer routed only the closer; `the-record.md`'s load condition disclaimed the dispatcher outright; `dispatch.md` and `roster.md` cite the file without instructing a load. **Both excluding hops widen**, and `dispatch.md` gains only the shared-block mention that makes output requirements assignment content. **PR #382's precedent — one report field written into `dispatch.md` — is the ladder's last rung**, and is treated here as the exception it was rather than the pattern to scale.

**Four report fields have no producer the dispatcher is told about, not one.** #383 filed the survey as unrun; run here, it returns a seat's raw count, the revision and tree each recipient reports, findings attributed well enough to name each high's finders, and what the review cost — the last being *"read off what each dispatch returned"* with `grep -rno "cost[a-z]*\|token[a-z]*" skills/adversarial-review/` returning, **at the base `8ed30af`, 11 occurrences over 9 distinct lines** — `SKILL.md:24` and `arbitration.md:15` carry two each — and 12 over 10 at this change's own revision, the twelfth being the clause it adds. None is in `dispatch.md`. **The first wording claimed one clause naming the class closes all four; the review falsified it.** Attribution's producer is the merge, already instructed at `arbitration.md:5`, and cost's is the dispatching harness — so the shipped clause names each field's own producer instead of asserting the dispatch is the only place any of them can be asked for. The load-condition rung still beats a per-field sentence, on the cheaper ground that one load condition reaches the reader for all four.

**What every recipient's output must carry is assignment content; only the role's own output shape is per-recipient.** That is `dispatch.md:5`'s ambiguity closed in the second direction. **The support first given for that was false and the review caught it**: the report does *not* read the same things off every recipient — per-seat counts are per seat, and there is no raw-count column for a defense or a judge. What is uniform is the *ask*, not the read, and the shipped sentence says so.

**Each dispatched recipient states whether its tree was its own or shared, and the report carries it.** This is the owner's ruled option, and it is the answer to a question PR #382's merge gate put and its merge did not rule — gate item 2's option (b), *"a report line, not a check, so inside your exclusion"*, recommended by that review's judge to land with #389. It answers isolation the way PR #382 answered the revision: with visibility rather than prevention.

## Rejected

- **Prose only.** Cheapest, and it lands almost entirely in the one file that owns these rules. Rejected by the owner: this is the same material that was obeyed and still failed — [#311](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/311)'s five probes each complied with the mutating-seat sentence as written — and nothing would observe whether the widened gate held any better than the narrow one.
- **Prose, the report line, and a built thing.** A script listing dispatched trees and saying which are removable is the only option that reduces the residue rather than describing it. Rejected: it needs the owner to rule that his standing exclusion's trigger fired, and he ruled it had not. Against it independently — a tool deleting on a close event stated for the first time is the direction that already removed a live seat's tree, and **most of the residue is not reachable by a dispatcher's rule at all**: 68 of the 85 trees sit under `.claude/worktrees/`, cut by the dispatching tool with no dispatcher choosing them.
- **A seat-output section written into `dispatch.md`**, following PR #382's precedent for one field. Rejected as D-388's last rung and the second copy that rule names.
- **An isolation field on `docs/reviews.jsonl`'s index row.** The owner ruled *one report clause*. Whether the index's *"facts about the run"* licence also admits isolation is a separate argument this change does not make, and `docs/seat-record.jsonl` — which carried an `isolated` boolean until 2026-08-19 — is frozen archive.
- **Shedding the mutating-seat sentence's `git worktree add` explanation**, expected when the gate widened on the theory that what was special-cased became general. Withdrawn on reading it: that text explains the two positional arguments and what each does, not the gate, and it is the only place the flag's behaviour is stated. **That withdrawal was right and the accompanying claim that the change shed nothing was false**: `dispatch.md:5`'s *"where it differs"* qualifier was deleted by the first commit and survived only as a quotation of the base inside this entry. The review's fix batch reinstated a qualifier of that shape, so what ships restores it.

## Left open

**Whether the harness's own trees are reachable by anything stronger than the close event.** The clause keys on the dispatch rather than on who ran `git worktree add`, which reaches them; nothing here changes *how* the tool cuts one, so they still arrive branch-attached and are still detached by the recipient. Round 2's own worktree was branch-attached, which the seat reported.

**Whether finder attributability needs its own clause.** The new sentence names it among the four recipient-produced fields, but nothing obliges a seat to structure its findings so the merge can attribute them; today it works by convention.

**Criterion 6's population is a moving number.** The artifact's falsifier says *"the 85 trees now on this machine"*; round 2 watched it reach 88 under its own dispatch and noted that whoever runs the check should re-count rather than reuse the figure. Recorded rather than fixed, because editing a judged criterion after its verdict returns the artifact to draft.
