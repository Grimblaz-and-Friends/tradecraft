**Status:** Accepted 2026-08-30 (PR #252)

## Context

Two shipped sentences told an adopter something that was this repository's answer rather than theirs.

**The routing rule, run backwards.** `skills/substrate/SKILL.md` shipped *"**Stdlib-first Python** here"* as a standard, under an Audience reaching *"a repo that adopts the practice"*, while `AGENTS.md` read *"**Substrate here is Python**, per the `substrate` cell's standard"* — deriving the local pick **from** the shipped cell. `skills/authoring/references/routing.md` bars exactly that: *"the standard ships ... and the repo's own files carry the application, never the reverse, and never duplicated prose that can drift."* PR #192's `cold-read` and `operational` seats found it, and the repository's external Codex reviewer found it independently as its highest-priority comment. What #192 landed was a **two-site scoping** — an Audience clause saying an adopting repository's substrate is its own, and a rule clause saying the pick is this practice's own — and [D-192](D-192-2026-08-25-substrate-cell-and-the-authoring-trigger.md) records that as narrowing the inversion rather than removing it: the same pick was stated twice, with two referents for *here* identical in this repository and divergent in an adopter. [#197](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/197) is that residue.

**A guard stricter than the rule it enforces.** `skills/substrate/references/text-mode.md` rule 2 said *"reach for it rather than hand-rolling"*. `check_stdio_wired` also requires the **import binding** — a locally defined function named `utf8_stdio`, doing the right thing, is refused, pinned by `test_a_local_utf8_stdio_does_not_satisfy_the_wiring_check`. No shipped prose said so. Surfaced by PR #232's second experience session, whose cold consumer wrote a new `tools/` script and got it right, and said why: it had opened the guard before writing, and named the case that fails — *"one that hand-rolled the stream setup would not."* [#241](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/241).

So rule 2 told an adopter to reach for a helper they cannot reach and, in the same sentence, that the obvious hand-roll is insufficient — leaving them a rule with no discharge at all. This is #197's third deferred item, resolved here rather than left open, because it constrains #241's wording.

## Decision

**The standard states no language.** Bullet 1 becomes *"**One substrate, chosen once and hardened**, because one substrate is one set of idioms to harden. Which one is the adopting repository's own call, made in its doctrine."* The minimal form, owner-ruled against a fuller standard saying what makes a pick honest.

**"Stdlib-first" goes with "Python", and that is a judgment rather than a trim.** That half is about admitting dependencies, and D-192 records #192 cutting a dependency rule as agent-proposed with no incident behind it, along with the description's *"or a dependency"* trigger, *"charging always-on cost for a question the body does not answer."* There is no general standard for it to generalise into, so keeping it would have re-admitted the cut rule under a broader wording — which the charter's admission order refuses.

**`AGENTS.md` makes the pick on its own authority**, dropping *"per the `substrate` cell's standard"*. The Audience clause #192 added to defuse the bullet becomes redundant once the bullet states no pick, and is this edit's outflow; the always-on surface falls 16,464 to 16,427 here and is unchanged at 10,546 for an adopter, the description being untouched.

**#197's last deferred question is answered in-line: the adopting repository's own doctrine is the place.** Six words on an unbudgeted body. The charter already routes a binding always-on rule there, so this states a consequence rather than admitting a rule; the alternative was to say nothing and leave a reader holding a standard with no home for their answer.

**Rule 2 states what a compliant setup *is* before naming a helper.** Two properties — UTF-8, *and* the platform's newline translation suppressed — so a reader with nothing to import still owes both halves and not the easy one. Then the helper, and **imported by name rather than defining your own**, with the reason: a local function with the right name satisfies the call site and sets nothing up, so a guard holding this rule reads the import binding and refuses it. Owner-ruled for both halves over the cheaper one-clause fix, which would have closed #241 while knowingly leaving the reachability defect above.

**The guard-writing section stops restating the mechanism and points at the rule.** It read *"Check the import binding too, or a local no-op with the right name satisfies the guard while setting nothing up"*; it now reads *"Check the import binding too, for the reason the second rule gives."* #241 offered *move it up, or accept the duplication with a reason*; **neither.** The two sentences are different imperatives to different readers — *do this* to a script writer, *check this* to a guard writer — so both are owed, but the **fact** beneath them was owned twice, which is the two-half-owners shape `skills/authoring/SKILL.md` bans and whose stated failure mode is that keeping copies in step by hand is the mechanism by which they drift. One owner, one pointer.

## Rejected

- **A general standard saying what makes a substrate pick honest** — available on every platform CI covers, stdlib sufficient, one test matrix. Owner-ruled out as the fuller of the two options. It asserts things no incident here produced, which is the ground #192 cut three draft clauses on.
- **One clause on rule 2** — adding *"imported by name, not defined locally"* and stopping. It closes #241 as filed and leaves the adopter an unreachable helper, which the check above had already established. Cheaper and knowingly incomplete.
- **Splitting the cell**, general standards in `SKILL.md` and this practice's discharge behind a reference. Priced out by the two-site finding: a restructure of a cell five days old, to separate two sentences.
- **Making `winio` importable by an adopter.** A packaging change, out of scope, and the wrong instrument for a prose defect. The remedy here is to state the discharge; whether adopters should reach shipped `lib/` at all is a live question this does not settle and is #197's residue.
- **The general standard [#241](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/241) floats** — *a shipped rule states its own strictness, because the guard that enforces it here does not ship*. Three instances stand behind it (#235 as filed, #241, [#237](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/237)), which clears the charter's admission bar on incident. **Deferred rather than refused:** landing it while #237 is open leaves it unhonoured on its own second instance, and a rule that ships already violated teaches the opposite of what it says. Worth filing when #237 is picked up.
- **Changing `lib/winio.py`, `check_stdio_wired`, or any test.** The guard is right; the prose was short. #241 is a prose defect and stays one.
