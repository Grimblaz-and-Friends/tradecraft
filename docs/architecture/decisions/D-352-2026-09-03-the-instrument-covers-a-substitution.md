## The condition

Every rule of the cold-seat A/B was written for an **ablation**: one arm holds the text, the other has nothing. The shape `skills/spikes/SKILL.md`'s own description names first — *"a candidate wording for a rule"* — is a **substitution**, where both arms hold something. D-337's acceptance criterion 7 promised to cover it; [that change's second experience session](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/pull/337#issuecomment-5521017493) ran a substitution against the shipped text, reached a correct verdict, and reported that it had derived plant placement by analogy and **translated** the keep rule into wording of its own, saying so: *"defensible but it is my reading, not the cell's words, and a different session could reasonably land elsewhere."* Criterion 7's stated falsifier never fired; the consumer built a plant, forced verdicts, reported nothing inapplicable, and translated anyway.

**Three premises, and the third is the one this entry exists to witness locally.** That the pre-change text makes a session translate, and that a substitution reaches `skills/spikes/references/licensing-a-deletion.md` anyway through the *"Where a plant must be scored"* limb, are each corroborated twice in this repository — at D-337:39 and in `docs/reviews.jsonl`'s `pr-337` row. The third is not corroborated here at all, and this change's review found that: **the seat-level confound is what decided that consumer's verdict.** In its own words, *"The cell treats the plant purely as a validity gate and never as a per-seat baseline. Using it as a baseline is what turns 1-vs-2 from a weak signal into no signal"* — plant-citers and text-citers were the same three seats across both arms, which explains the arm split more simply than any wording effect. That sentence lives only in the linked comment; `grep -rn "per-seat baseline" .` returns nothing in this tree. It is recorded here so a session re-deriving the ruling below from the local record finds all three premises witnessed rather than two.

## The decision

**A substitution is named as a third run shape, in the bullet that already hangs plant placement off run shape.** `screen` and `confirm` were there; `substitutes` joins them, its plant riding unchanged in both arms as a confirm's does, and the arms differing in which wording the sentence carries. **A named shape rather than a generalisation** of *the text under test* to *the arm's candidate*: generalising would have made the plant rule, the void gate, the keep rule and the citation test vaguer at once to cover one case, where a third name uses machinery already present. That call is the session's, reported on the issue.

**The scoring rules follow the shape into `skills/spikes/references/licensing-a-deletion.md`**, on the cut D-337 made — rules every run needs in the cell body, rules a scorer needs behind the pointer. A substitution's **disposition** (adopt the candidate or leave the current wording standing, the default being the wording already there), its **citation test** (substance one wording carries and the other does not; substance both carry is not a citation), and **what its null may claim** (a bound on the effect, not an absence of one) land there.

**The one fork was the owner's and they ruled it: the plant is read seat by seat on every run, not only in a substitution.** Where every arm carrying the plant has the same seats citing the plant as citing the text, an arm-to-arm difference in how many cited the text counts those seats rather than measuring the text, and such a separation returns a null. **Option A, stated generally**, over **B**, scoping it to a substitution — rejected because the confound is not substitution-specific and scoping it would read as if an ablation were immune — and over **C**, recording it without admitting it — rejected because the one substitution run this repository has would have been scored wrong without it. The ground for A is what a later session most needs, because the shipped sentence originally ended *"It bites hardest in a substitution, where the signal is weak by construction"*, which argues for B; the review struck that sentence, and this paragraph is what replaces it as the record of the scope.

## What the review changed, and the two claims it retracted

Five seats, a defense and a judge, on a 45-line diff. **Four sustained highs**, and they are one gap seen from four angles: **the change generalised what a scorer does after a run and left what a designer does before one written for a deletion.** The record search at the reference's `:7` (*"a hit ends it … such a hit keeps the text, and the run is not dispatched"*) bound a substitution and its remedy cancelled it — a hit witnesses the *incumbent* is live, which is what the default already assumes; fixed in the substitution paragraph, so every deletion run sees no change. The near-miss rule's property had two referents once the arms hold two wordings; it is now read of both. A **candidate that drops a clause removes it**, and such a run is a confirm on the dropped clause answering to the deletion rule — without which a clause could be deleted under a paragraph that disclaims a deletion licence. And the confound's own sentence admitted a reading under which it was unexecutable.

**Acceptance criterion 1 is not met as the criterion is written**, and the judge ruled it so rather than around it: its falsifier is *"reading whether it reports having derived a rule by analogy or supplied one of its own"*, unscoped, and this change's own cold consumer reported exactly that — on the record search, the one paragraph the change did not touch. It is met on the four edits the change made. The fix closes it and the post-fix experience session re-tests it. **Criterion 6 holds**, and the defence the change failed to make is the general one the review supplied: no disposition anywhere in the instrument keys on a citation *count* — deletion on *"no seat, in either arm, cited"*, `keeps` on one citation from the arm holding the text, `unresolved` on the arm it was removed from, void on plant-silence with the arms agreed — so the new clause, which fires only on a count difference, can move no ablation licence **and no ablation disposition**. `grep -rn "count" skills/spikes/` returns the word as a quantity exactly once, in the new sentence.

**Two figures this change asserted are retracted here.** The boundary statement said *"two frozen decision entries plus three `docs/recorded-findings.jsonl` rows name the current path"*, given as the reason the reference file was not renamed. It is **seven** rows — 143, 144, 147, 161, 163, 165 and 168 — with eight occurrences, and `docs/architecture/decisions/README.md:107` is an unmentioned fourth lint-blocking site beyond the two entries (`grep -c "skills/spikes/references/licensing-a-deletion\.md" docs/recorded-findings.jsonl`, against the same at `453b808`, which also returns 7). Nobody ran the count. The direction of error is conservative — the true figure argues the call harder — and the call stands. And **recorded finding 147 was credited with reserving the calibration of *how generously substance is read* to the owner; it says no such thing.** Row 163 does, verbatim; 147 says only that the discipline reads as complete without a reader learning that generosity decided the only deletion this instrument has licensed. So this change ships a **new** calibration — substance one wording carries and the other does not — with half the support it claimed for leaving it uncalibrated.

**And one of the session note's own sentences was found to contradict another.** Its first bullet says the consumer *"reported no translation"*; twelve paragraphs later it reports the one translation the consumer made and names it as *"the same class the change closes, surviving in the one paragraph it did not touch."* The note is a posted record and is not rewritten; the correction is here. So is its truncation reason: it said `docs/architecture/` and the `docs/*.jsonl` records were excluded *"which carry this change's own record"*, true of the first and false of the second — `grep -c "342" docs/recorded-findings.jsonl` returns 0. What those files actually carry is the seven rows above.

## What was rejected or left alone

- **Renaming the file.** Its H1 already reads *"Scoring a cold-seat A/B, and licensing a deletion from one"*, so scoring generally is already its subject, and the seven recorded rows plus four entry sites above cannot be edited to follow a rename in records that are append-only or frozen. The review found the half no guard enforces: nothing in `tools/lint.py` resolves paths in `docs/recorded-findings.jsonl`, so a rename would red on the four entry sites, go green, and silently strand all seven rows. That gap and the one-directional pointer guard beside it went to the board.
- **A width or an adoption threshold for a substitution.** Neither ships. Every available answer is a number with no evidence behind it, and this repository has twice refused a ruling arriving as a constant; it went to the board so its pickup can put the fork to the owner with data. Carried with it: an external reviewer's argument that requiring candidate-exclusive substance makes a pure clarity rewrite unmeasurable by construction, and that arm-attribution of shared-substance reliance is the better test. It is a good argument and it changes what the instrument measures, which the charter's admission bar does not let a review finding do; the citation test as landed states the case it covers rather than asserting no other test could apply.
- **A fourth run shape for the further arm** D-347 licensed, which strips no class, removes more than the text alone, and has no unstripped arm — so it satisfies none of the three now named, and the enumeration reads more closed than it did. Recorded. Recorded finding 164 priced that remedy on three grounds and one has eroded: *"the remedy is a third run shape in the cell body"* no longer holds, a third shape being in the cell body. Its other two stand, and its promoter did not fire.
- **A budget for `spikes` in `CELL_BODY_BUDGET_CHARS`**, on D-337's standing ground.
- **Recorded findings 143, 147, 163, 165 and 168.** All stay recorded; none is a limb of this ruling.
