# D-381: The posted brief is the agreement rather than the ask, and the artifact is withheld from a session that cannot reach the owner

**Status:** Accepted 2026-09-04 (PR #381)

## Context

Two defects in one passage of `skills/engagement/SKILL.md`, worked as one change under one brief.

[#373](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/373): the brief is put to the owner as an ask and posted as the record of what was agreed, and no sentence said the second form carries the answers the first collected. `:18` named *"what the owner is being asked"*; the charter calls the posted comment *"the record of what was agreed, never where they first read it."* A session following `:18` literally posts the question form — which happened during #303's convergence, and happened again in this change's own conversation until the owner stopped it. The cost is that an exclusion the owner set in conversation and never wrote down is silently demoted from the owner's to the session's, which is the axis `arbitration.md`'s third limb turns on.

[#374](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/374): the wait rule enumerates what a session may do when it cannot reach the owner, and the artifact was not among the three items. Read against the map, which puts the artifact inside the unattended stretch beginning at the affirmation, it is withheld; read against *"except the work itself"*, which a reader takes for the code, it is not. That issue records five of six cold consumers taking the second reading and building one, three writing out the conflict first.

**The second question was already half-answered and the half mattered.** [D-300](D-300-2026-09-01-the-boundary-and-its-two-crossings.md) records the owner rejecting all three licences [#286](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/286) offered for an unaffirmed brief — *"There is no scenario where they proceed without me."* But the same ruling's next sentence puts the unattended band's near end at *"implementation start"*, while the map puts it at the affirmation, and the artifact falls in the gap. That gap is what the five walked through.

## Decision

**Both forks went to the owner argued, and he ruled both.** *"A for both"*, then a requirement that reshaped the change: *"which also means I need to see the updated brief and approve it with no further changes before we continue on."* The brief was re-put carrying his rulings and affirmed in that form — #373's own fix, applied to itself before it was built.

**1. A session that cannot reach the owner may not draft the artifact.** The enumeration names it as withheld rather than leaving it to be inferred, and a job whose own terms demand it does not license it — that goes back to whoever set them. The owner's exclusion, carried in the brief and therefore his rather than the session's: **making the artifact available to a waiting session under any label, a draft included.**

**2. The brief that locks and gets posted is the agreement, not the ask.** Each ruling written in as settled, and anything ruled out stated so it stands without the conversation. The contents bullet and the specimen brief four lines above it both name what the owner has settled.

**3. The exclusion is carried in the owner's own words where those carry it, and in a restatement he affirms where they do not.** This is not what the change shipped first. It shipped *"in their own words rather than paraphrased"*, and the external pass showed that impossible in its own modal case — **this change's own convergence is the demonstration**: the ruling was *"A for both"* and the affirmation *"affirm"*, neither of which carries the exclusion, and the brief posted on #373 states it as a restatement, which the rule as first written called a defect.

**4. Edit A is sited beside the exclusion bullet rather than at the locking paragraph**, which governs posting and is the other candidate. That paragraph sits twenty-six lines below the material a session drafting a brief reads, and the defect is a session reading the brief section literally. The rule is stated once; the locking paragraph is left alone so no second copy exists to drift.

**5. `:92`'s false antecedent is struck rather than qualified.** As shipped it asserted that *a dispatcher* set the job's terms, which the same section contradicts — the owner routinely sets them, and the wait rule's precondition is that he is unreachable. The panel called this its most serious finding and proposed fifteen added words; the defense disproved the stated consequence and the terminal stage struck the false clause instead.

## Rejected

- **Making the artifact available as a labelled draft.** It is what five of six measured consumers actually did and it commits nothing, which is its whole case; against it, it is a route to proceed without the owner under a label, which is the second licence he refused on #286. Put to him with both sides; he took the withholding.
- **Keeping the posted brief as the ask with the answers attached beneath.** No restatement risk, but the record still reads as an open question — the exact shape the owner caught — and the artifact quotes the brief verbatim, so it would inherit a question form as its governing term.
- **Adding a clause at `:21` saying where the re-putting happens** (~25 characters), against deleting the sentence that created the need (44). Both were argued by seats. Deletion was taken because it is cheaper *and* more effective: a remedy that adds weight must argue that weight, and this one could not.
- **Conditioning the exclusion bullet's *"never a required part"***. The remedy writes a second copy of the new rule one line above it, against the charter's own drift rule. Recorded instead.
- **Folding the withholding into the wait paragraph's opening sentence.** Priced by two seats at a net reduction; the arithmetic omitted the rewrite it forces on the following paragraph, whose anaphor it orphans, and it deletes the one bolded lead-in a measured consumer quoted back as its grounds.
- **Amending the cell's Success clause.** It is a comprehension criterion, not an enumeration of what a brief must contain, and a brief carrying rulings satisfies it unchanged.

## Known limits

**The withholding is measured on two consumers, not a population.** Against a baseline where five of six built the artifact, two runs — one before the fix batch and one after — stopped and could name the sentence. That is the first evidence in either direction, and it is not a measurement.

**The panel's most serious finding was wrong, and the defense is what showed it.** `cold-read` filed two sole highs and lost both: one needs a reader who reaches `:92`, the other a reader who stops before `:90`, and `:92`'s anaphor has no antecedent but `:90` — disjoint populations. The seat still originated the finding real use confirmed.

**Three figure errors landed on this change, all in supporting clauses beside a load-bearing figure that was checked.** The author's size-ceiling claim, corrected on #373 before the artifact was written; the author's base body figure, wrong by one; and then two seats reporting that inherited figure as their own measurement, while the terminal stage's own first pass failed the same way through an unstated cp1252 decode. The standing correction — a seat reporting a figure names the command and the convention that produced it — is in the review report rather than a retired lens, because the failure crossed two lenses, the defense's subject and the judge.

**The review's assignment omitted two recorded findings it should have carried** — both about this cell's net growth, on a change whose largest merged finding was net growth. No seat met them; the external pass caught it.

**The second experience session's sharpest result came from an accident.** Its board inherited the first round's comment, so the consumer met a brief attributing to the owner words he had not said. It caught the fabrication using the clause the external pass had just forced into the batch. No arm designed for that run would have tested it, and the note says so.
