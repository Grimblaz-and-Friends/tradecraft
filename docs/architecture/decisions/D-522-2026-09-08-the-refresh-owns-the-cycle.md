# D-522 — The refresh owns the assessment cycle, and the note carries the answers rather than the argument

**Landed by** [PR #522](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/pull/522), closing [#486](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/486). Brief affirmed 2026-09-07 at [issue #486 comment](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/486#issuecomment-5575563963); artifact at [comment 5594780958](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/486#issuecomment-5594780958). Evidence pinned at `cf01b0b` unless stated.

## What was decided

**The board refresh is where the pool's assessment cycle runs**, and the refresh note is where its answers land. Two additions to `docs/cells/board/SKILL.md`, this repository's own repo-only cell. The `filing` cell ships the pool, `cycle`, and the assessment rules to every adopter and says a repository decides where the *push* runs; it says nothing about where `cycle` runs, so nothing claimed this ground before. The refresh is the moment the owner already triggers — *"I'm triggering a refresh after every round of development, usually 2 or 3 PRs in parallel, or when I need new direction on what to pickup. I'll automate when it's a better fit."* — so the asking goes there rather than into a second job nobody owns. That siting argument lived in the cell body until this change's review ruled it rationale and moved it here.

**The two questions [#486](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/486) deferred, settled.**

**How many a refresh assesses is the policy's, and the cell states no number.** `assessment.per_cycle` in `skills/filing/scripts/pool-policy.json` is 5 and `cmd_cycle` slices the unassessed list by it. A second copy of that number in the board cell would give one number two homes. **What the artifact got wrong, and the review caught: there are two keys, not one.** `cmd_shortlist` gates on `assessment.before_shortlist`, a different key, also 5. At equal values on one rating-sorted pool the cycle's five are a superset of the gate's blockers, so answering them clears the gate; lower `per_cycle` alone — exactly the re-pacing the original sentence invited — and the gate outlives the cycle, refusing on items the cycle never names. Probed at `per_cycle=2, before_shortlist=40`: `cycle` names 2, `shortlist` refuses on 17. The shipped sentence now names both keys.

**What the note owes when a refresh cannot reach them all: the count answered, each answer, and which were left.** The first draft said it owed only *which it left*, on the argument that an unanswered assessment is marked by nothing and so returns, where an abandoned crossing is marked by `raise` and goes quiet. **That argument is false and the review disproved it.** `cmd_pushed` writes nothing; only `cmd_raise`/`cmd_unraise` write the label, and `cmd_raise` runs after the ask exists — its own docstring records print-and-mark as *rejected* precisely so that "a refresh abandoned halfway would silence items no ask was ever put for". An uncarried crossing returns exactly as an unanswered assessment does; there was no asymmetry. In this repository the `raised` label has never been created, so `raise` refuses outright. **The real distinction is between the question and the answer:** the question returns for free, and the reading that produced the answer does not — `read_pool` derives `assessed` and stores nothing, `cmd_assess` writes one label and no reasoning, and there is no third store. An experience session found this from use: its consumer read five issues, worked out that #268 belongs under #487 and that four others are their own causes, was blocked from writing, and observed that the reasoning was gone. So the note carries the answers, because the note is the only surface a partial refresh can write to.

**The reach line and the drift look do not merge.** The drift look is an observation over what closed, derived from `gh issue list`; the reach line is a measurement over prose in the working tree, derived from `python tools/lint.py`. One heading over two derivations would leave a reader unable to tell which command produced which half.

**The reach line's scope is stated, because the figure is narrower than it looks.** The charter's pointer-reach row covers the charter and the eight shipped cells it reaches. This repository's always-on doctrine, `AGENTS.md`, additionally routes every session to four repo-only cells — `board`, `landing`, `records`, `siting` — totalling 39,130 characters at `cf01b0b`, 14.0% of the 278,886 a session here can actually reach. **The change was its own demonstration:** the pre-fix draft added 1,842 characters to `board` and the charter row did not move by one. (After the fix batch the cell lands +903 and the row does move, to 239,943 — because that batch also touched a shipped cell, which is the only reason it moves.) All five review seats reached this independently and the experience session reached it from use. The line now says what it covers and what it does not.

## What was rejected

**Reporting the repo-only cells' reach beside it.** It measures what a reader of these notes actually wants, and it needs `tools/figures.py`, a new row in the printed block, and its tests. The review ruled this the session's call rather than the owner's — the affirmed brief excludes exactly two things, the baseline pass and automation, and `tools/figures.py` was excluded only by the artifact's own boundary, which is the session's to cross. It is filed rather than built, so the values argument can be had over it on its own.

**Adding a filing to the refresh trigger list.** A filing is what puts an unassessed item in the pool, and `docs/cells/board/SKILL.md`'s trigger list ends *"A filing no longer moves it."* — so the only run of `cycle` is routed through a list excluding the event that creates its work. Reversing that sentence undoes a settled decision on the same line, so it is filed as a design call rather than taken here. The consuming half of the same finding was disproved: `tools/board.py` prints the shortlist command, the gate's refusal and its escape at the moment a session runs out of board, so that end self-services.

**Raising `assessment.per_cycle` because the pacing looks slower than arrivals.** A seat computed 24 refreshes × 5 = 120 against 136 arrivals and concluded the backlog grows. The judge re-derived it: only 92 of those 137 issues are open, so 120 > 92 and the backlog shrinks at compliance. The seat's denominator counted issues that never needed assessing.

## What this change got wrong before the review, recorded because the cell no longer says it

Three load-bearing claims shipped false, and all three were true when [#486](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/486) was filed on 2026-09-07 and false by the time they were written. They were carried out of the issue body without being re-derived.

- **"the shortlist refuses over an unassessed top, so its first move is blocked."** `python skills/filing/scripts/pool.py shortlist` exits 0 and raises five. The gate reads only the top of the pool, and the top five had been cleared by sub-issue links in the two refreshes since the filing. `--unassessed` is a second, independent falsifier the same cell already stated fourteen lines above.
- **"nothing a reader of these notes sees carries it."** The refresh note of 2026-09-08T21:36:53Z carries the charter's reach row, its figure and its command, 4h44m before the commit that denied it. The bullet then told the first refresher under the rule to write *"no predecessor"* and discard it.
- **"`raise` marks a crossing, so one read and not carried through goes quiet."** Above.

The shape is one shape: a supporting clause copied from an issue and not re-run. It is recorded here rather than only in the review report because the next session to revise this paragraph is the one that needs it.
