# D-825 — Convergence routes to the design form

**Purpose:** preserve why the owner chose a route-level design instruction over charter text and kept the instance-or-cause choice open. **Audience:** a future session changing convergence guidance or the brief's design and exception passages. **Success:** that session can recover the chosen route, its rejected alternative and its limits without re-deriving them.

Records [#769's affirmed brief](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/769#issuecomment-5960052915), [settled artifact](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/769#issuecomment-5960222233) and [holder reading](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/769#issuecomment-5960227172).

## Choice and meaning changes

At convergence, the entrance's guidance sends the holder to the design form before the brief's output form: design with the owner in turns, one decision per turn and starting from the problem's cause; bring the whole item only when the next turn would add execution detail. The target is `skills/engagement/references/design-sitting.md` at `a6394866dc0d0783bcababc9414f3b018e63d52d`. The direct brief-form route names the form its first turn takes. These routing additions answer the failure to reach the conversation method before composing the agreed item.

An instance-or-cause choice is opened with the owner as a turn even when the session has a recommendation, carrying that recommendation and the strongest case against it. It becomes an implementation-brief row with its reason only after the owner rules, because a recommendation does not settle an owner choice.

The implementation evidence is `BRIEF_GUIDANCE` and its `convergence` return in `lib/work.py` at `a6394866dc0d0783bcababc9414f3b018e63d52d`, and the “Designing, putting, and recording the item” paragraph in `skills/engagement/references/the-brief.md` at `a6394866dc0d0783bcababc9414f3b018e63d52d`. `test_missing_affirmation_routes_to_the_form_and_presence_check` in `lib/tests/test_work.py` at `a6394866dc0d0783bcababc9414f3b018e63d52d` demonstrates the returned wording, design-before-output path order, retained whole-item fields and presence-only check command.

## Cause and rejected alternative

The [filing](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/769) and affirmed brief record the cause: the turn form was reachable only through the engagement cell's routing table, which the entrance bypassed by sending the holder directly to the output form. The routing table is in `skills/engagement/SKILL.md` at `a6394866dc0d0783bcababc9414f3b018e63d52d`. The two instances were [Organizations of Verra #440](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/Organizations-of-Verra/issues/440) on 2026-09-27, where four forks arrived together with recommendations already made, and [tradecraft #783](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/783) on 2026-10-02, where the instance-only exception was put as a recommended row before the owner shaped it in a turn.

The owner chose A, pointing the entrance and brief form at the design form, over B, writing the turn rule into charter §3. Methodology stays with the component that teaches it, and no always-on text is added for adopters. The case against A was reach: a session that never runs the entrance still meets only charter §3, which is silent on turns; that section is in `skills/charter/SKILL.md` at `a6394866dc0d0783bcababc9414f3b018e63d52d`. The owner accepted the route-level fix with that case against it rather than widening the charter.
