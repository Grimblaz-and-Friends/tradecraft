# D-261: A rule holds when its compliance is visible on the artifact its reader is producing — the standard, the values citation moved onto the record entry, and an evidence-gated deletion in the outflow

**Status:** Accepted 2026-08-30 (PR #261)

## Context

[#256](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/256) carries the measurement and its per-rule derivations: twenty mechanically-checkable rules of this repository's own, measured over every PR merged into `main`, each re-derivable by a check over `git log`/`git show` on the squash commits plus the GitHub API where the trace is a comment. The distribution is bimodal, and the four explanations a reader reaches for first are all falsified there. **Enforcement does not separate the bands** — the purpose header holds at every governing document at every PR with nothing checking it, while the CI-blocking version bump has the worst first-pass rate measured. **Neither age nor surface separates them** — `AGENTS.md` carries both a rule at full compliance and the rule at zero, four paragraphs apart, and the `engagement` cell carries one rule whose two halves were written in the same sentence-neighbourhood on the same day and measure far apart.

What separates them is the **compliance mode**: whether satisfying the rule is a visible property of the artifact the reader is producing, or a judgment they must resolve against a document that is not in front of them. The first band sits at full compliance with no enforcement; the second is the weakest, and the one rule at zero sits in it.

**The measurement's limit is survivorship** — it sees merged work only, so every rate is an upper bound on what sessions actually do. That is why the standard this entry lands claims the *separation* and never a rate, and why no figure from the sweep is written into shipped prose.

## Decision

**1. The standard goes in `authoring`'s Writing standards, stated as a preference with a conversion move.** Prefer the rule whose compliance is visible on the artifact its reader is producing; where only the remembering form can carry the rule, give it a carrier the writer already has open — a key in the record being appended, a line in the template being filled. The conversion clause is the load-bearing half: a bare preference would be unactionable for the many rules that cannot be expressed as a visible property on their own, and the two repairs below are both instances of it.

**It landed in the body rather than in `skills/authoring/references/routing.md`, revising the placement reason the affirmed artifact recorded.** That artifact argued the body on the ground that the trigger is every rule-writing firing. Measuring the body against its cap made the choice cost something, so it was re-examined and the ground changed rather than the answer: routing depth loads when the home is the question or an always-on surface is edited, and **a session revising a rule already sited in a cell fires neither**. That gap is exactly what the standard's own falsifier probes for, so placing the standard behind a load condition it can miss would have shipped the failure it describes. The reason is recorded because it is a session call the artifact had argued the other way round.

**2. The values ranking's citation moves onto the record entry.** Every entry in this repository's findings record is by construction a worth-it judgment the evidence did not settle — the entries say so in their own words — and none cites the ranking the doctrine sends them to, though every one of them postdates that rule. The `record` ruling's entry shape in `adversarial-review` gains a `value` key beside `artifact`, `finding` and `promotion`, so the demand is met by filling in the entry a session is already writing rather than by remembering to open a document.

**[D-225] declined the analogous mechanism in `filing` and recorded it as available "on an actual sighting"; the measured zero is that sighting.** It is measured non-compliance from real work rather than a review finding about governing prose, which the charter excludes as an incident. Three of D-225's four grounds do not transfer to this site and the fourth is answered: the key ships **path-free**, so the zone wall is satisfied and an adopter is never sent to a `docs/` path; and where a repository declares no ranking the key is **omitted, its absence claiming nothing** — D-225's own clause, restated here because a mandatory-but-patchy tag whose absence carried meaning is the failure that ruling was built to avoid.

**No guard enforces the key, deliberately.** The record is append-only and this repository's doctrine forbids repairing it, so a guard over its rows would red the lint on a file no one may fix. The visible form is the whole mechanism, which is the standard above applied to its own first case.

**3. The outflow gains a fourth answer: deletion, gated on evidence.** [D-184] forbade the outflow to drop a rule. Since it landed, the always-on surface it governs rose across every commit that touched it, and of the commits that made a governing surface smaller, most grew the governing tree in the same commit because the shrinkage was relocation — the failure mode D-184 itself diagnosed in its predecessor. An instrument that can only route scores as reduction locally and growth globally.

Deletion is lawful **against evidence that the rule does not bind** — measured non-compliance, a premise it rested on since falsified, a mechanism that has superseded it — and that evidence is named with the deletion, where amendments are recorded. **The gate is the whole of the permission.** Ungated, the line that leaves is the cheapest one rather than the one that has earned it, and the outflow is owed on every always-on edit under a deliberately tight ceiling, which is precisely the pressure that would select for cheapness. The three relocation moves and the whole-surface measurement rule are unchanged.

**4. The addition was paid for by a move, not by a smaller addition.** `authoring`'s body was near its cap at the merge base — `python tools/figures.py --cell skills/authoring/SKILL.md --cell-budget 7359` prices it on whatever tree it is run on, and `76b29e3` is the base here. [D-253] landed the rule that a ceiling reached is a trigger for the moves and never a reason to shrink what is being added, so *What a cell carries* sheds whole to `skills/authoring/references/cell-structure.md` behind a pointer. Its three bullets are needed when a cell is created, restructured, or its description written, and by no other firing of this cell — the disqualifier the depth rule states, applied to the section that most clearly meets it. **The shed frees more than the addition needed**, and the section was not split to free exactly enough: it is one unit with one load condition, and a partial move would leave a heading promising bullets that had gone.

**5. No always-on surface is edited, so no outflow is owed.** `python tools/figures.py` reports the total on any tree; it is unchanged between `76b29e3` and this change's tip. **`AGENTS.md`'s pointer was considered and left byte-identical.** It reads *the `authoring` cell carries the three moves and what they may not do*, and both halves survive this change: the cell still carries three relocation moves, deletion being the answer that is not one, and what they may not do — delete without evidence, delete silently — is still carried. The line points at the cell; it never stated the never-drop rule itself, so it primes no belief this change falsifies. Recorded because a later reader will reach that sentence expecting it to have moved.

## Rejected

- **Rewriting the doctrine's Worth doing line alone** (fork A, option 2, owner-ruled). Cheapest diff and no shipped-surface entanglement, and it would have left the rule in the remembering form the measurement caught at zero — landing a standard and violating it in the same change.
- **Unconditional deletion in the outflow** (fork B, option 2, owner-ruled). Simplest, and consistent with this cell's existing *deletion is a first-class edit*; it discards the guard the never-drop clause was built as, and leaves a wrongly deleted rule no trail but one line.
- **Leaving the never-drop clause as it was** (fork B, option 3, owner-ruled). The measured consequence is the argument against it.
- **Putting the standard in `skills/authoring/references/routing.md`**, beside the admission order it refines. The load-condition gap above.
- **Editing `AGENTS.md`'s pointer to name the gate.** A zero-net swap was drafted — *what they may not do* for a clause naming the evidence deletion needs — and rejected: the sentence is true as it stands, and an always-on edit owes an outflow, which would have bought an audit of that file to fund an accuracy repair that was not needed. The trade is worth recording because the edit looked free and was not.
- **A guard over the `value` key.** Reason above.
- **Shipping any rate from the sweep.** Survivorship makes every one an upper bound, and a frozen number would outlive the tree that produced it.
