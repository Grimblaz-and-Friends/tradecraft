# D-345: A routing criterion covers text that keeps a reader away, where reach is the quantity least likely to survive

**Status:** Accepted 2026-09-03 (PR #345)

**Evidence.** Everything quoted from a live file is pinned to this change's base `ed5a524`, the tip of `main` the branch was cut from. The measurements this entry reasons from were made by [D-320](D-320-2026-09-02-the-fences-are-measured-and-nearly-all-of-them-are-live.md)'s fence run and are not re-run here; that entry's own evidence exposure — its figures resting on GitHub comments no in-repository reader can check — is inherited and is [#324](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/324). The brief and its affirmation are on [#339](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/339#issuecomment-5531637883); the settled artifact is [there too](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/339#issuecomment-5531866100). ## The condition

[D-338] landed the routing-criterion bullet earlier the same day — `git log --date=iso` on `ed5a524`, against this entry's own Status line — and left it positive-polarity throughout. Its subject was *"A criterion claiming a piece of text routes a reader"*, its quantities were *reaches the destination* and a *stated reason*, and its two enumerated reach-defeaters were both destination-shaped. That entry records the gap as sustained and not fixed, in its *What the review changed* section: *"The bullet is silent on routing *away* — a fence clause claims the mirror, and [D-320]'s disqualifier for that polarity … ships in no cell — which needs a design call and goes to the board."*

**The mirror is the polarity this repository has actually measured.** D-320's run tested twenty-one exclusion clauses across thirty seats. The run that produced the positive rule tested one clause across six — a comparison that motivates this change without carrying it, since D-338 also rests on #255's five-arm harness, and it was that second run rather than the first that decided its central judgement.

**And D-320 reached D-338's own thesis first, in this polarity, without generalising it:** *"The measure is the warrant, not the outcome … An instrument built on reach would have reported every clause dead whether or not it worked, which is the reading #272 exists to forbid."*

Half of what [#339](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/339) was filed about is not here, because it was discharged before this change started. The citation disqualifier the filing said shipped in no cell now ships in `skills/spikes/references/licensing-a-deletion.md`, landed by [D-337](D-337-2026-09-03-the-instrument-rules-move-to-the-cell.md) hours after the filing. The filer posted the correction themselves; this change acts on the remaining limb only.

## The decision

**One bullet, widened, and no second one.**

**Its subject covers both polarities** — a criterion claiming a piece of text *moves a reader, toward a destination or away from one* — and the two quantities are restated so they read in both: that the reader **goes where the text says**, or that the reader's **stated reason** names the text. **The first quantity carries an inline gloss — *which is its reach* — added in review**, and it is the only definition of that term anywhere in `skills/`: the base glossed it as *reaches the destination*, which is flatly wrong in this polarity, and the restatement that fixed the polarity severed the label from its anchor. Twenty-four characters, recorded here because this cell is under standing pressure to shed them and a later size pass would otherwise find no reason for it.

**A third route joins the enumeration, labelled as reasoned rather than measured: the reader's own indifference.** It applies only to text that keeps a reader away, and there it is the ordinary case. **The label is not decoration.** D-320 isolates no decline attributable to indifference as distinct from the destination's own description: its only per-clause-resolution decline is attributed to route 1 outright, and its screen's non-openings carry the very confound that entry records against #217 — *"it could not say whether the fence caused the correct route or the description's opening summary did."* So the mechanism is sound and the empirical predicate the other two carry is not available to it, and the bullet says which is which. This was the review's finding, not the drafting session's. A reader with no pull toward that destination declines it whether or not the text is there, and the situations that test such text at all are near misses, where the pull is weakest by construction. So reach is the quantity least likely to survive there.

**Least likely, and not unavailable** — which is the clause a later session is most likely to flatten. D-320 records **five clauses that changed conduct and not merely warrant**, seats declining the cell with the fence present and opening it without, of which the fence-free arm's **four of twenty** informative near-misses is the screen rate that four of those five are read through — one result and its restatement rather than two, which the first draft of this entry presented as additive and a review corrected. The conclusion survives on the non-overlapping part alone: `spikes` #0 is confirmed at clause resolution, on arms differing in that clause alone, and one such instance falsifies an absolute. A rule saying reach is simply unavailable in this polarity would be falsified by this repository's own record, so the rule is stated as a comparative and the record that forbids the absolute is why.

**Route 1's wording widens with it**, in two moves rather than one: the head noun goes from *the destination's own summary sentence* to *its description* — the container the enumeration needs once clauses count — and the qualifier goes from positional (*sitting above*) to functional (*settling the situation*), which is the load-bearing half. A review's first round shipped *above the trigger under test* alongside the widening; that position clause is dropped, because once another clause counts, a clause sitting *below* the trigger masks reach exactly as well. **D-320's one licensed deletion is route 1 measured in the mirror**: on that clause's own confirm run all six seats declined the cell, and every one gave a cell-specific reason naming that cell's **own positive trigger, four clauses above the one under test**, resolving it on timing rather than on topic and identically in both arms. The enumeration is therefore polarity-neutral in its first two members, and stating otherwise would have understated what has been measured against it. Route 2 is present in the mirror as well, though as a residual rather than a measured defeater — D-320 records the charter body carried unablated in every arm while naming four of the nine cells, and flags it as **structural** for [#303](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/303) rather than residual. That status is carried as that entry states it and is not resolved here, and the bullet now says so rather than calling both routes measured in this polarity.

**Route 1 is therefore wider than the warrant sentence beside it**, which still defeats warrant only on *the destination's name or summary sentence*. That asymmetry is deliberate and rests on evidence: D-320 records reconstruction *"from the cell name alone"* and no measured instance of a non-name, non-summary clause defeating warrant. It is recorded here rather than stated on the shipped surface, which would cost characters on the largest unbudgeted body for a reading nobody has observed going wrong.

**The closing warrant-failure sentence is unchanged**, because it already read in both polarities and D-320 records that failure mode in this one directly: *"a clause whose substance a seat can reconstruct from the cell name alone can never clear this instrument."*

## `engagement` does not name `spikes`, and that is the load-bearing placement call

The obvious remedy was for the widened bullet to point the criterion-writer at `skills/spikes/references/licensing-a-deletion.md`, where the citation disqualifier now lives. **`skills/authoring/references/cell-structure.md` refuses it**: a cell names no sibling, the charter is the only live exception here — the one-way repo-only exception is empty, both cells shipping — and the reserved reference form is a cell name rather than a path. A cold seat found this, and `tools/lint.py` catches it too — in `check_sideways_deps`, which reports both the path form and the cell-name form at the very line this change edits. The first draft of this entry claimed the lint did not, naming `check_cell_references`, which is the wrong function: that one enforces existence and the shipped/repo-only wall. A review probed both forms and the claim is withdrawn. Nothing here rests on the guard being blind; the standard is what refuses the pointer.

That standard names two remedies for a standard two cells need, and **both are declined with reasons rather than foreclosed**. Routing it up to the charter is refused because the charter body is the adopter surface #303 exists to shrink, and paying for this there is the most expensive character in the practice. Giving it one owning cell is what already holds: `spikes` owns it since D-337, and this change leaves it whole there.

**What survives is that `engagement` needs no copy.** The disqualifier is a rule for scoring a run; the criterion-writer's moment ends at naming which quantity is claimed and what defeats it, and the third route supplies exactly that in `engagement`'s own terms. A session that goes on to *run* the check reaches `spikes` by that cell's own trigger.

## What was rejected

**A second bullet for the mirror polarity.** It duplicates the closing warrant-failure sentence, which transfers unchanged, at a cost this entry first stated as roughly double and cannot derive — the transferring warrant sentence is about 240 characters against the 721 this change actually spent, so nearer 1.2x than 2x — in the cell with the largest body and no budget. Put to the owner as option C; not ruled.

**Leaving the bullet alone and routing the criterion-writer to `spikes`'s reference.** Cheapest in the cell that can least afford characters, and the substance is already written there. Rejected on its load condition: that file fires when a run is being *scored* or text *deleted*, not when a criterion is being written. **A second ground this entry first stated does not hold and is withdrawn** — that the claim *"a criterion-writer reaches `spikes`"* is an unfalsifiable reach claim because `engagement`'s body routes there. `grep -n "spikes" skills/engagement/SKILL.md` returns nothing: the body names the *method* at `:69`, unbackticked, not the cell in the reserved form this entry leans on three paragraphs earlier. And the rule was quoted with its own exception amputated — *or where what does routes it too weakly to mask the difference* — which a route through an unbackticked method word two paragraphs down is the paradigm case of. A review found both. Put to the owner as option B; not ruled. The sibling-naming standard above then made it unlawful as well, which is a second and later ground rather than the one it was declined on.

**Generalising further, to any criterion about reader behaviour.** D-338 declined this on the ground that nothing was measured about criteria of other shapes, and that ground still holds for shapes other than the mirror. The mirror is not another shape — it is routing, in the other polarity, and it is measured — which is what made this a filing rather than a scope note, and what keeps the widening from being the generalisation D-338 refused.

**A run to isolate the third route.** The mechanism is reasoned and the bullet says so rather than claiming it measured, which is the honest form available without a run; isolating indifference from the destination's own description is not this change's, and D-320 did not do it. A run built to re-confirm what that entry already reports would spend seats on a settled reading.

## The placement call, and what it cost

**The bullet stays in the body**, on D-338's own reasoning: the list's other items are all in the body, and an instance separated from the rule it instantiates is one a reader meets without the rule that explains it. Moving the list behind a pointer is a restructure of the cell and was in neither change's boundary.

**What it cost.** The `engagement` cell-body row of `python tools/lint.py`, run from the repository root at this change's base `ed5a524` and at its head. The cell was already first of thirteen and carries no body budget; recorded findings decline fixes on it for size, and this entry is on the record as having grown it anyway. **No always-on row moved** — the change is in a cell body, not a description.

## What the cold seat changed

Three seats, each a fresh dispatch, two adverse then `would`, against `ed5a524`, which every seat confirmed on its first read. The text the third judged was the artifact as inlined in its dispatch, LF-normalised, identified to it by sha256 `99b500db64380499180fd95fa339aaaf7bf232334b8a116fd05189bdcb284f1e` over 13,398 bytes — `sha256` and a byte count over those bytes, computed by the dispatching session and transcribed back by the seat, which is what `skills/engagement/references/cold-seat.md` asks the verdict to name. An external reviewer asked for that provenance; nothing between the two would detect a truncated inline artifact, which is a standing recorded finding against that file and not this change's to close.

**Round 1 landed on five points**, and two of them changed the rule rather than the document. The sibling-naming breach above is the first. The second is that the draft's asymmetry claim was **unconditional where its premise was conditional**, asserted against a contrary datum in the very entry the draft said it had consulted in full — D-320's five conduct-changing clauses, of which the draft carried only the supporting half. That is what produced the comparative.

**Round 2 landed on two**, and they are the reason route 1's wording moved. The draft claimed the two enumerated routes keep their positive-polarity scope on the ground that *"nothing measured says they defeat the mirror"*; the seat answered that D-320's one licensed deletion **is** that measurement. An acceptance criterion had been written whose failure condition would have scored that measured answer as failing.

**One figure was asserted falsely and is retracted here.** An early draft stated *"four recorded findings decline fixes on that cell for size."* The number was never derived. **The first draft's account of why was itself wrong on all three of its limbs, and a review falsified each**: it was carried from D-338, which states it of *the same cell and the same question*, not a different one; not from that entry's closing section but from *What the review changed*; and the difference in tree accounts for nothing, the same query — entries whose `artifact` names `engagement` and whose `finding` matches `largest cell body|largest body|body already|unbudgeted growth|net growth|pooled budget|budgeted cap|for size` over `docs/recorded-findings.jsonl` — returning **eight** at D-338's own base `70d59b7`, at `ed5a524` and at this branch's head alike. The figure was already wrong where it was first stated. What is left is the plain fact: nobody ran the query, in either entry. The claim is withdrawn; the artifact names no count there, and neither does this entry.

**Two loose statements the round-3 seat raised and declined to land on** are recorded rather than repaired, editing either having bought a fourth round for nothing the reading turned on: the one-clause-across-six comparison understates D-338's evidence base, which rests on two runs; and the artifact's *"two edits"* undercounts what it then specifies as three, route 1's widening being the third.
