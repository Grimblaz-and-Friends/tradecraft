# D-414: The conversation that produces a brief has a shape, and three of its habits qualify every ask

**Status:** Accepted 2026-09-05 (PR #414)

## Context

[#402](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/402) is aspect 8 of [#391](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/391)'s interaction map, whose thesis is that `skills/engagement/SKILL.md` governs the two *endpoints* of the owner interaction — the brief's lock, the artifact, the cold seat — and says nothing about the stretch that produces them. [#401](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/401) and [#403](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/403) took aspects 1 and 7 at the brief's first line, in [D-410]. This takes the conversation itself.

**What the owner was reacting to**, in his own words on 2026-09-05. Most common: *"you solve for me, leaving nothing for me."* Most painful: *"you force me to type things where I have no value to add, or I have to type it all myself."* On a recommendation: *"Without some version of pros and cons, it's just you telling me what to do."* On what went well: *"getting something actually built out was a delightful addition that I've rarely seen natively from an agent without me specifically asking for it, but it brought clarity to the situation much more quickly."*

**The rhythm was run before it was written.** On 2026-09-05 the session and the owner ran a structured version of a design conversation on a real question, testing each of its eight steps as they went — *"This is a great framework. Let's test out our rhythm while exploring each step to be sure we like it,"* and after the test, *"Now we're doing much better."* Two of the eight carry corrections that sitting's own failures forced: the case against a recommendation fired against the owner's framing and not against what the session was proposing, and the session marked things settled on its own inference.

**Nothing in the tree competed.** `grep -rn -iE "sitting|design conversation|shaping|ledger of|altitude|diverge"` over `skills/`, `docs/cells/`, `AGENTS.md`, `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/values.md` at `9b622fc` returned four hits, none about running a conversation with the owner. `docs/north-star/flow.md`'s *"Shaping, not ratification"* describes the ideal and binds nothing by its own statement.

## Decision

**1. The rhythm, the triggers and the close go behind a load condition; the three habits go in the body.** The eight steps and the trigger rule fire only when a session is deciding whether to open a sitting or running one, which is the `authoring` cell's disqualifier applied in the direction it actually runs — the many firings of this cell that never reach a sitting would load prose they had no use for. The three habits qualify *every* ask, sitting or not, which is the affirmed brief's own words, so putting them behind the load condition would make them fire only when a sitting opens — the opposite of what was settled.

**2. The trigger list is the brief's two, closed, and the invented-shape tell is a recogniser rather than a trigger.** The affirmed brief closes with *only*: more than one call in the brief is the owner's, or he opened the work as a design question. The filing on #402 recorded **three** tells "any one sufficient", the third being a shape the session invented with nothing in the record ruling it. **A cold seat's first verdict was `would not` on exactly that**: the third fires on a change carrying one fork, which the file's own *not for a change with one fork* forbade two lines later, and which the brief's *only* excludes. Where the affirmed brief and the filing conflict, the brief governs — this cell's own rule. The content survives as the recogniser it actually is: an invented shape is where a session miscounts the owner's calls as one, so the file says count the forks inside it and says outright that finding one is still one.

**3. Step 4 ships though the brief's rhythm sentence does not name it, and it was narrowed on the way.** That sentence enumerates without a closing word, so the brief is silent there rather than contrary, and the owner ran and affirmed all eight steps — the brief itself says *"tested it step by step on a real question."* Its absence from the plain-English condensation is consistent with that brief's own bar against a term the owner would have to look up, *altitude* being one. **As run it obliged both parties to name their altitude; as shipped it obliges only the session**, a proper subset of what he affirmed, because a session-facing cell purporting to bind the owner is a rule with no reader who can be held to it. **The first artifact claimed no step added an obligation the brief's enumeration does not carry, and that was false of step 4**; the claim was replaced by this reasoning rather than repaired.

**4. Habit 3 owns the rule about whose words a ruling is kept in, and the re-put bullet's trailing clause is deleted rather than left standing beside it.** The base bullet ended *"— their own words where those carry it, a restatement they affirm where they do not, since that is the sentence a later session is held to."* The `authoring` cell forbids two half-owners of one sentence kept in agreement by hand, and the drift is not hypothetical: the owner amended this exact rule the day it landed, and with two sites an amendment lands on one. The rule's reason travels with it. The route check that rule asks for is one hop inside one file — a session re-putting a brief has this cell's body loaded, and habit 3 sits in it — so no load condition, pointer or description had to widen.

**5. Two meaning changes ride in that move, and they are recorded here because the pull request first described the move as relocation plus a lock.** Both were sustained findings of this change's own review.

- **A condition was dropped.** The base rule was a conditional preference — the owner's own words are mandatory *where those carry it*, and a restatement is lawful only *where they do not*. Habit 3 is a bare disjunction: *"in their own words, or in the one restatement of those words they approved."* Under the shipped rule a session may take the restatement route even where his words carry it, provided he approves the restatement once. **This is faithful to the affirmed brief**, which is itself disjunctive, and to his amendment; what was wrong was calling it a relocation.
- **The rule widened from one document to any record of that ruling.** The base clause hung off *"each ruling written in as settled"* inside the re-put brief. Habit 3's *"is recorded"* reaches the work's issue, the pull request body, a decision entry and a review report — the surfaces this cell enumerates — for a ruling given in answer to an ask. It does **not** reach every record in the practice: the paragraph's lead-in is an explicit scope sentence, whose *"whether or not the work opened a design sitting"* clause exists only to do scope work.

**6. The owner's locked restatement lives in the affirmed brief and not in the cell, and that is deliberate.** His amendment on restating his words: *"there are times when it's better for you to rephrase my words… if this is done, it must be run by me for approval so you can then lock your rephrase of my words exactly the way your presented them to me… once you do and I approve it, you can use them as if they were my words, but they still must be kept exactly as approved in that case (1 rephrase, not continual)."* The restatement presented and approved under it, locked exactly as presented: **"Settled means your own words, or one restatement of them that you approved; once approved it is kept exactly and is never reworded again."** His approval: *"Approved, lock it and post the brief."* That sentence is second-person owner-facing text; the cell body is third-person session-facing prose, so shipping it verbatim into `SKILL.md:40` was never available. It is carried here with its provenance so a later session can find the locked wording rather than reconstructing it from the rule.

**7. Placement of the sitting pointer: after the systemic-cause paragraph, not where the issue's placement reading put it.** [D-410] landed the why-chain as its own paragraph, and the sitting's decision procedure runs that chain **first**, so a pointer above it would put the two in the wrong order for a session reading straight down. The chosen slot keeps the drafting run together — settle the brief, find the cause, and where it still cannot be written, sit — and leaves *"Then the artifact is written"* reading as the step after all of them. **The reason the artifact and the pull request first gave for setting the reading aside was false:** both said the reading predated #401 landing. It was posted at `2026-09-05T18:08:34Z` and PR #410 merged at `16:41:07Z` — **87 minutes earlier**, into a tree that already carried the paragraph. The substantive ordering argument is untouched; the basis was corrected under review.

**8. The pointer was rewritten twice, and the second rewrite is a net shed.** The first shipped text paraphrased trigger 1 as a conjunction — *the shape is settled by nothing already written **and** settling it is theirs more than once* — which is neither what the file states nor what the brief states, both requiring only the fork count. **An experience session then watched a cold consumer decline to open a sitting without ever loading the file that says when one opens**, deciding from that sentence alone; its decision was correct, which is what made it the finding. The response, a clause saying the sentence was not the rule, was **the wrong remedy**: the `authoring` cell's answer to a sentence a competent reader could go wrong on is to restate it, never to append a qualifier, and the clause was additionally false of the sentence containing it. The review's repair states trigger 1 as the fork count, states trigger 2 directly, and restores the file's own load condition — *and while running one*, whose omission meant a session **running** a sitting was told the file was not for it, the file being reachable by exactly one pointer in the tree. **605 → 496 characters.**

**9. The ledger's contradiction anecdote is recorded here rather than shipped.** Step 5 first carried *"in the sitting it caught a contradiction in the session's own design one turn after it was made."* That is evidence, whose home under the `authoring` cell is behind a link and whose home under the charter's *Where content goes* is a decision entry; the file's two other incidents are linked and this one was not. It is true and it is the strongest single piece of evidence that a visible ledger earns its place, which is why it is kept here and not merely deleted.

**10. No guard is built, either polarity.** Nothing this change ships is mechanically checkable without a judgement about a conversation. The `authoring` cell's preference for a rule whose compliance is visible on the artifact its reader is producing is satisfied by the ledger itself, which is an artifact the session is producing anyway.

## Rejected

- **A third sufficient trigger**, per decision 2 — and CodeRabbit's external comment asked for exactly that, to make the owner's ask a distinct third trigger and delete the *"nothing else"* wording. Rejected on the affirmed brief's *only*: the owner's ask is binding under the charter's Authority section without a trigger of its own, and the sentence claiming it was trigger 2 restated was deleted instead.
- **Naming the why-chain in the cell body** at the systemic-cause paragraph, which is the site the review's judge preferred for making the term resolvable. That paragraph is [D-410]'s owner-affirmed text, affirmed on sight and not this change's to edit; the term was removed from the sitting file instead, which achieves the same end and sheds rather than adds. Recorded because it is a departure from a terminal ruling's stated site, taken for a reason that ruling did not have.
- **Rewriting the cell's Success line** to reach the sitting and the habits. Sustained by the review and sent to the board instead: it is the third recorded recurrence on that line, rewriting it changes what every review of the cell judges, and two prior stages declined it on exactly that price.
- **An orphan check for `references/` files.** The tree cannot notice a depth file becoming unreachable — probed by deleting the pointer line, with `lint` and `roster` both green and the file stranded — but the same probe at the base revision does the same to a pre-existing file, and ten of sixteen `references/` files are reachable by exactly one pointer. Tree-wide and pre-existing; one guard closes all sixteen, which is the argument for the board.
- **A shedding walk over `cold-seat.md`**, whose two candidates recorded finding row 195 names still stand. Outside this diff; to the existing engagement-size board item, per that row's own instruction. What this change did shed is its own material: the pointer rewrite, `:9`'s third sentence, `:13`'s paragraph, `:25`'s anecdote and `:32`'s enumeration.
- **The habits stated in both places.** Step 1 first copied habit 1's proposition into a second amendable site, which is the shape decision 4 performed a deletion to escape; it now points at the habit by content rather than by ordinal, an ordinal being another agreement kept in step by hand.

The Success line, the orphan check, and the doctrine's Release bullet not naming shipped depth files — all three to the board. And the eight steps and the ledger, which two cold seats have read and no consumer has used: a run that reaches them needs a pickup carrying two owner forks the material cannot narrow, and an owner to converse with.
