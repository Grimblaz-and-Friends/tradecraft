# D-430: The intake gains a bar and a shape — a filing about governing prose carries an incident or a run, and a review enters the board by cause

**Status:** Accepted 2026-09-06 (PR #430)

## Context

[#405](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/405), one of the two roots the owner affirmed on 2026-09-05 under the cluster about full ceilings and copied sentences — *"I think your 2 roots are good."* Its sibling is [#404](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/404), which is `sequenced-after` this one.

**What the tree held at the base.** At `bab184c`, three sentences put a finding onto the board. Two self-gate: friction met under merged material and an experience session's finding are use-originated by construction. The third is `skills/adversarial-review/references/arbitration.md` clause (b), which routed a sustained finding *"to the board where it would be worth someone's time as its own issue — never filed merely to be seen not dropping it."* That phrase was the whole test. The consumer-consequence bar opening the same clause is scoped away from the board route in its own words — *"that is the precedence wherever fix and record both fit"*. And `skills/filing/SKILL.md` declined the question outright in its description: *"not for deciding whether a finding is worth filing at all."* So the cell every filing loads sent the worth question elsewhere, and the elsewhere said only that it should be worth it.

Nothing anywhere told a review to ask *why* before it filed. `filing`'s batch outcome was the nearest thing, and it is optional, written for a session holding defects in hand rather than for a review's terminal stage, and where the symptoms already carry numbers it explicitly sends the cause to a number of its own with the symptoms tied beside it. There was no form in which a finding existed on the board without a number of its own.

**Two briefs, and which one governs.** A design sitting on 2026-09-05 produced the brief at [comment 5555947037](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/405#issuecomment-5555947037), affirmed by the owner there — *"affirm both, post them."* A second session the same night posted a competing brief at [5556069086](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/405#issuecomment-5556069086) and built [PR #426](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/pull/426) from it, whose test was *a named wrong action* and whose scope excluded everything the sitting's brief bought. Put to the owner on 2026-09-06 as three options with a recommendation, he ruled *"B - post the amendment"*: the sitting's brief stands, amended on one point — the gate sits in `filing`, on every route onto the board, rather than at the review's terminal stage alone. That amendment is [5556337096](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/405#issuecomment-5556337096) and it is the term this change was built to.

**The probe that settled the amended point**, run by the superseded session over the 105 issues open on 2026-09-05 — `gh issue list --repo Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft --state open --limit 1000 --json number,title,body` — found 26 stating a review ruling as their provenance, 72 stating no route in at all, and 84 touching governing prose. A bar sited at the terminal stage alone would govern a minority of the visible intake, and with nothing recording which door an issue came in by, its effect could never be checked afterward. That is the whole reason the gate moved.

## Decision

**1. The bar is *an incident from real work, or a run*, and not *a named wrong action*.** The owner agreed to a run in the sitting — *"Agreed"* to the same situation put to a session with and without the change — and the superseded session's own record states that the demonstrated form was live on that fork and not taken. Both forms are stricter than what stood; the difference is that a named action is a claim the filer writes, where a run is evidence a reader can re-execute. The run is spelled out in `filing` in the owner's own terms — the same situation put to a session under the text as it stands and under what would replace it — because *"a run"* unqualified is a word a session satisfies with anything it happened to execute.

**2. The bar sits in `filing`, on every route.** From the probe above. The terminal stage is one filer among others and gets no bar of its own, which is why `arbitration.md` says *meeting whatever bar a filing must meet* and names no cell: `skills/authoring/references/cell-structure.md` bars a shipped cell from naming a shipped sibling. **The premise that the cell fires at all was tested before the brief locked**: all 30 of the newest open issues on 2026-09-05 carried `filing`'s tie block as their first element, its *"Why it will get picked up"* section, and its *"Deliberately deferred"* section. Across all 105 the figures were 82, 84 and 91, the shortfall being issues that predate those rules.

**3. The bar reaches filings about governing prose and no others, and that was the session's call.** A defect in a script or a guard already carries its consequence in the failure it produces, and the rate the filing's evidence measures is specific to prose. The owner was offered the wider scope explicitly in the superseded round and did not take it. The scope sentence is the negative control the artifact's third criterion names: an implementation that gated every filing would satisfy the first two criteria and fail that one.

**4. The disposal obligation ships in `filing` rather than living only in this repository's `landing` cell.** This is the one place D-415's decision 6 was not followed, and deliberately. That entry accepted an asymmetry — an adopter receives the tie and its ranking consequence and no landing rule — because, in its own words, the only shipped cell near the job of a pull request body was `persist-changes`, and putting the walk there *"would have been a fifth cell and a wider change than was affirmed"*. Scope, not the kind of thing being placed; an earlier draft of this entry said *procedure* and the review corrected it. Here the thing being placed is not a procedure but the **promise that makes the new outcome lawful**: `filing`'s fifth outcome tells a session to put a finding on someone else's issue and take no number for it, and the only thing standing between that and the burial the extend rule refuses is that the cause's fix disposes of it. Ship the outcome without the promise and an adopter gets a licence to bury. So `filing` carries *a change fixing a cause disposes of every finding recorded under it, clearing it or filing it then*, and `docs/cells/landing/SKILL.md` carries this repository's application of it — where in the flow it runs, and the command that reads the comments. That is the split `docs/cells/siting/SKILL.md` states, and the two do not restate each other.

**5. A promoted survivor does not re-run the bar.** Its cause met it, and the cause's fix landing while the finding still stands is itself the observation that the finding needs a fix of its own. Re-running the bar at promotion would bin exactly the instances the affirmed brief says must be captured. **The carve-out was first left implicit** — `filing`'s *"clearing it, or filing it then"* read as the more specific instruction — after a cold seat observed that the exemption's site was unstated and that an adopter would meet the bar and the obligation with nothing between them. **The experience session refused that reading and bought the clause.** A consumer writing a cause's fix at landing met the bar as live on the two survivors it was told to file, found neither carried an incident of its own, and resolved it by carrying the cause's incident while reporting the conflict as the thing it was least certain about: *"a stricter read of the filing bar says those two should not be filed at all, but then the landing rule would strand them when #77 closes."* The exemption is now a clause in that sentence rather than a reading a session has to reach.

**5a. The bar applies at three moments and the text now says which, because the experience session found two of the three read the other way.** Filing the cause: the bar applies. Recording a finding under an open cause: it does not — the cause met it on behalf of all of them. Promoting a survivor when the cause's fix lands: it does not, per decision 5. The second of those was bought by the same run as decision 5's clause, at the other end of the mechanism: a consumer filing a cause with three findings under it reported that `filing`'s *"an extending comment … meets the same evidence standard"* — which this change extended to a recorded finding — *"can be read as re-imposing the incident bar on each comment."* That reading empties the mechanism: no instance carries its own incident, which is the whole reason they ride under one cause. It recorded them correctly and flagged the reading as live, so the exception is now written into that sentence.

**6. A cause the bar refuses does not licence filing its findings singly.** They take the `record` ruling `arbitration.md` already carries, and recurrence promotes them, which is the affirmed brief's *"recorded under their cause, not binned"* discharged by a route that exists rather than by a second one. The cause is named in the finding the entry records rather than keyed to a number — a refused cause has no number — and `skills/adversarial-review/references/the-record.md` requires that entry's `artifact` name the durable subject and never the change under review.

**7. The `filing` description moved, and whether that was lawful is the owner's.** The affirmed brief puts *"any change to the always-on documents"* out of scope, and a cell's description is inside the always-on surface `skills/authoring/references/routing.md` measures. The session read the exclusion as reaching the *documents* — `AGENTS.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, the charter, `docs/values.md` — on two grounds: the exclusion was written while the sitting's third live option was *a standing allocation under the values document's lane model*, so what it refuses is solving this by amending those; and the description as it stood would ship a sentence this change makes false, in the one surface that loads in every session. **A third ground stood here and is withdrawn.** It attributed to the owner's amendment a phrase that comment does not contain — the words are a session's note on the superseded pull request, posted a minute after it, and the comment the amendment actually points at does not mention descriptions at all. The review sustained that as its highest finding. The two grounds above stood without it and stand now. **The wider reading remains live and the wrong pick costs one reverting edit** — edits to the outcome, the bar, `arbitration.md` and the landing walk all stand without it — which is why this was decided and reported rather than put as a fork. `ci.yml` flags the pull request for the owner's specific read on exactly this ground.

## Meaning changes, named

`skills/authoring/references/revising.md` requires every one where amendments are recorded.

1. **`filing` now decides, for one class, whether a finding is worth filing at all**, having disclaimed that question for every class. Non-prose filings are unaffected.
2. **The *Carried at creation* list becomes conditional.** Every element before this one is owed by every filing; the incident or run is owed only where the subject is governing prose.
3. **`filing` uses the term *governing prose* for the first time and carries the sense in its own words** — *prose a later session is expected to act on*. `skills/adversarial-review/SKILL.md` carries the same sense verbatim in the shipped tree; the cell-structure rule bars pointing at it, so the sense is restated rather than cited. No sentence elsewhere turns on a term this change redefined.
4. **The extend rule stops governing every comment-on-an-open-issue case.** A finding recorded under its cause is a comment on an open issue that the extend rule would refuse, and the fifth outcome says in terms that the rule does not govern it, naming what stands in for it. Four outcomes became five.
5. **A breached rule stops being a sufficient evidence form on governing prose.** *"Prose is the fallback… and it is the right form for what has no failing run: a want, an idea, an incident that occurred, and a breached rule"* now adds *and, on governing prose, half of what that filing carries*. Left alone, that sentence would have blessed exactly the filing the new bar refuses, two paragraphs after it.
6. **`arbitration.md` clause (b)'s board route no longer means one issue per finding.** *"as its own issue"* is deleted from the worth clause; the clause itself — *"where it would be worth someone's time — never filed merely to be seen not dropping it"* — is kept, because nothing else bars a worthless non-prose finding from the board and this change offered no evidence for dropping it.
7. **`filing`'s purpose header moved**, its Purpose gaining *and keep off the board what nothing acts differently for* and its Success gaining the two new jobs. `revising.md` requires the recheck; the old success criteria covered neither the bar nor the disposal, and a stale one misaims every future review of the cell.
8. **`docs/cells/landing/SKILL.md` now has the cause-fix pass read the cause's own issue**, one sentence after telling it never to read ties there. Both are true and the paragraph says which is which: ties are read off every open body because a symptom filed later writes the tie on itself; recorded findings live on the cause and nowhere else.
9. **`skills/adversarial-review/SKILL.md`'s pointer précis for `arbitration.md`** names the board route, a description of a `references/` file's contents rather than a rule.

**Six more the review's own fix batch made, listed here because a batch rewrites governing prose exactly as the change did.**

10. **`arbitration.md` stops restating what a recorded finding carries and how a numbered one is tied.** The sentence that did is deleted; the stage reads `filing` for both, as the same paragraph already instructs it to. This removes a second copy rather than adding one, and it is what discharges the review's finding that the file re-imposed the bar on every recorded finding — by subtraction, since copying the exception across is what `skills/authoring/references/cell-structure.md` calls never lawful.
11. **`arbitration.md`'s no-cause route is now under the same bar**, and names the `record` ruling where the bar refuses. It read as unconditional, which put it in direct conflict with `filing`'s answer for the same case.
12. **The bar reaches neither an extending comment nor a finding recorded under a cause**: each inherits the one its host or its cause met. The review found the change had silently gated extending comments, the creation list having gained the bar; it is ruled this way rather than gating them, because an extending comment's defect is one a ruling closing the host disposes of, so gating it would bury the defect the extend rule exists to surface.
13. **`landing` no longer restates the shipped disposition pair**, and now carries the promoted-survivor exemption this artifact's own statement of what gets built had specified for it and the implementation had put in `filing` instead — where the session filing a survivor never reads it.
14. **`filing` names the `charter` cell for what an incident is not.** The bar borrows the charter's term and the charter's exclusion is what gives it teeth: a stage that files its own review finding as the incident defeats the mechanism. A pointer rather than a restated exclusion, the charter being the one cell any cell may name.
15. **The evidence-form qualifier reaches all four forms on governing prose**, not the breached rule alone. A want or an idea about governing prose was blessed by one sentence and refused by another two paragraphs earlier.

## What this change cost

**Derived at write time on a frozen surface is barred**, so the commands are the claim: `python tools/lint.py`, which prices every ceiling in force and prints each cell's body beside it, and `python tools/figures.py --base bab184c` for the prose delta and the always-on rows. **An earlier version of this sentence named `python tools/figures.py --cell skills/filing/SKILL.md` as a third**, and the review found it exits 1 — `--cell` requires `--cell-budget`, and `filing` has no budget for one to be, so there is no correct invocation and any figure it could be made to print would be measured against a ceiling no guard backs. The lint's own per-cell line is what derives that figure. The lint exits clean on this tree, so nothing is over a ceiling and `docs/admissions.jsonl` gains no row. `filing` and `landing` are cells the lint reports as carrying no body budget. **`arbitration.md` is not measured at all**: the lint prices a cell's `SKILL.md` body and not its `references/`, and `adversarial-review`'s own body — which the pointer précis edit touches — does carry a budget and stays inside it.

**Net growth is argued because `skills/authoring/SKILL.md` requires it, and the argument the review found here first was not one.** It said every addition is a rule the affirmed brief bought — which answers why the rules exist and not why the characters do — and it rested on a false inventory of what left, claiming three words where four went from `arbitration.md` and a twelve-word non-trigger clause went from the `filing` description.

**What actually leaves, in full:** *"as its own issue"* from `arbitration.md`'s worth clause, which the cause route made false; *"not for deciding whether a finding is worth filing at all, and"* from the `filing` description, which is the deletion decision 7 turns on; and, in the review's own fix batch, `arbitration.md`'s restatement of `filing`'s outcome, *"a second time"* from `filing`, and `landing`'s restatement of the shipped disposition pair. **The change is still a net addition to all three cells**, and `python tools/lint.py` prices each of them.

**What was surveyed and what was not.** The outflow's three moves were run against the always-on edit and refused, above. **No survey of deletion candidates inside `filing`'s body was run**, and the review sustained that as a finding. It is not run here either, deliberately: which content leaves `filing` is the design call this change hands to the owner as a decision rather than settling in a fix batch. The first candidates any such survey meets are the batch bullet and the exhibit paragraph that follows it — and `docs/recorded-findings.jsonl` row 49 already ruled that the exhibit does not show the half it is cited for.
