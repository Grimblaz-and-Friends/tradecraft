## Decision

**1. The bar is *an incident from real work, or a run*, and not *a named wrong action*.** The owner agreed to a run in the sitting — *"Agreed"* to the same situation put to a session with and without the change — and the superseded session's own record states that the demonstrated form was live on that fork and not taken. Both forms are stricter than what stood; the difference is that a named action is a claim the filer writes, where a run is evidence a reader can re-execute. The run is spelled out in `filing` in the owner's own terms — the same situation put to a session under the text as it stands and under what would replace it — because *"a run"* unqualified is a word a session satisfies with anything it happened to execute.

**2. The bar sits in `filing`, on every route.** From the probe above. The terminal stage is one filer among others and gets no bar of its own, which is why `arbitration.md` says *meeting whatever bar a filing must meet* and names no cell: `skills/authoring/references/cell-structure.md` bars a shipped cell from naming a shipped sibling. **The premise that the cell fires at all was tested before the brief locked**: all 30 of the newest open issues on 2026-09-05 carried `filing`'s tie block as their first element, its *"Why it will get picked up"* section, and its *"Deliberately deferred"* section. Across all 105 the figures were 82, 84 and 91, the shortfall being issues that predate those rules.

**3. The bar reaches filings about governing prose and no others, and that was the session's call.** A defect in a script or a guard already carries its consequence in the failure it produces, and the rate the filing's evidence measures is specific to prose. The owner was offered the wider scope explicitly in the superseded round and did not take it. The scope sentence is the negative control the artifact's third criterion names: an implementation that gated every filing would satisfy the first two criteria and fail that one.

**4. The disposal obligation ships in `filing` rather than living only in this repository's `landing` cell.** This is the one place D-415's decision 6 was not followed, and deliberately. That entry accepted an asymmetry — an adopter receives the tie and its ranking consequence and no landing rule — because the walk it was placing was a *procedure*, and no shipped cell held the job of a pull request body. Here the thing being placed is not a procedure but the **promise that makes the new outcome lawful**: `filing`'s fifth outcome tells a session to put a finding on someone else's issue and take no number for it, and the only thing standing between that and the burial the extend rule refuses is that the cause's fix disposes of it. Ship the outcome without the promise and an adopter gets a licence to bury. So `filing` carries *a change fixing a cause disposes of every finding recorded under it, clearing it or filing it then*, and `docs/cells/landing/SKILL.md` carries this repository's application of it — where in the flow it runs, and the command that reads the comments. That is the split `docs/cells/siting/SKILL.md` states, and the two do not restate each other.

**5. A promoted survivor does not re-run the bar.** Its cause met it, and the cause's fix landing while the finding still stands is itself the observation that the finding needs a fix of its own. Re-running the bar at promotion would bin exactly the instances the affirmed brief says must be captured. **The carve-out has no sentence of its own**: `filing`'s *"clearing it, or filing it then"* is the more specific instruction and governs. A cold seat observed that the exemption's site was unstated in the artifact and that an adopter would meet the bar and the obligation with nothing between them; this is the answer, recorded here rather than bought with another sentence on a shipped surface.

**6. A cause the bar refuses does not licence filing its findings singly.** They take the `record` ruling `arbitration.md` already carries, and recurrence promotes them, which is the affirmed brief's *"recorded under their cause, not binned"* discharged by a route that exists rather than by a second one. The cause is named in the finding the entry records rather than keyed to a number — a refused cause has no number — and `skills/adversarial-review/references/the-record.md` requires that entry's `artifact` name the durable subject and never the change under review.

**7. The `filing` description moved, and whether that was lawful is the owner's.** The affirmed brief puts *"any change to the always-on documents"* out of scope, and a cell's description is inside the always-on surface `skills/authoring/references/routing.md` measures. The session read the exclusion as reaching the *documents* — `AGENTS.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, the charter, `docs/values.md` — on three grounds: the exclusion was written while the sitting's third live option was *a standing allocation under the values document's lane model*, so what it refuses is solving this by amending those; the owner's amendment says in its own words that the superseded pull request's *"placement is the one the amendment adopts"*, and that placement was the description clause together with the body rule; and the description as it stood would ship a sentence this change makes false, in the one surface that loads in every session. **The wider reading remains live and the wrong pick costs one reverting edit** — edits to the outcome, the bar, `arbitration.md` and the landing walk all stand without it — which is why this was decided and reported rather than put as a fork. `ci.yml` flags the pull request for the owner's specific read on exactly this ground.

## Meaning changes, named

`skills/authoring/references/revising.md` requires every one where amendments are recorded.

1. **`filing` now decides, for one class, whether a finding is worth filing at all**, having disclaimed that question for every class. Non-prose filings are unaffected.
2. **The *Carried at creation* list becomes conditional.** Every element before this one is owed by every filing; the incident or run is owed only where the subject is governing prose.
3. **`filing` uses the term *governing prose* for the first time and carries the sense in its own words** — *prose a later session is expected to act on*. `skills/adversarial-review/SKILL.md` carries the same sense verbatim in the shipped tree; the cell-structure rule bars pointing at it, so the sense is restated rather than cited. No sentence elsewhere turns on a term this change redefined.
4. **The extend rule stops governing every comment-on-an-open-issue case.** A finding recorded under its cause is a comment on an open issue that the extend rule would refuse, and the fifth outcome says in terms that the rule does not govern it, naming what stands in for it. Four outcomes became five.
5. **A breached rule stops being a sufficient evidence form on governing prose.** *"Prose is the fallback… and it is the right form for what has no failing run: a want, an idea, an incident that occurred, and a breached rule"* now adds *and, on governing prose, half of what that filing carries*. Left alone, that sentence would have blessed exactly the filing the new bar refuses, two paragraphs after it.
6. **`arbitration.md` clause (b)'s board route no longer means one issue per finding.** *"as its own issue"* is deleted from the worth clause; the clause itself — *"where it would be worth someone's time — never filed merely to be seen not dropping it"* — is kept, because nothing else bars a worthless non-prose finding from the board and this change offered no evidence for dropping it.
7. **`filing`'s purpose header moved**, its Purpose gaining *and keep off the board what nothing acts differently for* and its Success gaining the two new jobs. `revising.md` requires the recheck; the old success criteria covered neither the bar nor the disposal, and a stale one misaims every future review of the cell.
8. **`docs/cells/landing/SKILL.md` now has the cause-fix pass read the cause's own issue**, one sentence after telling it never to read ties there. Both are true and the paragraph says which is which: ties are read off every open body because a symptom filed later writes the tie on itself; recorded findings live on the cause and nowhere else.
9. **`skills/adversarial-review/SKILL.md`'s pointer précis for `arbitration.md`** names the board route, a description of a `references/` file's contents rather than a rule.

## The outflow, owed because an always-on surface was edited

`skills/authoring/references/routing.md` requires the order be run whenever an always-on surface is edited, not only at a ceiling, and a refusal be written where amendments are recorded. Run, and **discharged by a refusal**.

1. **A rule a guard now holds** — none does. `tools/board.py` fetches `number,id` and `number,title`, and nothing in this tree reads an issue body or an issue comment, so no guard can see either the incident-or-run element or a finding recorded under a cause. Building one is a separate change, and the pre-implementation artifact's boundary says so.
2. **A rule that binds only inside one activity moves to that activity's cell** — already satisfied twice over: the bar and the outcome are both in `filing`, the activity's own cell, and the walk is in `landing`. What is on the always-on surface is the **description**, which is the triggering surface and the one thing that cannot move: a description that does not name the job does not route to it, and the sentence being removed is one that routed the affected session *away*.
3. **A reason a decision entry already carries compresses to its citation** — this entry lands in the same pull request, and the description carries no reason to compress.

Nothing has a cheaper home. **The fourth answer was not reached**, no evidence being in hand that any existing description clause fails to bind. The summary-half clause *"and the line between the evidence a filing carries and the design it leaves for whoever picks it up"* was considered for deletion to pay for the addition and kept: it is a real signal about existing content, its removal has nothing to do with this change, and buying characters by dropping unrelated routing is the bad move `routing.md` names as what a matched-cost reading produces.
