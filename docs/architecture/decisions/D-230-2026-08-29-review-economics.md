# D-230: The fix bar is a decision test, a finding can be recorded, and a lens can retire

**Status:** Accepted 2026-08-29 (PR #230)

## Context

The owner reported on 2026-08-26 that the review skills "end up finding tons of junk that ends up with high cost to find, judge, implement, and then even fills up the application with things that aren't truly needed." [#216](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/216) was filed for three changes drawn from the north star's Judge stage: an admission bar on the finder, boundary triage against the affirmed criteria, and a rule that no finding vanishes by the builder's hand.

**Two of the three were dropped at convergence, on measurement.** A classification pass over the final reports of PRs #210, #193 and #194 against each change's own affirmed acceptance criteria put ~7% of 163 sustained findings in the cell an undemonstrated-and-untied bar would reject, and every one was already low, latent, recorded or handed on rather than fixed. It put ~60% in demonstrated-but-untied — including #193's `O14`, where `main` had taken the version the branch claimed and the merge-base-only gate stayed green, so two shipped trees would have carried one version number. **A bar keyed to the affirmed criteria would have routed that away.** The pass also found the tie rate is mostly a function of how broadly a change's criteria happened to be worded: #191's were broad and its review came out 28 of 42 tied, where #177's and #199's were mechanism-specific. That is one seat's classification, unreadable on 9 of 163 and reconstructing #194's total at ±3, and it is recorded as the reason a filed want was dropped rather than as a measurement anything rests on.

The owner then reframed the question twice, and the reframing is what this entry exists to record. **First:** the cost is paid the moment a finding exists, so if a class is not worth fixing the lever is what the seats are pointed at, not what they may report. **Second:** the venue question is not scope but what acting costs in *judgment* — "the bar to fix things now should be a lot lower as long as it doesn't need my attention or design work" — and filing is its own value test, because "filing a bunch of new issues doesn't fix anything, it just makes a mess." The board stood at 52 open on 2026-08-29, fed by 78 routed findings across 19 panel reviews.

## Decision

**A lens retires, on either of two grounds.** The class is repeatedly not worth acting on, or **a mechanism now catches it** — the practice's admission order read at the review rather than at authoring time. The second ground is deliberately written before any mechanism exists to use it, so [#217](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/217)'s suites retire the transmission class of prose findings by collecting on this rule rather than amending it. **What retires is any standing instruction to look** — a seat's brief, a lens in the evidence standards, a scope clause naming a target — not only a lens promoted under the paragraph above it; a cold probe found the narrower wording did not reach this change's own exemplar.

**The exemplar retirement is performed here, not merely made available.** `after-the-fix.md` no longer sends the post-fix look at the merged finding list, the report, the commit message and the PR body. One rule manufactured that class while a second, the relevance terminus, existed to notice the first one working. Of the eight rows that ever carried the artifact-versus-apparatus split, five returned 2–11% apparatus-facing and two of the last three returned 52% and 41%; the queryable field stopped being recorded when [D-194] removed the row's arithmetic, though the report still carries the split. **Read as input is not read as subject** — the merged list still locates each sustained high's territory.

**The criteria stop being the admission test.** `SKILL.md` shed *"A finding names the success criterion it impairs, for the stated audience"* and *"A true observation that impairs no criterion is dismissed at the terminal stage without ceremony"*, for *"Impairing a stated criterion is not the test of a finding: criteria say what a change is for, not everything that must be true of the tree it produces."* This is the change's largest meaning change and the measurement above is its whole warrant — 60% of what these reviews sustained impairs no affirmed criterion, and the load-bearing findings are in that 60%. It is named here because a review found it named nowhere, which is what `revising.md` requires of an amendment.

**The terminal stage's second clause becomes what acting costs in judgment.** Needing neither the owner's attention, a design call, nor a change to what this change is → **fix**, whatever the finding's scope. Any of the three → a **decision, not a fix**: to the owner argued, or to the board only where it would be worth someone's time as its own issue. **Scope and the affirmed boundary are different things** — what a change merely happened not to touch is open; what its artifact named as out is the owner's, by construction, because affirming the artifact is what made the boundary theirs. Deliberately not a size threshold. **A finding whose sites fall on both sides takes one ruling in two tranches**; both that and the scope-versus-boundary distinction were written because a cold terminal stage hit each of them unprompted and said so.

**`record` is a disposition** — real and demonstrated, evidence to justify acting not yet there. It appends to a standing file the repository keeps, and **the next review's assignment fetches it**, which is the half a probe showed was failing: the obligation first sat in the closing-the-review file, whose load condition reads *"Nothing before the terminal ruling needs it,"* so a dispatching session obeying the pointers would never learn the record exists. Appending belongs to the closing stage; reading belongs to the dispatch contract.

**`routed` and `priced_out` are retired**, absorbed by fix / record / file, which dissolves [#171](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/171)'s vocabulary collision rather than patching it. The index needed no change: [D-194]'s cutover already forbids `dispositions` on new rows.

**A remedy that adds weight argues that weight**, and where the only available remedy is more prose on a governing surface, `record` beats `fix`. This is the half the owner's own instruction does not cover: "lower the bar as long as it needs no design" is a cheapness test, and the cheapest remedy for a prose finding is one more sentence. The justification half needs no prose here — [D-225]'s doctrine line already sends a worth-it judgment whose evidence has run out to `docs/values.md` by number, and the zone wall forbids a cell naming that path.

**Fixing needs no permission; declining to fix does.** A drop, a record and a lens retirement are ruled by a stage that is not the session that built the change. This is smaller than #216's filed version because a low fix bar makes fixing the builder's normal move, and it is what keeps the low bar from becoming the builder deciding what is obvious.

**`external` narrows back to what configured automation posts on a pull request.** [D-222] had widened it to cover a `codex review` the session invokes itself, on the sound ground that neither receives the panel dispatch or seat contract. **That ruling is kept and its bucket is not**: publishing-before-the-panel concurrency, push-triggered re-review, and stubbing/rate-limiting/skipping are all false of a run the session asks for, and `pr-222`'s row already carries both provenances in one frozen string. A self-invoked run is a **commissioned pass**, dispositioned on the terminal stage's docket and reported in prose, never under `seats` or `external`.

## Rejected

- **The admission bar on the finder**, as #216 was filed. Measured at ~7% of sustained findings, none of them costly. Coverage-first stays: a cold seat's unprompted yield is where the unpredicted defects come from, and the place to stop a worthless class is the brief.
- **Boundary triage against the affirmed criteria**, as the north star words it. It would have routed away the version collision and #210's falsified `experience-session` prescription, and it keys on how broadly a change's criteria happened to be worded.
- **Introduced-by-this-change versus pre-existing** as the venue axis. The implementing session proposed it and the owner rejected it: it still sorts by provenance rather than by what acting costs, and it manufactures board mess for pre-existing defects with determined one-line remedies.
- **A no-growth criterion on the always-on surface.** `authoring`'s own `routing.md` holds that reading outflow as a matched cost "either refuses a lawful addition or forces a bad move to pay for one," so the criterion is argued growth. The change grew an adopter's surface, derived by `python tools/figures.py` on the tree this PR produces against merge base `8b080c8`, and paid for it with a move: a third copy of the acceptance-criteria rule left the charter, already owned by `adversarial-review` and `engagement`. **Two further rule-bearing clauses left the charter in the same hunk and are named here rather than dropped in silence**, which `routing.md` permits and silence does not. *Outside a review, the drop is recorded on the work itself* — restored in the fix batch, because the new asymmetry routes every decline to a stage that does not exist outside a review. *Filing requires the remedy belong neither in this change nor in a guard* — **not** restored: a determined guard-shaped remedy is now a `fix` under clause (b), which is stricter than a bar on filing it, and what is genuinely lost is only the bar on filing a guard-shaped remedy that needs a design call.
- **A lint guard for the record file.** Deferred rather than refused: the file starts empty and its shape firms up in use.
- **Absorbing [#223](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/223) and [#203](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/203)**, both of whose sentences this change edits. #223's remedy needs a design call, which this change's own new test makes a decision rather than a fix — the first live application of the rule, and it applied against the implementing session's convenience. #203 was declined on a different ground and the distinction matters to anyone reading this as precedent: its remedy is an edit to the cell's `description`, deferred to #217's routing evals so that a description change is tested rather than argued.
