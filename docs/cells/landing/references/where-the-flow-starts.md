# Where the flow starts

**Loaded when** your change may be mechanical work, or may deliver the truth of some text, and you want to know what that changes about where this cell's flow starts. Which work is mechanical is not decided here; this says which step such work skips and which it never skips, and names the surface that decides the class.

**Where the flow starts depends on the change.** Mechanical work has no brief and no artifact to settle, so it branches and then goes straight to *build*; the branch is never skipped, `main` refusing direct pushes; which work is mechanical *for the brief and the artifact* is the doctrine's Convergence bullet, which is loaded in every session already. Stated as a pointer rather than a copy because that exemption has one owner — and scoped, because it is not the only such list: what a change owes in sessions and declining lines is the `experience-session` cell's, whose list is narrower — a bump exempts only where it preserves behaviour — while the outflow's is the same work as this one and exempts only from the outflow an always-on edit owes. Stated at all because the flow chain in this cell's body reaches the brief at its second step, so a session doing a typo reaches this cell and meets a step that does not apply to it. **And where what the change delivers is the truth of some text, a slice of build precedes the artifact**, as that same chain marks and the `engagement` cell states; the artifact stays where it was, settled before the first commit.
