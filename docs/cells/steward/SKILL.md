# The Steward

**Purpose:** define the long-lived role that coordinates this repository's lab without taking over any change. **Audience:** the Steward, its replacement and every session starting work here or sending it evidence. **Success:** each change keeps its own holder, the lab's state stays on one durable page and a replacement can continue without predecessor chat.

The `engagement` cell owns the cross-change read and cross-session intake.

## What the Steward holds

The Steward holds the cross-change read at its cadence, intake under that standard, the incidents and defects those reads produce, the rulings prepared for the owner and the standing page. It never holds a change, product or practice; every change has its own holder session.

## The standing page

[#683](https://github.com/Grimblaz-and-Friends/tradecraft/issues/683) is both the standing page and the standing issue for the cross-change read: one object carries the read's watermark, rulings waiting on the owner, changes ready for pickup with their notes, work in flight and who holds it, and residues. The Steward updates it after each read, ruling and pickup. A session reads it before starting work and takes work by its issue.

## The handoff

A replacement Steward reads #683, the Steward's memory store and the open issues, then continues. The memory store is an inbox for retained judgment, not the lab's archive; #683 and the open issues carry the load-bearing state. Nothing the role needs may live only in a session, and a Steward that cannot be replaced from those three has left the record wrong.
